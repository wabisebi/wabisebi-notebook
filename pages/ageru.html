N5 Grammar

あげる

Polite

Kekkon Dekinai Otoko · Episode 1

今いま ごはん あげますからね。

"I'll give you your dinner now, okay?"

あげる means to give: something goes away from you (or the person the story is about) to someone else. The thing gets を, the person who gets it gets に: 友達に花をあげる.

It never goes towards you: when someone gives something to you, that's くれる.

On this card, she's just come home and is about to feed her dog. あげます is the polite form.

Casual

Teasing Master Takagi-san · Season 2, Episode 5

これ あげる。

"Here, this is for you."

The casual form, plain あげる. On this card, she holds something out to him: これ (this) あげる (I give).

Quick Examples

A coworker asks what you did with your old bike.

弟おとうとにあげました。

"I gave it to my little brother."

The receiver gets に: 弟に. Who gave? You, so no need to say it.

Polite

Your friend admires your scarf. You don't wear it anymore.

それ、あげるよ。

"You can have it."

Literally "I'll give it (to you)". From you, to them: あげる.

Casual

It's Valentine's Day. Your friend is holding a box of chocolates.

誰だれにあげるの？

"Who are you giving them to?"

It also works for other people giving: here your friend gives, someone else gets.

Casual

At the park, a child asks if they can feed your dog.

犬いぬにパンをあげないでください。

"Please don't give the dog bread."

Feeding an animal is also あげる: 犬に + パンを.

Polite

With and without

Without

友達ともだちが本ほんをくれました。

"My friend gave me a book." The book comes to you.

With あげる

友達ともだちに本ほんをあげました。

"I gave my friend a book." The book goes away from you, to your friend.

Examples from Media

Polite

Good Morning Call · Episode 13

こいつにあげるとかは ありえないです。

"Giving it to this guy is out of the question."

こいつに: the person who would get it takes に.

Polite

Kekkon Dekinai Otoko · Episode 8

はい 今いまごはん あげますからね。

"Okay, I'm giving you your food now."

Talking to the dog. ごはんをあげる: give food, feed.

Casual

Only Yesterday

タエ子しにあげる 私わたしも。

"I'll give mine to Taeko too."

Her sisters don't like the fruit, so they hand it to タエ子. The person who gets it takes に.

Casual

Teasing Master Takagi-san · Season 2, Episode 2

じゃ チョコあげるね。

"Then I'll give you some chocolate."

Valentine's Day. チョコ(を)あげる: the を is dropped, as often in speech.

Casual

Aggretsuko · Season 2, Episode 5

ほら かき揚あげ あげる。

"Here, have some kakiage."

かき揚げ is a kind of tempura. ほら: "here", while holding it out.

Casual

Midnight Diner: Tokyo Stories · Season 2, Episode 4

人ひとにあげるものじゃないでしょ？

"That's not something you give to people, is it?"

人にあげるもの: "a thing you give to people". The verb describes もの.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 38

これあげる。

"Here, you can have this."

Handing something over.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 45

少すこしあげようか。

"Shall I give you a little?"

あげよう + か makes it an offer.
