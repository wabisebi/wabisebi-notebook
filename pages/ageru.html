N5 Grammar

あげる

あげる means to give: something goes away from you (or the person the story is about) to someone else.

The thing gets を, the person who receives gets に: 友達に花をあげる, "give flowers to a friend". It never goes towards you: when someone gives something to you, that's くれる.

Polite: あげます, あげました. Casual: あげる, あげた.

Examples

A coworker asks what you did with your old bike.

弟にあげました。

"I gave it to my little brother."

The receiver gets に: 弟に. Who gave? You, so no need to say it.

Polite

Your friend admires your scarf. You don't wear it anymore.

それ、あげるよ。

"You can have it."

Literally "I'll give it (to you)". From you, to them: あげる.

Casual

It's Valentine's Day. Your friend is holding a box of chocolates.

誰にあげるの？

"Who are you giving them to?"

It also works for other people giving: here your friend gives, someone else gets.

Casual

At the park, a child asks if they can feed your dog.

犬にパンをあげないでください。

"Please don't give the dog bread."

Feeding an animal is also あげる: 犬に + パンを.

Polite

With and without

Without

友達が本をくれました。

"My friend gave me a book." The book comes to you.

With あげる

友達に本をあげました。

"I gave my friend a book." The book goes away from you, to your friend.

Sentence mining

Casual

Weakest Beast · Episode 5

ここは 朱里にあげる。

"I'll give this place to you, Shuri."

朱里 + に ("to") + あげる ("give") = "I'll give it to Shuri". ここ: "this place".

Polite

Weakest Beast · Episode 10

マンションをあげる癖があります。

"I have a habit of giving away condos."

マンション ("condo") + を + あげる ("give") + 癖 ("habit") = "a habit of giving away condos".

Casual

I am Mita, Your Housekeeper · Episode 4

お母さんにあげた石が１つない！

"One of the stones I gave Mom is missing!"

お母さんに ("to Mom") + あげた ("gave") + 石 ("stones") = "the stones I gave Mom".

Polite

Kino's Journey · Episode 11

あなたに花をあげましょう。

"I'll give you some flowers."

あなたに ("to you") + 花を ("flowers") + あげましょう = "I'll give you flowers", a polite offer.

Casual

Border · Episode 9

それをあげようか？

"Want me to give you that?"

それを ("that") + あげよう ("let me give") + か = "shall I give you that?".

Polite

Hyouka · Episode 12

あげますよ。

"You can have it."

あげます ("I'll give") + よ = "you can have it". Who gets what is clear from the scene.

Polite

Assassination Classroom · Episode 2

先生から 一つアドバイスをあげましょう。

"Let me give you a piece of advice."

Advice can be "given" with あげる too.

Casual

Good Morning Call · Episode 1

団子あげるよ

"I'll give you a dango."

団子 + あげる: the dumpling goes from the speaker to the other person.
