What is katakana?

Katakana is the second Japanese alphabet. It has the same sounds as hiragana, just other shapes: か and カ are both ka. The shapes are sharp and straight, made from small pieces of kanji.

Loanwords
Words taken from other languages, mostly English.コーヒー · coffee
Names
Foreign names, countries and cities.アメリカ · America
Emphasis
Sound words and words that should stand out, a bit like italics.ドキドキ · heart pounding

ー makes the sound before it longer: コーヒー is koohii.
