N5 Grammar

どちら・どっち

Politeどちら
Casualどっち

Same meaning: "which one (of two)". Only the politeness changes.

Polite

Brush Up Life · Episode 10

どちらが宇野うのさんですか？

"Which one of you is Uno-san?"

どちら means "which one", picking between two (or a few). On this card, two people are standing there and she asks which of them is Uno.

どちら is the polite version, どっち the casual one: どっちが宇野さん？

どちら is also the polite way to ask "where" or "which way": 駅はどちらですか？

Casual

Weakest Beast · Episode 5

どっちがいい？

"Which one do you want?"

どっち is the casual どちら: "which one" when there are two choices. On this card, どっち + がいい asks which of the two the other person wants.

Quick Examples

Holding up two shirts for a friend.

どっちがいい？

"Which one is better?"

Casual

A waiter at a café.

コーヒーと紅茶こうちゃ、どちらがいいですか？

"Coffee or tea, which would you like?"

Polite

Lost, asking a stranger.

駅えきはどちらですか？

"Which way is the station?"

A politer どこ.

Polite

Small talk with a friend.

犬いぬと猫ねこ、どっちが好すき？

"Dogs or cats, which do you like?"

Casual

With and without

With どれ (3 or more)

どれがいい？

"Which one do you want?" From a whole row of things.

With どっち (two)

赤あかと青あお、どっちがいい？

"Red or blue, which do you want?" Only two to choose from.

Examples from Media

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 9

で　お届とどけはどちらまで

"And where shall we deliver it to?"

どちら as a polite どこ, the way shop staff talk. まで = "to, as far as".

Polite

Kekkon Dekinai Otoko · Episode 4

ご主人しゅじん どちらから？

"Where is your husband from?"

どちら as a polite "where".

Polite

Good Morning Call · Episode 13

これから どちらへ。

"Where are you off to now?"

どちらへ: a polite "where to?"

Polite

1 Litre of Tears · Episode 1

ワンちゃんはどっちですか？

"Which one is the doggie?"

Casual どっち, polite です: people mix them.

Polite

Death Note · Episode 10

ではどちらにするか決きめてください。

"Then please decide which it will be."

どちら + に + する ("decide on") = "decide which one". か makes it "whether this or that".

Casual

Kotaro Lives Alone · Episode 4

コタローはどっちの味方みかただ？

"Kotaro, whose side are you on?"

どっちの + noun: "which one's".

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 27

マサキはどっちが好すき

"Masaki, which one do you like?"

Two coffees to compare, so どっち. A question without か: the rising voice does the job.

Casual

Midnight Diner: Tokyo Stories · Season 2, Episode 10

どちらもいらない。

"I don't need either."

どちらも + negative: neither of the two.
