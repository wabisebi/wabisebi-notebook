N5 Grammar

ほしい (欲しい)

Polite欲しいです
Casual欲しい

Same meaning: "want". Only the politeness changes.

Polite

Aggretsuko · Season 1, Episode 8

でも 彼氏かれし欲ほしいですよね？

"But you want a boyfriend, right?"

欲しい means want, for a thing: a toolbox, a coffee, new shoes. The thing you want takes が: 彼氏が欲しい.

On this card, she asks a coworker: 彼氏 (が) 欲しいですよね？ The が is dropped, as often in speech, and です makes it polite.

For an action, use たい instead: 買いたい (want to buy).

Casual

YouTube · video

普通ふつうの工具こうぐ箱ばこが、欲ほしいんだよな。

"I just want a normal toolbox."

On this card, it answers "What do you want?": a normal toolbox, 工具箱が欲しい. んだよな adds "that's the thing, you know".

Quick Examples

Your birthday is coming up. A friend asks.

新あたらしいスマホが欲ほしいな。

"I want a new phone."

な = thinking out loud.

Casual

At a café, to the staff.

お水みずが欲ほしいんですが。

"Could I get some water?"

んですが turns it into a soft request.

Polite

Shopping with a friend.

何なにが欲ほしい？

"What do you want?"

Casual

A busy week. A coworker asks how you are.

ちょっと休やすみが欲ほしいです。

"I could use a little break."

Polite

Someone offers you their old stuff.

そんなもの、欲ほしくないよ。

"I don't want that kind of thing."

It works like an い-adjective: 欲しい → 欲しくない.

Casual

With and without

Without

コーヒーを飲のむ。

"I'm having a coffee." Just what I do.

With 欲しい

コーヒーが欲ほしい。

"I want a coffee." What I wish I had.

Examples from Media

Polite

I am Mita, Your Housekeeper · Episode 11

欲ほしいもの ないですか？

"Is there anything you want?"

欲しい ("want") + もの ("thing") = "a thing you want". ないですか: "isn't there?".
