N5 Grammar

もう

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 3

ユーカリ？　かわいく食たべてますけど、もう、あれ限界げんかいです。

"Eucalyptus? He looks cute eating it, but he's [already] at his limit."

もう means already: the change has happened, sooner than you'd think. It goes before the part it's about: もう + 限界 = "already at the limit".

On this card, it answers "has it happened yet?" with "yes, already". He looks fine, but he's done.

もう itself never changes. Polite or casual only shows at the end: もう限界です / もう限界だ.

Casual

YouTube · video

もう着火ちゃっか剤ざいない。着火ちゃっか剤ざいはない。

"We're out of fire starters. There are no fire starters."

With a negative at the end, もう becomes not anymore: there were some before, now they're gone.

Quick Examples

Your friend offers to cook you dinner.

もう食たべた。

"I already ate."

Casual

You look at the clock at a party.

あ、もう11時じですね。

"Oh, it's already 11."

Polite

Someone asks if you still live in Osaka.

もう住すんでいません。

"I don't live there anymore."

Polite

After the third slice of cake.

もう食たべられない。

"I can't eat any more."

Casual

With and without

Without

宿題しゅくだいをした。

"I did my homework." Just a fact.

With もう

もう宿題しゅくだいをした。

"I already did my homework." It's done, no need to ask.

Without

お金かねがない。

"I have no money." Maybe never had any.

With もう

もうお金かねがない。

"I have no money left." There was some, now it's gone.
