N5 Grammar

もう

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 3

ユーカリ？　かわいく食たべてますけど、もう、あれ限界げんかいです。

"Eucalyptus? He looks cute eating it, but he's [already] at his limit."

もう means already: the change has happened, sooner than you'd think. It goes before the part it's about: もう + 限界 = "already at the limit".

On this card, it answers "has it happened yet?" with "yes, already". He looks fine, but he's done.

もう itself never changes. Polite or casual only shows at the end: もう限界です / もう限界だ.

Casual

Midnight Diner: Tokyo Stories · Season 2, Episode 8

もう俺おれに憧あこがれないでくれ。

"Don't look up to me anymore."

With a negative, もう becomes not anymore. Here it's a negative request: 憧れないでくれ, "don't admire me", and もう makes it "no more".

Quick Examples

Your friend offers to cook you dinner.

もう食たべた。

"I already ate."

Casual

You look at the clock at a party.

あ、もう11時じですね。

"Oh, it's already 11."

Polite

Someone asks if you still live in Osaka.

もう住すんでいません。

"I don't live there anymore."

Polite

After the third slice of cake.

もう食たべられない。

"I can't eat any more."

Casual

With and without

Without

宿題しゅくだいをした。

"I did my homework." Just a fact.

With もう

もう宿題しゅくだいをした。

"I already did my homework." It's done, no need to ask.

Without

お金かねがない。

"I have no money." Maybe never had any.

With もう

もうお金かねがない。

"I have no money left." There was some, now it's gone.

Examples from Media

Polite

Tekkonkinkreet

なんだかもう冬ふゆですね。

"Somehow it's already winter."

もう + something that's now true = "already". なんだか = somehow.

Casual

Dungeon Meshi · Episode 11

もう来きた?

"They're here already?"

もう: already.

Casual

Good Morning Call · Episode 5

もう寝ねるぞ。

"I'm going to bed now."

Casual

Quartet · Episode 9

もう 十分じゅうぶん。

"That's enough."

もう + 十分 ("enough") = "that's already enough".

Casual

Vinland Saga · Episode 18

もういい。

"That's enough."

もういい: "enough already".

Casual

From Up on Poppy Hill

もう そんな時間じかん！

"Is it that late already?"

もう + そんな時間 ("that time") = "is it that late already?". Sooner than expected.

Casual

Good Morning Call · Episode 16

もう遅おそいし。

"It's already late."

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 15

もう夏なつだね。

"It's already summer."

もう = already.
