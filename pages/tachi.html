N5 Grammar

～たち

Polite

YouTube · video

子こどもたちも農のう作業さぎょうを楽たのしんでいます。

"The children are also enjoying the farm work."

たち turns one person into a group of people: 子ども (a child) becomes 子どもたち (children). It goes right after the word.

On this card, it answers "just one, or several?": here it's several kids. Japanese doesn't mark plurals otherwise, so たち is how you say "we", "they", "the kids".

It's used for people (and sometimes animals), not for things. Same in polite and casual speech.

Casual

Steins Gate · Episode 13

あの人ひとたちは？

"Who are those people?"

たち makes a group out of a person word. On this card, あの人 ("that person") + たち = "those people".

Quick Examples

You and your friend are talking about the weekend.

私わたしたちは土曜どよう日ひに会あいます。

"We're meeting on Saturday."

私 (I) + たち = 私たち (we). The same works for 僕たち and 俺たち.

Polite

A student asks where the teachers are.

先生せんせいたちは会議かいぎ室しつにいます。

"The teachers are in the meeting room."

Polite

Talking about a group of girls over there.

あの子こたちは双子ふたごです。

"Those kids are twins."

The group word (あの子) can be a person, and たち makes it "those kids".

Polite

With and without

Without

私わたしは公園こうえんに行いきます。

I'm going to the park. Just me.

With たち

私わたしたちは公園こうえんに行いきます。

We're going to the park. Me and others.

Examples from Media

Polite

YouTube · video

私わたしたちは、時間じかんがあるので、近ちかくの道みちの駅えきに買かい物ものに行いきます

"Since we have some time, we're going shopping at a nearby roadside station."

私たち is the plain, polite "we". It works in any situation.

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 37

僕ぼくたちにもください。

"Give some to us too, please."

僕 + たち = we.

Casual

Pokémon The Series: Indigo League, Episode 3

やったよ、俺おれたちは実力じつりょくでポケモン勝負しょうぶに勝かったんだ！

"We did it! We won the Pokémon battle with our skills."

俺たち: "we", said by a guy. 俺 is the rough "I", so 俺たち is the rough "we".

Casual

YouTube · video

すごい狭せまい席せきに案内あんないされたけど僕ぼくたちは全然ぜんぜんオッケー。

"We were shown to a really narrow seat, but we're totally fine with it."

僕たちは: "as for us". 僕 is the soft, everyday "I" for guys.

Casual

I'm Taking the Day Off · Episode 6

ねえ、君きみたち。

"Hey, you guys."

君 ("you") + たち = "you guys": talking to more than one person.

Casual

Weakest Beast · Episode 4

私わたしたちって 今いま…

"Right now, we..."

私 ("I") + たち = "we". って: a casual は. The sentence trails off.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 43

"We're best friends, after all."

私 + たち = we.
