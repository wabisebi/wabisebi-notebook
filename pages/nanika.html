N5 Grammar

何か・誰か・どこか

Polite

Legal High · Episode 2

何なにかありましたか？

"Did something happen?"

Add か to a question word and it stops being a question: 何 "what" → 何か "something".

Same trick for the others: 誰か "someone", どこか "somewhere". On this card, 何か + ありました ("happened") = "did something happen?".

が and を often drop after them: 何かありましたか, not 何かがありましたか.

Casual

Teasing Master Takagi-san · Season 3, Episode 3

なんだこれ何なにかの罠わなか？

"What is this? Some kind of trap?"

On this card, 何かの罠 = "some kind of trap".

Quick Examples

Your friend looks hungry.

何なにか食たべる？

"Want to eat something?"

Answer is just yes or no: うん、食べる.

Casual

A shop assistant, after explaining something.

何なにか質問しつもんはありますか？

"Do you have any questions?"

Polite

You're stuck under a heavy box.

誰だれか手伝てつだって！

"Someone help!"

Casual

A coworker on Friday.

週末しゅうまつ、どこか行いきますか？

"Are you going anywhere this weekend?"

Polite

With and without

With 何

何なにを食たべる？

"What will you eat?" A real question, wants a name.

With 何か

何なにか食たべる？

"Want to eat something?" Yes or no is enough.

Examples from Media

Polite

Brush Up Life · Episode 6

何なにかありました？

"Did something happen?"

何か = something. 何かありました？ is the go-to "is something wrong?"

Polite

The Wind Rises

あと 何なんかいりますか？

"Do you need anything else?"

あと ("else, more") + 何か + いります ("need") = "do you need anything else?".

Casual

YouTube · 1 Hour Easy Japanese Immersion (BBQ)

でどこかに乗のっける?

"So, should we put it on top somewhere?"

どこかに, not どこに: "somewhere", not "where".

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 2

何なにか乾かわき物もの買かってくる

"I'll go buy some dry snacks or something."

何か + a noun = "some ___". No を needed after it.

Casual

Last Samurai Standing · Episode 3

何なにかいる。

"Something's there."

Casual

The Journalist · Episode 6

何なにか言いってよ。

"Say something."

何か ("something") + 言って ("say") = "say something!". よ makes it pleading.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 28

何なにかお勧すすめある？

"Do you recommend anything?"

何か = something, anything.

Casual

Goblin Slayer · Episode 6

何なにか来くるわ。

"Something's coming."
