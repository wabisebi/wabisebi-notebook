N4 Grammar

のに

Noun or な-adjective before it? Add な first.

WordAddEnding
Noun学生がくせいなのにEven though I'm a student
な-adjective静しずかなのにEven though it's quiet
Verb行いく–のにEven though I'm going
い-adjective高たかい–のにEven though it's expensive
Polite

Kekkon Dekinai Otoko · Episode 6

断ことわってるのに しつこく メールしてくるんですよ

"I keep turning them down, and yet they keep emailing me."

のに means "even though": A, and yet B. B isn't what you'd expect after A, and usually you're annoyed, disappointed or surprised about it.

It goes after a verb or い-adjective as is (断ってる + のに), after a noun or な-adjective with な (雨なのに). On this card, saying no should stop the emails, and yet they keep coming. The speaker is fed up.

The politeness comes from the end of the sentence, のに itself never changes.

Casual

1 Litre of Tears · Episode 7

寒さむいのに 待またせちゃってごめんね。

"Sorry for making you wait when it's so cold."

On this card, it's cold, and yet he had to wait outside. She feels bad about it.

Examples

You studied all week, and still failed.

勉強べんきょうしたのに、落おちた。

"I studied, and I still failed."

You expected to pass. The のに shows the frustration.

Casual

It's Sunday, but your boss calls.

日曜日にちようびなのに、仕事しごとの電話でんわが来きました。

"It's Sunday, and yet I got a work call."

After a noun, add な.

Polite

Your friend didn't tell you about the party.

言いってくれれば行いったのに。

"If you'd told me, I would have gone."

At the end of a sentence, のに is pure regret: "and now it's too late".

Casual

With and without

With けど

勉強べんきょうしたけど、落おちた。

I studied, but I failed. Just a plain fact.

With のに

勉強べんきょうしたのに、落おちた。

I studied, and still failed! It feels unfair.

Sentence mining

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 5

パフェなのに　とってもヘルシーなんです

"It's a parfait, and yet it's really healthy."

Here the surprise is a happy one. Noun + な + のに.

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 45

冬眠とうみん中ちゅうなのに車くるま出だしてくださって ありがとうございます

"Thank you for driving us even though you're hibernating."

He should be asleep, and yet he drove them. Thanks with a bit of guilt.

Casual

The Wind Rises

はるばる訪たずねてきたのに 工場こうじょうは立たち入いり禁止きんしだとよ

"We came all this way, and they say the factory is off limits."

All that travel, and they can't even get in.

"Wait, it's morning, and there's a spider?"

Spiders should be gone by morning. Noun + な + のに.

Casual

"If that's the case, I'd have lent you my shower."
