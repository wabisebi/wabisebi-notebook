N5 Grammar

何でも・誰でも・どこでも

Polite

Vinland Saga · Episode 13

なんでもいいんですよ。

"Anything is fine."

Add でも to a question word and it means "any-": 何でも "anything", 誰でも "anyone", どこでも "anywhere", いつでも "any time".

The verb stays positive. On this card, なんでも + いい ("fine") = "anything will do", polite with んです. 何でも is often written なんでも.

Casual

YouTube · video

都会とかいの生活せいかつは便利べんり、何なんでもあるし。

"City life is convenient. There's everything [you could want]."

On this card, 何でもある: whatever you name, the city has it.

Quick Examples

A friend asks what you want for dinner.

何なんでもいいよ。

"Anything's fine."

Casual

A teacher talks about a free event.

誰だれでも参加さんかできます。

"Anyone can join."

Polite

Someone asks where to sit on a train with free seating.

どこでも座すわっていいですよ。

"You can sit anywhere."

Polite

Saying bye to a friend at your door.

いつでも来きてね。

"Come by any time."

Casual

With and without

With 何も

何なにも食たべない。

"I don't eat anything." も + a negative verb.

With 何でも

何なんでも食たべる。

"I eat anything." でも + a positive verb.

One question word, three endings: 何か "something", 何も "nothing" (with a negative verb), 何でも "anything".

Examples from Media

Polite

Aggretsuko · Season 2, Episode 3

何なんでもありません。

"It's nothing."

Careful: 何でもない / 何でもありません with a negative means "it's nothing".

私わたしどんなことでもしますから。

"I'll do anything."

どんなことでも: "any kind of thing".

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe

すみません、何なんでもないです。

"Sorry, it's nothing."

何でもない is a fixed phrase: "it's nothing, never mind". Good to know as a whole.
