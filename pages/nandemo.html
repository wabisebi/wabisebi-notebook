N5 Grammar

何でも・誰でも・どこでも

YouTube · video

都会の生活は便利、何でもあるし。

"City life is convenient. There's everything [you could want]."

Add でも to a question word and it means "any-": 何でも "anything", 誰でも "anyone", どこでも "anywhere", いつでも "any time".

The verb stays positive. On this card, 何でもある: whatever you name, the city has it.

Works the same in polite speech: 何でもあります.

Examples

A friend asks what you want for dinner.

何でもいいよ。

"Anything's fine."

Casual

A teacher talks about a free event.

誰でも参加できます。

"Anyone can join."

Polite

Someone asks where to sit on a train with free seating.

どこでも座っていいですよ。

"You can sit anywhere."

Polite

Saying bye to a friend at your door.

いつでも来てね。

"Come by any time."

Casual

With and without

With 何も

何も食べない。

"I don't eat anything." も + a negative verb.

With 何でも

何でも食べる。

"I eat anything." でも + a positive verb.

One question word, three endings: 何か "something", 何も "nothing" (with a negative verb), 何でも "anything".
