N5 Grammar

ごろ (頃)

Casual

Midnight Diner (深夜食堂) · opening

営業えいぎょう時間じかんは夜よる12時じから、朝あさ7時じごろまで。

"My hours are from midnight until about seven in the morning." [the diner's opening hours]

ごろ means around, about, for a point in time. It goes right after the time: 7時 + ごろ = "around 7 o'clock".

On this card, midnight is a fixed time, so no ごろ. Seven is a rough end time, so it gets ごろ. It answers "When?": around seven.

Same word in polite and casual speech. In writing you'll also see it as 頃.

Polite

Million Yen Woman · Episode 7

何時なんじ頃ごろに帰かえってきますか？

"Around what time will he be home?" [a reporter asking]

ごろ goes right after a time and means "around". On this card, 何時 ("what time") + ごろ asks for a rough time, so "around 9" is a fine answer. ます makes the question polite.

Examples

A coworker asks when you'll get to the office.

9時じごろ着つきます。

"I'll get there around nine."

Some people add に: 9時ごろに着きます. Same meaning, with or without it.

Polite

Your friend asks when you went to bed.

2時じごろかな。

"Around two, I think."

Casual

Talking about when the cherry blossoms come out.

桜さくらは4月がつの初はじめごろです。

"The cherry blossoms are around early April."

Not only clock times: days, months and seasons work too.

Polite

Planning when to meet up.

じゃあ、6時じごろ駅えきで。

"Okay, at the station around six, then."

Casual

With and without

Without

7時じに起おきました。

"I got up at seven." Sounds exact.

With ごろ

7時じごろ起おきました。

"I got up around seven." A rough time.

ごろ or ぐらい?

ぐらい: how long

2時間じかんぐらい寝ねました。

"I slept for about two hours." An amount of time.

ごろ: when

2時じごろ寝ねました。

"I went to bed around two." A point in time.

Weakest Beast · Episode 10

"Around what time is Shinkai coming?"

何時 ("what time") + ごろ = "around what time?".

Polite

Border · Episode 1

12時じごろから5時じごろまでです。

"From around 12 to around 5."

ごろ works with から and まで too.

Polite

Legal High · Episode 9

症状しょうじょうが出で始はじめたのは いつごろですか？

"Around when did the symptoms start?"

いつ ("when") + ごろ = "around when?". 症状が出始めた: "the symptoms started".

Casual

I am Mita, Your Housekeeper · Episode 9

今日きょう 帰かえり何時なんじごろ？

"Around what time are you coming home today?"

何時 ("what time") + ごろ = "around what time?". 帰り: "coming home". Casual: no です.
