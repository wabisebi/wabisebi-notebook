N5 Grammar

い-Adjective (Negative)

YouTube · video

じゃあ、あんまり名古屋詳しくない。

"Well, [then] I don't really know Nagoya that well."

To say something is not a certain way, swap the last い of an い-adjective for くない: 詳しい (knows well) → 詳しくない (doesn't know well).

The speaker just worked out they've barely spent time in Nagoya. あんまり + くない is "not very". It answers "Do you know Nagoya?".

Polite: 詳しくないです. Watch out: いい becomes よくない, never いくない.

Examples

A friend worries the hike will be too much for you.

大丈夫、そんなに遠くないよ。

"It's fine, it's not that far."

Casual

The shop clerk asks if the coat is too heavy.

いえ、全然重くないです。

"No, it's not heavy at all."

For polite, just put です after くない.

Polite

Someone offers you the spicy curry. You try it.

あれ、辛くない。

"Huh, it's not spicy."

Casual

A coworker asks how you're feeling today.

ちょっと調子がよくないです。

"I'm not feeling great."

いい is the odd one. It turns into よくない, never いくない.

Polite

With and without

Is

高い。

"It's expensive."

Isn't

高くない。

"It's not expensive." Drop the い, add くない.

Polite

高くないです。

Same thing, polite. Also fine: 高くありません, a bit stiffer.

Not this

高いじゃない。

じゃない is for nouns and な-adjectives. い-adjectives use くない.
