N5 Grammar

で (Means)

YouTube · video

箕面市は電車で来ることができます。

"You can get to Minoh City by train."

で after a thing says what you use to do something: by, with, in. 電車 + で = by train. 箸で (with chopsticks), 日本語で (in Japanese) work the same way.

On this card, the sentence answers "How do I get there?": by train.

It's the same で as "at a place" (家で, at home), only here it comes after a tool, a vehicle or a language. で never changes; polite or casual shows at the end.

Examples

A friend asks how you get to work.

自転車で行く。

"I go by bike."

Casual

At a restaurant, the waiter offers you a fork.

大丈夫です、箸で食べます。

"It's okay, I'll eat with chopsticks."

Polite

At the register, your friend asks if you're paying cash.

ううん、カードで払う。

"No, I'll pay by card."

Casual

The teacher asks if you wrote your essay in English.

いいえ、日本語で書きました。

"No, I wrote it in Japanese."

Polite

With and without

Plain

東京に行きます。

"I'm going to Tokyo."

With で

新幹線で東京に行きます。

"I'm going to Tokyo by shinkansen." で says how.

With で

箸で食べます。

"I eat with chopsticks." The chopsticks are the tool.

Not this

箸を食べます。

"I eat chopsticks." を makes them the food.

Sentence mining

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 6

あれか愛車でアメリカを横断した時のやつさ

"Oh, that? It's from when I drove across America in my car."

愛車 is "my beloved car". 愛車で: by car.

Polite

この動画の感想を是非日本語でコメント欄に書いてください。

"Please write what you thought of this video in the comments, in Japanese."

日本語で: in Japanese. コメント欄に: where it goes.

昨日、僕は飛行機で名古屋から福岡に来ました。

"Yesterday I flew from Nagoya to Fukuoka."

飛行機で: by plane. English says "flew", Japanese says "came by plane".

"In English, "key" means 鍵."

英語 ("English") + で = "in English". The language is the tool you say it with.

Polite

"That's something you could have settled by phone."

Casual

"Take the stairs."

階段 ("stairs") + で: the stairs are how you get there. 行けよ is a rough "go".
