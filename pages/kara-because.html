N5 Grammar

から (Because)

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 12

雨ちょっと強いから、気をつけてな。

"The rain's a bit heavy, so be careful."

から after a sentence means because or so. It goes right after the reason: 雨ちょっと強い + から = "because the rain's a bit heavy".

The reason comes first, then what follows from it. The sentence answers "Why should I be careful?": the rain.

This one is casual. Politely you'd say 強いですから、気をつけてください.

Polite

YouTube · video

大きい花火大会ですからね。

"It's a big fireworks festival, after all."

After a noun you need です or だ before から: 花火大会 + です + から. The sentence can stop at から when the result is already clear.

Examples

You and a coworker are about to leave, and it's pouring.

雨ですから、タクシーで行きましょう。

"It's raining, so let's take a taxi."

Noun + です + から. Reason first, then the plan.

Polite

Back home after a long walk with a friend.

お腹すいたから、何か食べよう。

"I'm hungry, so let's eat something."

Verbs and い-adjectives take から directly. No だ needed.

Casual

A friend asks why you didn't come yesterday.

忙しかったから。

"Because I was busy."

Answering "why?" with just the reason + から. The rest is in the question.

Casual

You and a friend are looking for a place to study.

図書館は静かだから、そこで勉強しよう。

"The library is quiet, so let's study there."

な-adjectives work like nouns: 静か + だ + から. Never 静かな + から.

Casual

With and without

Without

寒い。窓を閉めて。

"It's cold. Close the window." Two separate lines. You have to guess they're linked.

With から

寒いから、窓を閉めて。

"It's cold, so close the window." Now the cold is clearly the reason.

Not the same as から (From). That one comes right after a place, time or person (駅から). This one comes after a whole reason, which ends in a verb, an adjective, です or だ.

Sentence mining

Polite

YouTube · video

カビが生えてほしくないから、もう毎朝拭きますよ。

"I don't want mold to grow, so I wipe it every morning."

The reason is a whole little sentence: カビが生えてほしくない. から goes after all of it.

Polite

YouTube · video

本当に外国の雰囲気を味わうことができるからいいですよね。

"It's nice, because you can really enjoy the feel of a foreign country."

Reason (you can enjoy the foreign feel) + から + the verdict (いいですよね). English puts "because" in the middle; Japanese puts the reason first.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 2

最近運動不足だから、肩こっちゃって。

"I haven't been exercising lately, so my shoulders are stiff."

運動不足 is a noun, so it needs だ: 運動不足 + だ + から.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 5

大丈夫、体型似てるから。

"It's fine, [because] our body shapes are similar."

Here the result comes first (大丈夫) and the reason is added after, ending in から. Very common in speech.
