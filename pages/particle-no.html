N4 Grammar

Particle + の

Noun + particle + の + Noun

Before a verb, the particle is enough:
友達と約束した = "I made a promise with a friend"

Before a noun, add の:
友達との約束 = "a promise with a friend"

ParticleBefore a verbBefore a noun
と友達と約束した
I made a promise with a friend		友達との約束
a promise with a friend
で日本で暮らす
I live in Japan		日本での生活
life in Japan
から母から手紙が来た
A letter came from my mom		母からの手紙
a letter from my mom
へ東京へ行く
I go to Tokyo		東京への旅
a trip to Tokyo
まで駅まで歩く
I walk to the station		駅までの道
the way to the station

That's all. と, で, から, へ and まで can't go straight before a noun, so の goes in between. The meaning doesn't change.

Polite

YouTube · video

やっぱり、この人ひとと人ひととの交流こうりゅうですね。

"In the end, it's the connection between people, isn't it?"

との is と ("with") + の. A particle can't sit right in front of a noun, so の is added to hook it on.

The particle keeps its meaning: と still says who with whom, の just lets it describe the noun after it.

On this card, 人と人との交流 is "the connection between person and person".

Casual

Slam Dunk · Episode 86

俺おれには 父親ちちおやとの約束やくそくがある。

"I have a promise with my father."

On this card, he promised his dad he'd join the family business. 父親の約束 would be "my dad's promise", one his dad made; 父親との約束 is a promise the two made together.

Examples

You're telling a friend about your weekend.

彼女かのじょとのデート、楽たのしかった。

"The date with my girlfriend was fun."

Casual

A coworker asks how you like living abroad.

日本にほんでの生活せいかつは楽たのしいです。

"Life in Japan is fun."

日本で生活する = "live in Japan" (verb). 日本での生活 = "life in Japan" (noun).

Polite

Your roommate hands you an envelope.

お母かあさんからの手紙てがみだよ。

"It's a letter from your mom."

Casual

At a bookshop, picking a gift.

田中たなかさんへのプレゼントです。

"It's a present for Tanaka."

へ ("toward") + の: a present going to Tanaka.

Polite

With and without

With の only

山田やまださんの約束やくそく

"Yamada's promise." A promise Yamada made, to anyone.

With との

山田やまださんとの約束やくそく

"The promise with Yamada." A promise you made together with Yamada.
