N5 Grammar

どれ・どの

YouTube · video

どれにしようかな

"Which one should I go with?"

どれ means "which one", picking from three or more things. It stands on its own, like これ・それ・あれ.

どの asks the same, but needs a noun right after it: どのケーキ ("which cake"). Same pair as これ and この.

にする = "go with, decide on", かな = "I wonder". Polite: どれにしますか？

Examples

At a bakery with a full display.

どれがいい？

"Which one do you want?"

Casual

Asking the staff.

どれがおすすめですか？

"Which one do you recommend?"

Polite

At a big station, asking for help.

どの電車に乗りますか？

"Which train do I take?"

どの + noun (電車). どの never stands alone.

Polite

A friend shows you a class photo.

どの人？

"Which person?"

Casual

With and without

With これ

これがいいです。

"This one is good." You've already picked.

With どれ

どれがいいですか？

"Which one is good?" Swap これ for どれ. Before a noun it's この → どの.
