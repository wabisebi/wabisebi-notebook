N5 Grammar

でも

Polite

YouTube · video

めっちゃ苦にがい。でも、苦にがさが違ちがいます。

"It's really bitter. But it's a different kind of bitter."

でも means but. It doesn't attach to anything: it stands at the start of a new sentence.

On this card, the first sentence ends (苦い。), then でも turns things around: bitter, but not the bitterness you'd expect.

Works in polite and casual speech alike. Usually followed by a small pause (でも、).

Casual

The Journalist · Episode 6

でも狭せまいぞ。

"But it's small."

でも at the start of a sentence means "but", same in polite and casual talk. On this card, 狭い = "small, cramped", and ぞ adds a rough "I'm telling you".

Quick Examples

A friend asks about your new apartment.

駅えきから遠とおい。でも、広ひろいよ。

"It's far from the station. But it's big."

Casual

Your teacher asks if you did the homework.

はい、やりました。でも、家いえに忘わすれました。

"Yes, I did it. But I left it at home."

Polite

Someone says sushi is expensive in Japan.

でも、回転かいてん寿司すしは安やすいですよ。

"But conveyor-belt sushi is cheap."

The first sentence can come from the other person. でも pushes back on what they just said.

Polite

Your friend wants to go out, but you're tired.

行いきたい。でも、眠ねむい…

"I want to go. But I'm sleepy..."

Casual

With and without

Without

この店みせは高たかい。おいしい。

"This place is expensive. It's good." Two facts, no link.

With でも

この店みせは高たかい。でも、おいしい。

"This place is expensive. But it's good." Now the second sentence pushes back against the first.

Same "but" as けど and が (But), just in a different spot. けど and が glue onto the end of the first part (高いけど、おいしい). でも starts a fresh sentence (高い。でも、おいしい).

Examples from Media

Polite

Legal High · Episode 6

でも、無駄むだでした。

"But it was pointless."

でも ("but") + 無駄でした ("it was pointless"). でも starts a new sentence that goes against the last one.

Polite

Sailor Suit and Machine Gun · Episode 4

でも 幸しあわせです。

"But I'm happy."

でも ("but") + 幸せです ("I'm happy"). Despite everything before, she's happy.

Casual

YouTube · video

でも、さすがにもう穴掘あなほりもしたいからな。

"But I really want to do some digging now too."

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 14

でもむきむきすぎないの

"But not too muscly [okay?]"

In fast casual speech the pause after でも often disappears.

Casual

The Cat Returns

でも ムタさんは？

"But what about Muta?"

でも ("but") + ムタさんは？ ("what about Muta?"). は？ asks about someone else.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 5

でもどうして？

"But why?"

でも starts the new sentence.
