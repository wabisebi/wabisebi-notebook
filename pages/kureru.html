N5 Grammar

くれる・てくれる

Polite

Samurai Champloo · Episode 26

母かあさんが死しぬとき、そう教おしえてくれました。

"When my mom was dying, that's what she told me."

くれる means someone gives to me (or to my side: my family, my friends). After a て-form it means someone does something for me.

On this card: 教えて + くれる, "told me", as something she gave him. It answers "Who told you that?": my mom.

Polite past: くれました. Casual: くれた.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 2

じゃ、白熊しろくま君くん、肩かた揉もんでくれる？

"Then, Polar Bear, will you massage my shoulders?"

Casual: plain くれる. Asked as a question, it's a friendly favor: "will you do this for me?"

Quick Examples

Your friend just got back from Kyoto and handed you a box of sweets. You tell your sister.

友達ともだちがお土産みやげをくれた。

"My friend gave me a souvenir."

Plain くれる: the thing comes to me. No "me" needed, くれる already says it.

Casual

A coworker likes your new pen and asks where it's from.

これ、部長ぶちょうがくれました。

"My boss gave me this."

Only toward you or your side. When YOU give something, it's a different verb (あげる).

Polite

You missed the bus, but your mom drove you to the station.

母ははが駅えきまで送おくってくれました。

"My mom drove me to the station."

送って + くれる: she did it for me.

Polite

Your friend is about to leave, but you need one more minute.

ちょっと待まってくれる？

"Can you wait a sec?"

A question with くれる = asking a favor.

Casual

With and without

Without

友達ともだちが手伝てつだいました。

"My friend helped." Just a fact. Helped who? Not said.

With くれる

友達ともだちが手伝てつだってくれました。

"My friend helped me out." It was for me, and I'm glad.

Examples from Media

Polite

The Way of the Househusband · Episode 4

みんなのためにサンタさんが来きてくれてます。

"Santa has come for everyone."

来てくれる = "come for us", a kindness. てます is the spoken ています.

Polite

Aggretsuko · Season 1, Episode 7

社長しゃちょうも分わかってくれますよ。

"The boss will understand too."

分かってくれる = "understand (for me)", be on my side.

Polite

Oishinbo · Episode 87

サンタクロースさんが来きてくれました。

"Santa Claus has come to see us."

来て + くれました. Santa came, for us.

Casual

Vinland Saga · Episode 19

教おしえてくれ。

"Tell me."

てくれ: a rough request, "do it for me".

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 12

パンダのストラップもつけてくれるし

"And they even put a panda strap on it for you."

つけて + くれる: the shop does it for you.

Casual

Goblin Slayer · Episode 10

大好物だいこうぶつのシチューを作つくってくれた。

"She made my favorite stew for me."

Casual past: くれた. 大好物 = "favorite food".

Casual

Death Note · Episode 5

ちょっと待まってくれ。

"Wait a second."

Casual

Slam Dunk · Episode 43

任まかせてくれ。

"Leave it to me."
