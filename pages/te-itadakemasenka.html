N4 Grammar

～ていただけませんか

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 5

お花はな、選えらんでいただけますか？

"Could you please choose some flowers?"

～ていただけませんか (or ～ていただけますか) is the most polite way to ask a favor: "could you please...?". It's the humble いただく in its can-form: "could I receive this from you?".

On this card, Panda asks the florist to pick flowers for him. Very polite, for a stranger at a shop.

Even softer: ～ていただけないでしょうか.

Polite

Oishinbo · Episode 38

悟さとるさんを説得せっとくしていただけないでしょうか？

"Would you please talk Satoru round?"

On this card, a woman begs Yamaoka to help. いただけないでしょうか: as polite as it gets.
