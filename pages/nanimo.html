N5 Grammar

何も・誰も・どこも

YouTube · Road to Vostok #1

何も持てませんよ。

"I can't carry anything." [My bag is full.]

Add も to a question word and put a negative verb after it: 何も + ません = "nothing, not anything".

Same trick for the others: 誰も "nobody", どこも "nowhere". The verb has to be negative.

Casual: 何も持てない.

Examples

You open the fridge.

何もない。

"There's nothing."

Casual

You call out in an empty office.

誰もいません。

"Nobody's here."

Polite

A friend asks about your weekend.

どこにも行かなかった。

"I didn't go anywhere."

With に, it goes between: どこ + に + も.

Casual

A doctor asks about breakfast.

今朝は何も食べませんでした。

"I didn't eat anything this morning."

Polite

With and without

With 何か

何か食べた？

"Did you eat something?"

With 何も

何も食べなかった。

"I didn't eat anything." も + a negative verb.

Sentence mining

Polite

YouTube · video

あ、ちょっと何もないけど左に曲がりますね。

"Ah, there's nothing here really, but I'll turn left."

何も + ない = "there's nothing".

Polite

YouTube · video

あ、これ何も塩胡椒してないですね。

"Ah, I haven't put any salt or pepper on this."

何も + してない = "haven't done anything (to it)".
