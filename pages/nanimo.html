N5 Grammar

何も・誰も・どこも

Polite何も～ません verbs
何も～ないです adjectives, and verbs in everyday speech
Casual何も～ない

Same meaning: "nothing, not anything". Only the politeness changes.

Polite

YouTube · Road to Vostok #1

何なにも持もてませんよ。

"I can't carry anything." [My bag is full.]

Add も to a question word and put a negative verb after it: 何も + ません = "nothing, not anything".

Same trick for the others: 誰も "nobody", どこも "nowhere". The verb has to be negative.

Casual: 何も持てない.

Casual

Dungeon Meshi · Episode 3

何なにもない!

"There's nothing!"

何も + a negative means "nothing". On this card, he opens a box: 何も + ない = "nothing at all".

Quick Examples

You open the fridge.

何なにもない。

"There's nothing."

Casual

You call out in an empty office.

誰だれもいません。

"Nobody's here."

Polite

A friend asks about your weekend.

どこにも行いかなかった。

"I didn't go anywhere."

With に, it goes between: どこ + に + も.

Casual

A doctor asks about breakfast.

今朝けさは何なにも食たべませんでした。

"I didn't eat anything this morning."

Polite

With and without

With 何か

何なにか食たべた？

"Did you eat something?"

With 何も

何なにも食たべなかった。

"I didn't eat anything." も + a negative verb.
