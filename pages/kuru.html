来る

PoliteCasual
come来ます (きます)来る (くる)
don't come来ません (きません)来ない (こない)
came来ました (きました)来た (きた)
didn't come来ませんでした (きませんでした)来なかった (こなかった)

Same meaning in each row: only the politeness changes. The kanji stays 来, only its reading moves: く in 来る, こ in 来ない and 来なかった, き everywhere else.

Japanese has three types of verbs. This page is about the third one, the irregular verbs: 来る.

How to tell which type a verb is
  1. Step 1

    Is it する or 来る?

    Yes: Irregular. No: go to step 2.

  2. Step 2

    Does it end in る?

    No: う-verb (書く, 飲む). Yes: go to step 3.

  3. Step 3

    Is the sound just before る an i or an e?

    No: う-verb (分かる wa-ka-ru, 作る tsu-ku-ru).

    Yes: usually a る-verb (見る mi-ru, 食べる ta-be-ru).

A few break step 3. These look like る-verbs but are う-verbs: 帰る, 入る, 走る, 知る.

Polite

When Marnie Was There

おばちゃんが来きました。

"Auntie's here."

来る (くる) means to come: to move toward the place where the speaker is. It's an irregular verb, so the kanji stays the same but the reading changes (くる, き, こ).

On this card, Auntie has just arrived where the speaker is: 来る → 来ました (きました), the polite past.

Casual

My Neighbor Totoro

あっ、ちょうど来きたよ。

"Oh, here it comes."

On this card, what they've been waiting for has just shown up. 来た (きた) is the casual past, and ちょうど = "just, right now".

Quick Examples

You're on the phone with a friend. Your dinner plans are tonight.

今夜こんや、うちに来くる？

"Are you coming to my place tonight?"

Just 来る with a rising voice, no か needed.

Casual

You're the manager. A new hire is starting tomorrow.

明日あしたは何なん時じに来きますか。

"What time will you come tomorrow?"

来る → 来ます. Say it きます.

Polite

A friend asks if Ken is joining the barbecue.

ケンは今日きょうは来こないよ。

"Ken isn't coming today."

Not 来らない. The く turns into こ: こない. Polite: 来ません (きません).

Casual

Your friend asks who showed up at the office this morning.

社長しゃちょうが朝あさ早はやく来きました。

"The boss came in early this morning."

Polite

Your mom calls you down: dinner is ready.

今いま、行いく！

"I'm coming!"

English says "coming", Japanese says "going" (行く) when you head to where the other person is. 来る is for coming to where you are, or will be.

Casual

With and without

Casual

友達ともだちが来くる。

"A friend comes." Reading: くる.

Not coming

友達ともだちが来こない。

"A friend doesn't come." Reading: こない.

Polite

友達ともだちが来きます。

Same as 来る, polite. Reading: きます.

Not this

友達ともだちが来こらない。

That's the う-verb move (帰る → 帰らない). 来る is irregular: く becomes こ.
