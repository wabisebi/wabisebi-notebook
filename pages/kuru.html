N5 Grammar

来る

Polite来ます (きます)
Casual来る (くる)

Same meaning: "to come". Only the politeness changes.

Casual

YouTube · video

ここには、ゴンドラで来ることもできる。

"You can also come here by gondola."

来る (くる) means to come: to move toward the place where the speaker is, or will be. It's an irregular verb, so the kanji stays the same but the reading changes (くる, き, こ).

On this card, the sentence answers "How can you get here?": by gondola. 来る is the casual dictionary form, and it sits right before こと.

Polite

YouTube · video

はい、今、注文した五平餅が来ました！見えますか？

"Okay, the gohei-mochi I ordered just arrived! Can you see it?"

Polite past: 来る → 来ました (きました). The food came to the table where he is, so 来る is "arrived". Casual would be 来た (きた).

Examples

You're on the phone with a friend. Your dinner plans are tonight.

今夜、うちに来る？

"Are you coming to my place tonight?"

Just 来る with a rising voice, no か needed.

Casual

You're the manager. A new hire is starting tomorrow.

明日は何時に来ますか。

"What time will you come tomorrow?"

来る → 来ます. Say it きます.

Polite

A friend asks if Ken is joining the barbecue.

ケンは今日は来ないよ。

"Ken isn't coming today."

Not 来らない. The く turns into こ: こない. Polite: 来ません (きません).

Casual

Your friend asks who showed up at the office this morning.

社長が朝早く来ました。

"The boss came in early this morning."

Polite

Your mom calls you down: dinner is ready.

今、行く！

"I'm coming!"

English says "coming", Japanese says "going" (行く) when you head to where the other person is. 来る is for coming to where you are, or will be.

Casual

With and without

Casual

友達が来る。

"A friend comes." Reading: くる.

Not coming

友達が来ない。

"A friend doesn't come." Reading: こない.

Polite

友達が来ます。

Same as 来る, polite. Reading: きます.

Not this

友達が来らない。

That's the う-verb move (帰る → 帰らない). 来る is irregular: く becomes こ.

Sentence mining

Polite

YouTube · video

さっきの方々は、ツアーで来てますね。

"The people from earlier are here for a tour."

来て + います (said as 来てます). They came and they're still here, so English says "are here".

Polite

YouTube · video

この建物も来たことがあります。

"I've been to this building, too."

来た (casual past) + ことがあります = "I have done it before".

Polite

From Up on Poppy Hill

お寿司が来ましたよ！

"The sushi's here!"

来ました (きました): polite past.

Casual

The Journalist · Episode 2

また来るよ。

"I'll come again."

また ("again") + 来る (くる) = "I'll come again". The dictionary form covers "will".

Casual

Weakest Beast · Episode 5

誰か来たの？

"Did someone come?"

誰か ("someone") + 来た (きた, "came") = "did someone come?". の makes it a soft question.

Casual

Legal High · Episode 1

何しに来た！

"What are you here for?!"

何しに ("to do what") + 来た ("came") = "what did you come here for?!". Rough and angry.

Casual

Million Yen Woman · Episode 1

どっから来たの？

"Where did you come from?"

どっから (spoken どこから, "from where") + 来た ("came") = "where did you come from?".
