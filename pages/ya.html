N5 Grammar

や

NHK News Web Easy · article

外に出るときは、帽子や日傘を使ってください。

"When you go outside, use [things like] a hat or a parasol."

や joins nouns like と does: "A and B". It goes between them: 帽子 + や + 日傘.

The difference: と is the full list, や is just a few examples. Hat, parasol, that kind of thing. A cap or a towel would be fine too.

Often など ("and so on") comes after the last noun to make that clear. It only joins nouns, and it's the same in polite and casual speech.

Examples

A friend asks what you had at the izakaya.

焼き鳥や枝豆を食べた。

"I had yakitori, edamame, stuff like that."

Telling a guest what's in the fridge.

ビールやお茶などがあります。

"There's beer, tea and so on."

など after the last noun: "and other things too".

Talking about your weekends.

週末は公園やカフェに行きます。

"On weekends I go to places like the park or a café."

The particle (に) comes once, after the last noun, same as with と.

With and without

With と

ペンとノートを買いました。

I bought a pen and a notebook. That's it, nothing else.

With や

ペンやノートを買いました。

I bought pens, notebooks, that sort of stuff. There was probably more in the bag.

Polite

晴れた日には、南アルプスや富士山が見えます。

"On a clear day you can see the Southern Alps, Mount Fuji and more."

Two highlights from the view. Other mountains could be in it too.

"On the night of the 12th, the aurora was seen in places like Hokkaido and Ishikawa."

Not only those two places, so や and など. Then で once for the whole list.
