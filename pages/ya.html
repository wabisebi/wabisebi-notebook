N5 Grammar

や

Polite

NHK News Web Easy · article

外そとに出でるときは、帽子ぼうしや日傘ひがさを使つかってください。

"When you go outside, use [things like] a hat or a parasol."

や joins nouns like と does: "A and B". It goes between them: 帽子 + や + 日傘.

The difference: と is the full list, や is just a few examples. On this card: hat, parasol, that kind of thing. A cap or a towel would be fine too.

Often など ("and so on") comes after the last noun to make that clear. It only joins nouns, and it's the same in polite and casual speech.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 23

紅茶こうちゃやコーヒーは別べつ料金りょうきんなのよ！

"Things like tea and coffee cost extra!"

や lists examples, with maybe more not mentioned. On this card, 紅茶 ("tea") + や + コーヒー ("coffee") are just two of the drinks, and 別料金 = "extra charge".

Quick Examples

A friend asks what you had at the izakaya.

焼やき鳥とりや枝豆えだまめを食たべた。

"I had yakitori, edamame, stuff like that."

Casual

Telling a guest what's in the fridge.

ビールやお茶ちゃなどがあります。

"There's beer, tea and so on."

など after the last noun: "and other things too".

Polite

Talking about your weekends.

週末しゅうまつは公園こうえんやカフェに行いきます。

"On weekends I go to places like the park or a café."

The particle (に) comes once, after the last noun, same as with と.

Polite

With and without

With と

ペンとノートを買かいました。

I bought a pen and a notebook. That's it, nothing else.

With や

ペンやノートを買かいました。

I bought pens, notebooks, that sort of stuff. There was probably more in the bag.

Polite

Dungeon Meshi · Episode 22

こうして祖父そふや両親りょうしんと食卓しょくたくを囲かこんでいました。

"Just like this, I'd sit at the table with my grandfather, my parents and so on."

や lists examples: "grandfather, parents, and others".

Polite

NHK News Web Easy · article

12日にちの夜よる、オーロラが北海道ほっかいどうや石川いしかわ県けんなどで見みえました。

"On the night of the 12th, the aurora was seen in places like Hokkaido and Ishikawa."

Not only those two places, so や and など. Then で once for the whole list.

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 8

お店みせで使つかう野菜やさいやハーブを育そだてているんですよ。

"We grow vegetables and herbs for the shop."

野菜 ("vegetables") + や + ハーブ ("herbs"): vegetables, herbs and other things like that. を comes once, after the last one.
