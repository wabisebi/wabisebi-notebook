N5 Grammar

が

は vs が is a deep topic: whole books are written about it. This rule covers most sentences you'll meet. The rest comes with time and lots of listening.

A bit more emphasis onExample
がwhat comes before it私が行く"It's ME who goes." (not someone else)
はwhat comes after it私は行く"As for me, I'll GO." (not stay home)
Casual

Demon Slayer · Mugen Train Arc · Episode 5

禰豆子ねずこちゃんは 俺おれが守まもる

"Nezuko-chan... it's ME who protects her!"

が goes right after a noun and puts a bit more emphasis on that noun. Here the noun is 俺: 俺 + が + 守る.

On this card, Zenitsu decides he will protect Nezuko. 禰豆子ちゃんは sets the topic, and 俺が puts the emphasis on him: "it's ME, not someone else."

Hidden question: who protects Nezuko? Answer: me.

Polite

1 Litre of Tears · Episode 1

検査けんさの結果けっかが出でました。

"The test results are in." [the doctor, calling out of the blue]

The emphasis can also be soft. On this card, the doctor just puts the test results up front, because that's why he's calling.

With は (検査の結果は出ました) the emphasis moves to 出ました: "The results? They're IN."

Examples

Someone at an exhibition asks who made this illustration.

私わたしが描えがきました。

"I drew it." (me, the one standing here)

Polite

You look out the window and notice something.

あ、雨あめが降ふってる！

"Oh, it's raining!"

Casual

A friend asks what kind of animals you like.

猫ねこが好すき。

"I like cats."

With 好き, the thing you like always takes が. That's just how 好き works, no extra emphasis.

Casual

は or が?

The green part gets the emphasis. With が, it's the word before が. With は, the word before is just the topic, and the emphasis goes to what comes after.

With は

Introducing yourself.

私わたしは山田やまだです。

"Me? I'm YAMADA."

With が

Someone asks: "Which one is Yamada?"

私わたしが山田やまだです。

"ME, I'm Yamada."

With は

A friend asks: "And Tanaka?"

田中たなかさんは来きたよ。

"Tanaka? He CAME."

With が

A friend asks: "Who came?"

田中たなかさんが来きたよ。

"TANAKA came."

With は

Someone asks: "Where's the cat?"

猫ねこはソファーにいるよ。

"The cat? It's on the SOFA."

With が

You walk into a room and spot something.

あ、猫ねこがいる！

"Oh, there's a CAT!"

Sentence mining

Polite

Kekkon Dekinai Otoko · Episode 11

今夜こんやから みちるちゃんは 私わたしが家いえまで送おくります

"From tonight, it's ME who takes Michiru home." [not you]

みちるちゃんは sets the topic, 私が puts the emphasis on her. Next she says: "I won't ask you anymore."

Polite

Good Morning Call · Episode 12

全部ぜんぶ 俺おれが悪わるいんです

"It's all MY fault." [not hers]

Hidden question: whose fault is it? Answer: me. He takes the blame so she doesn't get it.

Polite

Good Morning Call · Episode 1

一緒いっしょに住すむ カップルがいました

"There was a couple living together."

The emphasis is on カップル: that's who was there.

Polite

The Journalist · Episode 5

家族かぞくがいました

"He had a family."

The emphasis is on 家族 (family): that's what he had.

Casual

Pokémon: Indigo League · Episode 2

これがわたしたちの手配てはい写真しゃしん。

"THIS is our wanted poster."

The emphasis is on これ: THIS one, right here. With これは it would just be "this is our poster".

Casual

1 Litre of Tears · Episode 2

今日きょうは私わたしがやる。

"Today it's ME who does it." [not you, Mom, you're tired]

Hidden question: who does the cooking today? Answer: me, not Mom.

Casual

Kekkon Dekinai Otoko · Episode 11

私わたしが話はなす

"It's ME who talks to him." [not you]

Someone strange is waiting outside, and her friend asks "Then what do we do?" Hidden question: who talks to him? Answer: me, you don't have to.

Casual

Kotaro Lives Alone · Episode 10

だったら私わたしが行いくよ

"Then it's ME who goes." [shopping, not you]

Hidden question: who goes shopping? Answer: me, because you're busy.
