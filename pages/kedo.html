N5 Grammar

けど・けれども

Polite～ですけど everyday polite
～けれども careful, to an audience
Casual～けど

Same meaning: "but". けど is fine in polite speech too.

Casual

YouTube · video

ちょっと見みにくいけど、日本にほんの国旗こっきがあります。

"It's a little hard to see, but there's a Japanese flag."

けど means but. It goes right after a whole sentence part: 見にくい + けど = "it's hard to see, but...".

On this card, the second half goes against what the first half makes you expect: hard to see, but it's there.

けど is the everyday "but", fine with friends and even in polite speech (ですけど). It's the short form of けれども.

Polite

YouTube · video

色々いろいろ選択せんたく肢しがあるんですけれども、とりあえず、まあ、魚釣さかなつりから始はじめてみましょうか。

"There are lots of options, but for now, let's start with fishing."

けれども is the full, careful version of けど, in the same spot. You hear it when someone talks politely to an audience, like this YouTuber.

Examples

A friend asks about the new ramen place.

高たかいけど、おいしいよ。

"It's pricey, but it's good."

い-adjective + けど. Nothing to add in between.

Casual

Your friend invites you out tonight.

行いきたいけど、明日あした早はやいんだ。

"I want to go, but I have an early day tomorrow."

Casual

Talking about your Japanese.

日本にほん語ごは好すきだけど、文法ぶんぽうは難むずかしい。

"I like Japanese, but grammar is hard."

After a な-adjective or a noun, add だ first: 好きだけど, 学生だけど. Without だ it sounds cut off.

Casual

At a hotel desk, asking for help.

すみません、Wi-Fiが使つかえないんですけど…

"Excuse me, the Wi-Fi isn't working... [can you help?]"

Stopping at けど makes it soft. You say the problem and let the other person offer help.

Polite

With and without

Without

この部屋へやは狭せまい。安やすい。

"This room is small. It's cheap." Two facts, no link.

With けど

この部屋へやは狭せまいけど、安やすい。

"This room is small, but it's cheap." Now the second part pushes back against the first.

Same job as が (But). が sounds a bit more formal; けど works almost everywhere.

Sentence mining

Polite

YouTube · video

楽たのしいんですけど、売上うりあげは下さがってきてます。

"It's fun, but sales are going down."

んです + けど: very common in polite speech. Fun job, but the numbers say otherwise.

Polite

YouTube · video

僕ぼくは豚骨とんこつラーメンも好すきなんですけど、味噌みそラーメンの方ほうが好すきです。

"I like tonkotsu ramen too, but I prefer miso ramen."

Polite

I'm Taking the Day Off · Episode 4

分わかってますけど。

"I know that."

ますけど: polite, with a "so what?" feel.

Casual

YouTube · video

そう、時間じかんを作つくるのは難むずかしいけど

"Yeah, making the time is hard, but... [it's worth it]"

The sentence stops at けど. The "but" is there, the rest is left unsaid.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 3

新あたらしいパンダグッズ、買かっちゃったんだけど

"I bought some new panda goods, so... [want to see?]"

Here けど isn't really "but". It just opens a topic and waits for a reaction. Very common at the end of a casual sentence.

Casual

Overprotected Kahoko · Episode 3

分わかるけど

"I get it, but..."

分かる ("understand") + けど = "I get it, but...". The "but" is left hanging.

Casual

Sword Art Online · Episode 5

ああは言いったけど

"I said that, but..."

言った ("said") + けど = "I said it, but...". ああは: "like that".

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 29

おいしいけど。

"It's good [but...]."

The sentence stops at けど, the "but" is left unsaid.
