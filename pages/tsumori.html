N5 Grammar

～つもりだ

Polite～つもりです
Casual～つもりだ plain statement
～つもり just the word, very common

Same meaning: "plan to". Only the politeness changes.

Polite

Last Samurai Standing · Episode 1

また、刀かたなを抜ぬくおつもりですか？

"Are you planning to draw your sword again?"

つもり means plan to, intend to: something you've already decided to do.

It goes right after the dictionary form: 抜く + つもり. On this card, it answers "what have you made up your mind to do?": draw the sword.

Add です to make it polite. Here it's asked about someone else, so お is added on top: おつもりですか.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 14

ペン子こさんにアタックするつもりで

"With the plan of making a move on Penko-san..."

Casual: つもり on its own, or つもりだ. Here it's つもりで, "with that plan in mind", and the sentence carries on.

Quick Examples

A friend asks what you're doing this weekend. You've already booked the tickets.

土曜どよう日ひに映画えいがを見みるつもり。

"I'm planning to see a movie on Saturday."

見る + つもり. Already decided, not just a wish.

Casual

Your boss asks about your plans after the contract ends.

来年らいねん、日本にほんに引ひっ越こすつもりです。

"I'm planning to move to Japan next year."

引っ越す + つもり + です.

Polite

Your friend thinks you'll go to the party. You won't.

今日きょうは行いかないつもり。

"I'm not planning to go today."

For "plan not to", put the verb in ない form: 行かない + つもり.

Casual

A coworker sees you packing up early.

今日きょうは早はやく帰かえるつもりです。

"I'm planning to go home early today."

帰る + つもり + です.

Polite

With and without

Without

京都きょうとに行いきたいです。

"I want to go to Kyoto." Just a wish, maybe one day.

With つもり

京都きょうとに行いくつもりです。

"I'm planning to go to Kyoto." You've decided.

Examples from Media

Polite

Aggretsuko · Season 1, Episode 7

つ… 続つづけるつもりです。

"I-I plan to keep going."

続ける + つもりです: she's decided to stay.

Polite

Kekkon Dekinai Otoko · Episode 5

家いえに入はいるつもりですか？

"Are you planning to marry into the family?"

家に入る: here, to marry into someone's family.

Polite

I am Mita, Your Housekeeper · Episode 1

どう責任せきにん取とるつもりですか？

"How do you plan to take responsibility?"

どう ("how") + 責任取る ("take responsibility") + つもり = "how do you plan to take responsibility?".

Casual

Goblin Slayer · Episode 9

そのつもりだ。

"That's the plan."

Casual

Slam Dunk · Episode 53

出でるつもり？ 先輩せんぱい。

"You're planning to play? Senpai?"

Casual

Dungeon Meshi · Episode 9

そんなつもりでは。

"That's not what I meant..."

そんなつもりでは(ない): "I didn't mean it that way".

Casual

Good Morning Call · Episode 16

どうするつもり？

"What are you going to do?"

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 16

そのイスに座すわるつもりか？

"Are you planning to sit on that chair?"

Dictionary form + つもり.
