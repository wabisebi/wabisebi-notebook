N5 Grammar

～てください

Polite～てください
Casual～て

Same meaning: "please do". Only the politeness changes.

Polite

1 Litre of Tears · Episode 1

受験じゅけん生せいは、急いそいでください。

"Exam takers, please hurry." [a staff member at the exam building]

てください after a verb means please do it: you ask someone to do something.

It goes on the て-form. Make it like the past form, just with て instead of た: 急いだ → 急いで, 食べた → 食べて, 行った → 行って. On this card, it answers "what do you want me to do?": hurry.

ください makes it polite: for strangers, shops, teachers. With friends you drop it and just say the て-form.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 14

大丈夫だいじょうぶ、任まかせて。

"It's fine, leave it to me."

Same request, just the て-form with nothing after it. 任せる → 任せて: "leave it (to me)".

Examples

In a shop, you point at a bag behind the counter.

そのかばんを見みせてください。

"Please show me that bag."

見せる → 見せて.

Polite

You get into a taxi.

駅えきまで行いってください。

"To the station, please."

行く → 行って. Literally "please go to the station".

Polite

The teacher spoke too fast and you missed it.

もう一いち度ど言いってください。

"Could you say that again, please?"

言う → 言って.

Polite

Your friend is walking off and you still need your shoes on.

ちょっと待まって！

"Wait a sec!"

待つ → 待って.

Casual

Your friend is busy right now.

じゃあ、あとで電話でんわしてね。

"Okay, call me later."

する → して. The ね makes it softer.

Casual

With and without

Without

窓まどを開あけます。

"I'll open the window." You do it yourself.

With てください

窓まどを開あけてください。

"Please open the window." You ask someone else to do it.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 2

戻もどしてね。

"Put it back, okay?"

戻す ("put back") → 戻して + ね = "put it back, okay?".

Casual

NHK News Web Easy · article

熊くまから自分じぶんを守まもるスプレー、使つかい方かたに気きをつけて。

"Bear spray: be careful how you use it."

気をつける → 気をつけて. A headline, so it's short like casual speech.

Casual

I'm Taking the Day Off · Episode 3

こっち見みて

"Look at me."

Casual request: just the て-form, no ください.

Casual

"Wait for me!"

待つ → 待って: a casual "please wait".
