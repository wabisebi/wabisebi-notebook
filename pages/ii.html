N5 Grammar

いい (良い)

Politeいいです
Casualいい

Same meaning: "it's good". Only the politeness changes.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 5

ふーん和わのテイストもいいけど

"Hmm, a Japanese style is nice too, but..."

いい means "good" or "nice". It's an い-adjective: put it at the end to say something is good, or right before a noun: いい天気 (good weather).

On this card, he's weighing ideas and likes the Japanese-style one. 和 is "Japanese", テイスト is "taste, style". It answers "Is it good?".

Polite: いいです. Watch out: every other form comes from 良い (よい): よくない (not good), よかった (was good). Never いくない or いかった.

Polite

Odd Taxi · Episode 9

ドブさんっていい人ひとなんですね。

"So Dobu is actually a good guy."

Here いい sits right before a noun: いい人, a good person. なんですね at the end makes it polite and adds "so it turns out".

Quick Examples

You open the curtains. Blue sky.

いい天気てんき！

"Nice weather!"

いい right before a noun, like any い-adjective. No な needed.

Casual

A friend asks if they can borrow your pen.

いいよ。

"Sure."

Here いい is "okay, fine", not "good".

Casual

A colleague asks how the new office chairs are.

すごくいいです。

"They're really good."

Polite

A friend asks how your knee is after the run.

あんまりよくない。

"Not great."

"Not good" is よくない, never いくない. It comes from 良い.

Casual

With and without

Good

天気てんきがいい。

"The weather's good." Right now, plain and simple.

Not good

天気てんきがよくない。

"The weather's not good." Switches to よ.

Was good

天気てんきがよかった。

"The weather was good." Also よ.

Not this

いくない・いかった

Feels logical, sounds wrong. Only the plain "good" uses いい.

Examples from Media

Polite

Midnight Diner: Tokyo Stories · Season 2, Episode 8

カレーと麺めんのバランスがいいですよ。

"The curry and the noodles go really well together."

Literally "the balance is good". Something が いい = "it's good"; です makes it polite.

Polite

Dungeon Meshi · Episode 21

最近さいきんの物ものは特とくに評判ひょうばんがいいですね。

"The recent ones have a really good reputation."

評判がいい = "well thought of". 特に = especially.

Polite

Kekkon Dekinai Otoko · Episode 10

あら いいですね。

"Oh, how nice."

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 4

ああいうのもかっこいいな

"Something like that is cool too."

かっこいい means "cool". な at the end: thinking out loud.

Casual

Midnight Diner: Tokyo Stories · Season 2, Episode 2

あなたは今いまのままでいい。

"You're fine just the way you are."

ままでいい = "it's fine as it is". Casual: いい alone, no です.

Casual

YouTube · Japanese Listening Practice With A Story (Informal) #3

みどりさん準備じゅんびはいい

"Midori, are you ready?"

準備 is "preparation", so this is literally "Is your prep good?", which is just "Ready?". いい as "okay".

Casual

YouTube · Japanese Demonstrative Pronoun #1

はははいいよ気きにしないで

"Hahaha, it's okay, don't worry about it."

いいよ here is "it's fine", not "good".

Casual

1 Litre of Tears · Episode 6

いいよ。

"Sure."

いいよ: "okay, sure".
