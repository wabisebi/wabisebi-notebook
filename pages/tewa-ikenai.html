N5 Grammar

～てはいけない

Polite～てはいけません
Casual～てはいけない firm, rules ～ちゃだめ spoken, softer

Same meaning: "you must not". Only the politeness changes.

Examples

A sign in the library.

ここで食べてはいけません。

"No eating here."

食べる → 食べて + はいけません.

Polite

A parent to a kid by the road.

一人で行っちゃだめよ。

"You can't go on your own."

行って + は → 行っちゃ. だめ = no good.

Casual

The teacher before the test.

辞書を使ってはいけません。

"You may not use a dictionary."

使う → 使って.

Polite

Your friend is about to open your present early.

まだ開けちゃだめ！

"Don't open it yet!"

開けて + は → 開けちゃ.

Casual

With and without

Without

写真を撮ってもいいです。

"You may take photos." Allowed.

With てはいけない

写真を撮ってはいけません。

"You must not take photos." Not allowed.

Sentence mining

Polite

I am Mita, Your Housekeeper · Episode 5

混乱してはいけませんので

"...so there's no confusion."

Polite: てはいけません. Here with ので, giving the reason.

Polite

I am Mita, Your Housekeeper · Episode 11

諦めちゃダメですよ。

"You can't give up."

諦める ("give up") → 諦めて + は, said 諦めちゃ + ダメ = "you can't give up". ですよ keeps it polite.

Polite

ReLIFE · Episode 9

失敗は 忘れてはいけないと思います。

"I don't think I should forget my failure."

忘れる ("forget") → 忘れて + はいけない = "mustn't forget". と思います: "I think".

Polite

Border · Episode 9

必要以上に はかなんではいけません

"You mustn't grieve more than you need to."

はかなむ ("grieve") → はかなんで + はいけません.

Casual

I'm Taking the Day Off · Episode 4

余計なものは見てはいけない。

"Don't look at anything else."

見る ("look") → 見て + はいけない = "mustn't look". 余計なもの: "anything unnecessary".

Casual

Mob Psycho 100 · Episode 12

人を傷つけてはいけないんだぞ。

"You mustn't hurt people."

傷つける ("hurt") → 傷つけて + はいけない = "mustn't hurt". んだぞ: said firmly, like a lesson.

Casual

Overprotected Kahoko · Episode 1

寝ちゃダメよ。

"Don't fall asleep."

寝る ("sleep") → 寝ては, said 寝ちゃ + ダメ = "don't fall asleep".

Casual

I'm Taking the Day Off · Episode 7

そこに甘えちゃダメだよ。

"Don't take advantage of that."

甘える ("lean on, take advantage") → 甘えちゃ + ダメ = "don't take advantage of that".
