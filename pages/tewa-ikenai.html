N5 Grammar

～てはいけない・
～ちゃいけない

Polite～てはいけません
Casual～てはいけない firm, rules
～ちゃいけない same, said faster
～ちゃだめ spoken, softer

Same meaning: "you must not". Only the politeness changes. In speech, ～ては becomes ～ちゃ, and ～では becomes ～じゃ.

Polite

Mob Psycho 100 · Episode 5

人ひとに向むけてはいけません

"You must not point it at people."

To say something isn't allowed, use the verb's て-form + はいけません. It's firm, like a rule.

On this card, 向ける ("point") → 向けて → 向けてはいけません.

Casual

Quartet · Episode 7

見みちゃダメ

"Don't look."

In speech ては becomes ちゃ, and だめ is softer than いけない. On this card, 見ては → 見ちゃ + ダメ.

Examples

A sign in the library.

ここで食たべてはいけません。

"No eating here."

食べる → 食べて + はいけません.

Polite

A parent to a kid by the road.

一人ひとりで行おこなっちゃだめよ。

"You can't go on your own."

行って + は → 行っちゃ. だめ = no good.

Casual

The teacher before the test.

辞書じしょを使つかってはいけません。

"You may not use a dictionary."

使う → 使って.

Polite

Your friend is about to open your present early.

まだ開あけちゃだめ！

"Don't open it yet!"

開けて + は → 開けちゃ.

Casual

Your little brother reaches for the hot pan.

それ、触さわっちゃいけないよ。

"You can't touch that."

触って + は → 触っちゃ. Same as 触ってはいけない, just said faster.

Casual

Hiking with a friend, who bends down to a stream.

この水みずは飲のんじゃいけないよ。

"You can't drink this water."

飲んで + は → 飲んじゃ. The て-form ends in で, so you get じゃ, not ちゃ.

Casual

With and without

Without

写真しゃしんを撮とってもいいです。

"You may take photos." Allowed.

With てはいけない

写真しゃしんを撮とってはいけません。

"You must not take photos." Not allowed.

Sentence mining

Polite

I am Mita, Your Housekeeper · Episode 5

混乱こんらんしてはいけませんので

"...so there's no confusion."

Polite: てはいけません. Here with ので, giving the reason.

Polite

I am Mita, Your Housekeeper · Episode 11

諦あきらめちゃダメですよ。

"You can't give up."

諦める ("give up") → 諦めて + は, said 諦めちゃ + ダメ = "you can't give up". ですよ keeps it polite.

Polite

ReLIFE · Episode 9

失敗しっぱいは 忘わすれてはいけないと思おもいます。

"I don't think I should forget my failure."

忘れる ("forget") → 忘れて + はいけない = "mustn't forget". と思います: "I think".

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 12

持もち込こんじゃいけません！

"You can't bring that in!"

Verbs ending in んで take じゃ.

Casual

I'm Taking the Day Off · Episode 4

余計よけいなものは見みてはいけない。

"Don't look at anything else."

見る ("look") → 見て + はいけない = "mustn't look". 余計なもの: "anything unnecessary".

Casual

Mob Psycho 100 · Episode 12

人ひとを傷きずつけてはいけないんだぞ。

"You mustn't hurt people."

傷つける ("hurt") → 傷つけて + はいけない = "mustn't hurt". んだぞ: said firmly, like a lesson.

Casual

1 Litre of Tears · Episode 2

動物どうぶつ飼かっちゃいけないの。

"We're not allowed to keep animals."

飼う ("keep a pet") → 飼って + は, said 飼っちゃ + いけない. の: explaining the situation.

Casual

Overprotected Kahoko · Episode 1

寝ねちゃダメよ。

"Don't fall asleep."

寝る ("sleep") → 寝ては, said 寝ちゃ + ダメ = "don't fall asleep".

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 21

飼育しいく係かかりが自分じぶんで食たべちゃダメでしょ。

"A keeper can't go eating it themselves."

ダメ can take the place of いけない.
