N5 Grammar

まだ

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 10

まだ眠ねむいよ、もうちょっと寝ねよう。

"I'm still sleepy, let's sleep a bit more."

まだ means still: something hasn't changed yet. It goes before the word it's about: まだ + 眠い = "still sleepy".

On this card, it answers "is it over yet?" with "no, it's still going". Sleepy before, sleepy now.

まだ itself never changes. Polite or casual only shows at the end: まだ眠いよ / まだ眠いです.

Polite

YouTube · video

今いまのところ、僕ぼくはまだ動物どうぶつを見みていません。

"So far, I haven't seen any animals yet."

With a negative at the end, まだ becomes not yet: it hasn't happened, but it still might. Here: no animals so far, maybe later.

Quick Examples

Your friend checks if you're ready to leave.

まだ食たべてる。

"I'm still eating."

Casual

The waiter asks if you've decided.

すみません、まだです。

"Sorry, not yet."

まだです on its own is a full answer: "not yet". Casual: まだ。

Polite

Someone asks if the shop is open at 9 pm.

まだ開ひらいていますよ。

"It's still open."

Polite

A friend asks if you've seen the new movie.

まだ見みてない。

"I haven't seen it yet."

"Haven't yet" uses ～ていない (casual ～てない), not just ない. 見ない would mean "I won't watch it".

Casual

With and without

Without

雨あめが降ふっている。

"It's raining." Just what's happening now.

With まだ

まだ雨あめが降ふっている。

"It's still raining." It was raining before, and it hasn't stopped.

Without

宿題しゅくだいをしていない。

"I haven't done my homework." Sounds like you might not do it at all.

With まだ

まだ宿題しゅくだいをしていない。

"I haven't done my homework yet." You plan to.

まだ大学だいがく生せいです。

まだ + 大学生 ("college student") = "still a college student". With a noun, まだ means "still".

Polite

Weakest Beast · Episode 1

まだ月曜げつようなんで。

"It's still only Monday."

まだ + 月曜 ("Monday") = "still only Monday". なんで: "so, you see".

The scary part is over, but the feeling hasn't gone yet.
