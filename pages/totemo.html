N5 Grammar

とても

Polite

Oishinbo · Episode 10

とてもおいしいです。

"It's very delicious." [tasting the grilled fish]

とても means very. It goes right before the describing word: とても + おいしい = "very delicious".

On this card, she tastes the dish and answers "how good?" with "a lot". Without とても, おいしいです is just "it's delicious".

とても itself never changes. Polite or casual only shows at the end: とてもおいしいです / とてもおいしい.

Casual

I am Mita, Your Housekeeper · Episode 8

とてもいいと思おもうな、あの家族かぞく。

"I think that family is really nice."

とても goes before the word it makes stronger. On this card, とても + いい ("good") = "really nice", and the topic, あの家族 ("that family"), comes at the end as an afterthought.

Quick Examples

Small talk with a neighbour on a winter morning.

今日きょうはとても寒さむいですね。

"It's very cold today, isn't it."

Polite

Someone asks what your hometown is like.

とても静しずかな町まちです。

"It's a very quiet town."

Before a noun, とても still sits in front of the describing word: とても静かな + 町.

Polite

You get home after a long day of moving boxes.

とても疲つかれた。

"I'm really tired."

とても also works with feeling words that are verbs: 疲れる, 困る, 驚く.

Casual

A friend lets you try their curry.

とってもおいしい！

"Super tasty!"

とても sounds a little neat. With friends, people often say とっても (warmer, stronger) or すごく instead.

Casual

With and without

Without

この本ほんは面白おもしろいです。

"This book is interesting." A plain fact.

With とても

この本ほんはとても面白おもしろいです。

"This book is very interesting." You really mean it.

Examples from Media

Polite

Howl's Moving Castle

あの子こはとても危険きけんです。

"That boy is very dangerous."

とても + 危険 = "very dangerous". とても sits right before the describing word.

Polite

Kekkon Dekinai Otoko · Episode 1

そうですか とても残念ざんねんです。

"I see. That's a real shame."

Polite

Aggretsuko · Season 1, Episode 9

私わたし 今いま とっても幸しあわせです。

"I'm really happy right now."

Polite

1 Litre of Tears · Episode 2

とっても大切たいせつなことなんですよ。

"It's a very important thing."

とっても is とても with extra feeling.

Polite

Quartet · Episode 8

心こころというのは とても弱よわいものです。

"The heart is a very weak thing."

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 22

ありがとう、とっても嬉うれしい！

"Thank you, I'm so happy!"

とっても is a warmer とても.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 16

当あたりはずれがとても大おおきい。

"It's very hit or miss."

とても + an adjective.

Casual

Teasing Master Takagi-san · Season 2, Episode 11

とっても酸すっぱいのさ。

"It's really sour."

とっても: a spoken とても.
