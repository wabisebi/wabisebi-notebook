Big numbers go in steps of four zeros: 10,000 is 一万 (いちまん), 100,000 is 十万 (じゅうまん), 1,000,000 is 百万 (ひゃくまん). 0 is ゼロ or れい.

The number goes after the particle: りんごを三つ買った. From 11 on, つ isn't used, just the plain number (じゅういち). 20 years old is はたち. じゅっ can also be said じっ.

半 (はん) is half past: 三時半 = 3:30. 午前 (ごぜん) is a.m., 午後 (ごご) is p.m., and they go first: 午後二時. 分 is the same word for "5 past" and "5 minutes long".
