Big numbers go in steps of four zeros, not three: 10,000 is 一万 (いちまん), 1,000,000 is 百万 (ひゃくまん), 10,000,000 is 千万 (せんまん). 万 and 億 always take いち: いちまん, never まん. 0 is ゼロ or れい.

The number goes after the particle: りんごを三つ買った. From 11 on, つ isn't used, just the plain number (じゅういち). 個 is very common in speech, often in place of つ. 20 years old is はたち. じゅっ can also be said じっ.

Add 目 to 番 for "the Nth one": 二番目 (にばんめ), the second one. 羽 is also used for rabbits. 階 and 回 sound the same (いっかい), only 階 turns into がい after 3 and なん.

半 (はん) is half past: 三時半 = 3:30. 午前 (ごぜん) is a.m., 午後 (ごご) is p.m., and they go first: 午後二時. 分 is the same word for "5 past" and "5 minutes long".
