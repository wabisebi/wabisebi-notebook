N5 Grammar

ちょっと

Polite

Death Note · Episode 11

ちょっと待まってください。

"Just a moment, please."

ちょっと means a little, a bit. It goes right before the part it makes smaller: ちょっと + 細い = "a bit thin".

It also softens things. On this card, ちょっと待ってください = "just a moment, please". Without ちょっと, 待ってください sounds more like an order.

To say no gently: 今日はちょっと… ちょっと never changes; polite or casual only shows at the end.

Casual

He Who Can't Marry · Episode 1

うーんこれはちょっと細ほそいから厳密げんみつにはスパゲティーニ

"Hmm, this one's a bit thin, so strictly speaking it's spaghettini."

Here it's the plain "a bit". On this card, he looks at the pasta: it's ちょっと細い, a bit too thin to be spaghetti, so strictly it's spaghettini.

Quick Examples

You try on a jacket in a shop. Your friend asks how it fits.

ちょっと大おおきい。

"It's a bit big."

Casual

At a restaurant, the waiter asks how the curry is.

ちょっと辛つらいです。

"It's a little spicy."

Polite

Your friend is about to leave, but you still need your shoes on.

ちょっと待まって！

"Wait a sec!"

Here ちょっと isn't really "a little". It just makes 待って softer.

Casual

A coworker asks if you want to go drinking tonight. You don't.

今日きょうはちょっと…。

"Today's a bit…"

The rest is left unsaid. Everyone understands it as a soft no.

Examples from Media

Polite

Brush Up Life · Episode 4

ちょっとお茶ちゃしませんか？

"Want to grab a quick tea?"

お茶する = "have tea, go for a coffee". ちょっと keeps the invite light.

Polite

Kotaro Lives Alone · Episode 7

僕ぼく、ちょっと具合ぐあい悪わるいです。

"I'm feeling a bit unwell."

具合が悪い = "feel sick" (が is dropped in speech). ちょっと softens it.

Polite

Sakamoto Days · Episode 6

ちょっと様子ようすを見みてきます。

"I'll just go and check what's going on."

様子を見る = "check on things". ちょっと = just quickly.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 3

ちょっとお手本てほん見みせてよ

"Come on, show me how it's done."

The softening ちょっと: it makes the request sound lighter.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 6

クマ野郎やろうちょっと焼やいてこいよ

"Hey, you bear, go get yourself a bit of a tan."

ちょっと + 焼いてこい: go tan a bit. Rough, teasing talk between friends.

Casual
