N5 Grammar

～たい

Polite～たいです
Casual～たい

Same meaning: "want to". Only the politeness changes.

Polite

YouTube · video

今日きょうは、ちょっと仮眠かみんを取とりたいですね。

"Today I'd like to grab a quick nap." [tired after traveling]

たい means want to: it's about an action you want to do, not a thing you want.

Take the ます form and swap ます for たい: 取ります → 取りたい. On this card, it answers "what do you feel like doing?": a nap.

Add です to make it polite: 取りたいです.

Casual

YouTube · video

じゃあ、宝たからくじが当あたったら何なにをしたい？

"So, if you won the lottery, what would you want to do?"

Casual: just たい, no です. します → したい. "Don't want to" works like an い-adjective: たい → たくない.

Examples

It's 35 degrees out. You and a friend are walking past the beach.

海うみで泳およぎたいなあ。

"I want to go swimming in the sea."

泳ぎます → 泳ぎたい. なあ = thinking out loud.

Casual

A shop clerk asks if you need help. You point at a jacket.

これ、着きてみたいんですが。

"I'd like to try this on."

着てみます → 着てみたい. んですが makes it a soft request.

Polite

Your friend wants to go out. You're exhausted.

今日きょうはどこも行いきたくない。

"I don't want to go anywhere today."

行きたい → 行きたくない. Same as い-adjectives: い → くない.

Casual

A coworker asks about your plans for the holidays.

京都きょうとに行いきたいです。

"I'd like to go to Kyoto."

行きます → 行きたい + です.

Polite

With and without

Without

ラーメンを食たべます。

"I'm going to eat ramen." Just what you'll do.

With たい

ラーメンを食たべたいです。

"I want to eat ramen." What you feel like doing.

ちょっとあの、大だい音量おんりょうにはしたくないんですよね。

"I don't really want to turn the volume way up."

したい → したくない. んです makes it polite here, no です after たくない needed.

Casual

YouTube · video

狼おおかみがうろうろしてるので、近ちかづきたくない。

"There are wolves wandering around, so I don't want to get close."

近づきたい → 近づきたくない.

Casual

YouTube · video

人ひとの少すくない場所ばしょに行いきたい、そうして、僕ぼくは電車でんしゃに乗のりました。

"'I want to go somewhere with fewer people.' And so I got on the train."

The thought itself is casual: 行きたい. The narration after it is polite.

Casual

Weakest Beast · Episode 5

海うみが見みたい。

"I want to see the sea."

見る ("see") → 見たい = "want to see". 海 ("the sea") + が: with たい, が often replaces を.

Casual

"I want to go home."

帰ります drops ます, adds たい.

Casual
