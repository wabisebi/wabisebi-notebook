N5 Grammar

じゃない・ではありません

Polite

Pokémon: Indigo League · Episode 1

ボウヤじゃありません。

"[I'm] not a little boy."

じゃない says what something is NOT. It's the opposite of です / だ, and it goes in the same spot: right after a noun. Thing + じゃない = "it's not (a) thing".

He's been called ボウヤ ("little boy") and pushes back: ボウヤじゃありません, "I'm not a little boy." He's talking to an adult, so he says it politely.

Casual: じゃない. Polite: じゃないです or じゃありません. In writing and formal speech you'll see ではありません. All the same meaning.

Casual

Yu-Gi-Oh! · Season 0, Episode 1

はいおしまい、これは売り物じゃないんでな

"Okay, that's it. This isn't for sale."

Casual version: じゃない. Grandpa shuts down a customer: 売り物 ("something for sale") + じゃない = "not for sale". The んでな after it just means "so, you see".

Examples

A client points at a sketch and asks if it's the final cover.

いいえ、表紙じゃありません。

"No, it's not the cover."

Your friend asks the same thing over coffee.

表紙じゃないよ。

"It's not the cover."

Someone at the post office guesses you're German.

ドイツ人じゃないです。オランダ人です。

"I'm not German. I'm Dutch."

You look outside. The ground is wet, but nothing's falling.

雨じゃない。

"It's not raining."

With and without

だ

雨だ。

"It's raining." Says what it is.

じゃない

雨じゃない。

"It's not raining." Says what it isn't. Casual.

です

雨です。

"It's raining." Polite.

じゃないです / じゃありません

雨じゃありません。

"It's not raining." Polite. じゃないです is a bit softer and more everyday.

Sentence mining

Polite

YouTube · video

このバスは貸切じゃないですよ。

"This bus isn't chartered, you know."

Polite じゃないです. The よ tells you something you didn't know.

Polite

YouTube · video

味噌じゃないけど、名古屋の手羽先も美味しいです。

"It's not miso, but Nagoya's chicken wings are tasty too."

A polite sentence (です at the end), but the じゃない in the middle stays plain. That's normal: only the end needs to be polite.

Casual

New Atashin'chi · Episode 1

それ動物虐待じゃねえの？

"Isn't that animal abuse?"

じゃねえ is a rough, slangy じゃない. Mostly guys, mostly with friends.
