N5 Grammar

じゃない・ではありません

Politeじゃないです everyday, softer
じゃありません standard polite, a bit firmer
ではありません most formal: news, speeches, writing
Casualじゃない

Same meaning: "isn't". Only the politeness changes.

Polite

Pokémon: Indigo League · Episode 1

ボウヤじゃありません。

"[I'm] not a little boy."

じゃない says what something is NOT. It's the opposite of です / だ, and it goes in the same spot: right after a noun. Thing + じゃない = "it's not (a) thing".

On this card, Satoshi's been called ボウヤ ("little boy") and pushes back: ボウヤじゃありません, "I'm not a little boy." He's talking to an adult, so he says it politely.

Casual

Yu-Gi-Oh! · Season 0, Episode 1

はいおしまい、これは売り物じゃないんでな

"Okay, that's it. This isn't for sale."

Grandpa shuts down a customer: 売り物 ("something for sale") + じゃない = "not for sale". The んでな after it just means "so, you see".

Examples

A client points at a sketch and asks if it's the final cover.

いいえ、表紙じゃありません。

"No, it's not the cover."

Your friend asks the same thing over coffee.

表紙じゃないよ。

"It's not the cover."

Someone at the post office guesses you're German.

ドイツ人じゃないです。オランダ人です。

"I'm not German. I'm Dutch."

You look outside. The ground is wet, but nothing's falling.

雨じゃない。

"It's not raining."

Sentence mining

Polite

YouTube · video

このバスは貸切じゃないですよ。

"This bus isn't chartered, you know."

貸切 ("chartered, reserved") + じゃないです = "isn't chartered". よ: telling you something you didn't know.

Polite

YouTube · video

味噌じゃないけど、名古屋の手羽先も美味しいです。

"It's not miso, but Nagoya's chicken wings are tasty too."

A polite sentence (です at the end), but the じゃない in the middle stays plain. That's normal: only the end needs to be polite.

Polite

YouTube · video

もちろん今でも不満は尽きませんが、それでも今の自分は嫌いじゃありません

"Of course, I still have plenty of complaints, but even so, I don't dislike who I am now."

嫌い ends in い but it's a な-adjective, so it takes じゃありません like a noun. 嫌いじゃない is a modest way of saying "I kind of like it".

Polite

YouTube · video

実はこの作品を主導的に製作した金澤麻由子さんはコンピュータサイエンティストではありません

"Actually, Mayuko Kanazawa, who led the making of this work, isn't a computer scientist."

ではありません: the formal version of じゃありません, same meaning. You hear it in news, presentations and narration like this.

Casual

New Atashin'chi · Episode 1

それ動物虐待じゃねえの？

"Isn't that animal abuse?"

動物虐待 ("animal abuse") + じゃねえ, a rough, slangy じゃない (mostly guys, with friends). の？ makes it "isn't that...?".

Casual

Teasing Master Takagi-san · Season 1

いや、たいしたことじゃないよ。

"Nah, it's no big deal."

たいしたこと is "a big deal", じゃない makes it "not". The よ makes it reassuring.

Casual

YouTube · video

マヨラーじゃないけど、ケチャラ。

"I'm not a mayo person, but I'm a ketchup person."

マヨラー is slang for someone who puts mayo on everything. じゃないけど: "I'm not X, but...".
