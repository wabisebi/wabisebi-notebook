N5 Grammar

～とき (時)

Polite

NHK やさしいことばニュース · 暑い日が続く　熱中症に気をつけて

たくさん汗あせが出でたときは、塩分えんぶんをとってください。

"When you've sweated a lot, have some salt." [hot weather advice]

とき means when: it marks the moment something else happens. 汗が出たとき = when you've sweated.

Put it after a plain verb, like a noun with a description: 出た + とき. On this card, it answers "When should I have salt?": when you've sweated a lot.

With a noun, add の: 子供のとき (when I was a kid). With a な-adjective, な: 暇なとき. Politeness only shows at the end of the sentence.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 4

そういう時ときもあるね。

"There are times like that."

時 works like a noun, so it can take particles like も. On this card, そういう時 ("times like that") + も + ある = "there are times like that".

Quick Examples

Telling a friend about growing up.

子供こどものとき、よく川かわで遊あそんだ。

"When I was a kid, I often played in the river."

Noun + の + とき.

Casual

Getting to know a coworker.

暇ひまなときは、何なにをしますか。

"What do you do when you have free time?"

な-adjective + な + とき.

Polite

Your mom reminds you.

寝ねるとき、電気でんきを消けしてね。

"Turn off the light when you go to bed."

Dictionary form: right as it happens.

Casual

Talking about a trip.

日本にほんに行いったとき、富士ふじ山やまを見みました。

"When I went to Japan, I saw Mt. Fuji."

Past form: it already happened.

Polite

With and without

Without

雨あめが降ふります。家いえにいます。

"It rains. I stay home." Two separate facts.

With とき

雨あめが降ふるときは、家いえにいます。

"When it rains, I stay home." The first part says when.

Examples from Media

Polite

Midnight Diner: Tokyo Stories · Season 2, Episode 5

そのときに借金しゃっきんのことも聞ききました。

"That's when I also heard about the debt."

そのとき = "at that time". とき + に pins the moment.

Polite

YouTube · video

これは、熱ねつが出でた時ときに体温たいおんを測はかるための機械きかいです。

"This is a device for taking your temperature when you have a fever."

時 is とき in kanji.

Polite

Princess Mononoke

はい、タタリ神かみに矢やを射いる時とき、心こころを決きめました。

"Yes. When I shot the arrow at the demon god, I made up my mind."

Verb (射る, shoot) + 時 = "when I shoot". 心を決める = make up your mind.

Polite

1 Litre of Tears · Episode 7

病気びょうきのことを話はなした時とき、言いわれました。

"When I told them about the illness, that's what they said."

Past verb + 時: "when I told". 言われる = "be told".

Polite

Kotaro Lives Alone · Episode 6

彼かれに問とい詰つめられた時ときは違ちがうと言いい続つづけたんです。

"When he pressed me, I kept saying it wasn't true."

時は makes the moment the topic: "as for when he pressed me".
