N5 Grammar

ます

YouTube · video

ここに、お湯を入れます。

"[You] pour hot water in here."

ます goes on the end of a verb and makes it polite. 入れる (to put in) becomes 入れます. The meaning stays the same.

Someone shows how to use something, step by step. The sentence answers "What do I do?".

Past: 入れました (put in). Not: 入れません (don't put in).

Examples

A coworker asks what you're doing this weekend.

京都に行きます。

"I'm going to Kyoto."

行く becomes 行き + ます. The last sound changes from u to i before ます.

Polite

The waiter asks if you liked the curry. The plate is empty.

全部食べました。

"I ate it all."

ました is the past of ます: 食べます (eat) → 食べました (ate).

Polite

Your host offers you a beer. You're driving.

お酒は飲みません。

"I don't drink alcohol."

ません is the "not" of ます: 飲みます (drink) → 飲みません (don't drink).

Polite

A client asks when you'll get to the office tomorrow.

9時に来ます。

"I'll come at 9."

来る → 来ます, read きます. ます covers "will" too: no separate future form.

Polite

With and without

Without ます

毎朝コーヒーを飲む。

"I drink coffee every morning." The verb as it is in the dictionary.

With ます

毎朝コーヒーを飲みます。

Same meaning, now polite. For people you don't know well, at work, in shops.

ru-verbs

食べる → 食べます

Ends in える or いる: take off る, add ます. 見る → 見ます.

u-verbs

飲む → 飲みます

The last u sound turns into i, then ます. 待つ → 待ちます, 帰る → 帰ります.

The two odd ones

する → します　来る → 来ます

来ます is read きます.

Sentence mining

Polite

ちょっと厳選します。

"I'll be a bit picky."

厳選する is "to choose carefully". する becomes します. ます also covers the future: "I'll do it".

成田空港で食べました。

"I ate it at Narita Airport."

ました is the past: 食べます → 食べました.

Polite

YouTube · video

このエリアに異常はありません。

"There's nothing wrong in this area."

ある (there is) → あります → ありません (there isn't). 異常 is "something abnormal".
