N5 Grammar

～すぎる

Polite～すぎます
Casual～すぎる・～すぎ すぎ alone: spoken, very casual

Same meaning: "too much". Only the politeness changes.

Casual

New Atashin'chi · Episode 2

もう！しみちゃん、笑いすぎ。

"Geez! Shimi-chan, you're laughing way too much."

～すぎる means too much: more than is good. It goes on the verb's ます-stem (笑います → 笑い + すぎる), on an い-adjective without い (高い → 高すぎる), or on a な-adjective as it is (静か → 静かすぎる).

On this card, she's on the phone and Shimi-chan won't stop laughing. In casual speech the る often drops, like here: 笑いすぎ.

Polite

Mob Psycho 100 · Episode 3

細かすぎますよ 志村さん

"You're being too picky, Shimura."

すぎます is the polite すぎる. On this card, 細かい drops the い and adds すぎます: "too fussy about details".

Examples

The morning after a big dinner, a coworker asks why you look tired.

ゆうべ、食べすぎました。

"I ate too much last night."

食べます → 食べ + すぎます.

Polite

In a shop, you put the bag back on the shelf.

このかばんは高すぎます。

"This bag is too expensive."

い-adjective: drop the い. 高い → 高すぎる.

Polite

You and a friend walk into an empty café.

ここ、静かすぎない？

"Isn't it a bit too quiet in here?"

な-adjective: just add すぎる. 静か → 静かすぎる.

Casual

You wake up and look at the clock.

やばい、寝すぎた！

"Oh no, I overslept!"

すぎる changes like a る-verb: すぎた, すぎない, すぎて.

Casual

With and without

Without

この靴は高い。

"These shoes are expensive." Maybe you still buy them.

With すぎる

この靴は高すぎる。

"These shoes are too expensive." You won't buy them.

Sentence mining

Polite

Quartet · Episode 3

ちょっと条件が厳しすぎません？

"Aren't the rules a bit too strict?"

厳しい ("strict") → 厳し + すぎません.

Polite

Legal High · Episode 6

服部さん。　詳し過ぎます。

"Hattori-san. That's far too detailed."

詳しい ("detailed") → 詳し + 過ぎます. 過ぎる is すぎる in kanji.

Polite

Sailor Suit and Machine Gun · Episode 2

頑張りすぎるんですよ 組長は

"You push yourself too hard, boss."

頑張ります → 頑張り + すぎる. 組長: the head of the gang.

Polite

Weakest Beast · Episode 2

こっちも 焼き過ぎちゃいました

"I overcooked this one too."

焼きます ("grill, bake") → 焼き + 過ぎる. ちゃいました: "ended up (oops)".

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 1

僕、張り切り過ぎてて、途中でばてちゃった。

"I got too excited and wore myself out halfway."

張り切る ("be all fired up") → 張り切り + 過ぎる. ばてる: to run out of energy.

Casual

I'm Taking the Day Off · Episode 1

うん…　飲み過ぎちゃって

"Yeah... I drank too much."

飲みます → 飲み + 過ぎる.

Casual

Overprotected Kahoko · Episode 1

昔から甘すぎるんだよ

"You've always been too soft on her."

甘い ("sweet", here "soft, lenient") → 甘 + すぎる.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 18

怖すぎるね…

"That's way too scary."

怖い drops い, adds すぎる.
