N5 Grammar

ぐらい・くらい

Polite

Odd Taxi · Episode 6

もう100回かいぐらい確認かくにんしました。

"I've already checked about a hundred times."

ぐらい means about, roughly. It goes right after an amount: 100回 + ぐらい = "about 100 times".

On this card, he didn't really count. ぐらい tells you the number is a rough guess. It answers "How many times?": around 100.

くらい and ぐらい are the same word. Say whichever comes out, both are fine in polite and casual speech.

Casual

Brush Up Life · Episode 10

あと２週間しゅうかんくらい？

"About two more weeks?" [when his time off ends]

Same thing with くらい: ２週間くらい = "about two weeks". On this card, she's asked when his break ends and gives a rough answer. Casual, so no です at the end.

Quick Examples

Someone asks how long you've studied Japanese.

3年ねんぐらいです。

"About three years."

Without ぐらい it's exactly three years. With it, "give or take".

Polite

Asking the hotel how far the station is.

駅えきまで歩あるいてどのくらいかかりますか？

"How long does it take to walk to the station?"

どの + くらい = "about how much?": the question version. どれくらい means the same. Answer: 10分ぐらいです.

Polite

Your friend is cooking rice and asks you.

ご飯はん、どれくらい食たべる？

"How much rice do you want?"

You can answer with your hands: このくらい. "About this much."

Casual

A friend asks how much your new bike cost.

5万まん円えんくらいかな。

"About 50,000 yen, I think."

くらい + かな: you're not quite sure of the price.

Casual

With and without

Without

駅えきまで10分ぷんかかります。

"It takes 10 minutes to the station." Sounds exact.

With ぐらい

駅えきまで10分ぷんぐらいかかります。

"It takes about 10 minutes to the station." A rough guess.

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 3

草くさを食たべるくらいです。

"Eating grass is about it." [asked if he has any tricks]

くらい after a verb: "about that much", only that.

Casual

Whisper of the Heart

俺おれくらいの奴やつはたくさんいるよ。

"There are lots of guys like me."

くらい after a person = "about my level". 俺くらいの奴 = "a guy at my level".
