N5 Grammar

ぐらい・くらい

Polite

YouTube · video

フレーバーの紅茶こうちゃで、100種類しゅるいぐらいあるんですよ！

"There are about 100 kinds of flavored tea!"

ぐらい means about, roughly. It goes right after an amount: 100種類 + ぐらい = "about 100 kinds".

On this card, she didn't count them. ぐらい tells you the number is a rough guess. It answers "How many?": around 100.

くらい and ぐらい are the same word. Say whichever comes out, both are fine in polite and casual speech.

Casual

YouTube · video

最初さいしょの1時間じかんは無料むりょうなんだけど、それ以降いこうは1時間じかん100円えんくらい。

"The first hour is free, but after that it's about 100 yen an hour."

Same thing with くらい: 100円くらい = "about 100 yen". Casual, so no です at the end.

Examples

Someone asks how long you've studied Japanese.

3年ねんぐらいです。

"About three years."

Without ぐらい it's exactly three years. With it, "give or take".

Polite

Asking the hotel how far the station is.

駅えきまで歩あるいてどのくらいかかりますか？

"How long does it take to walk to the station?"

どの + くらい = "about how much?": the question version. どれくらい means the same. Answer: 10分ぐらいです.

Polite

Your friend is cooking rice and asks you.

ご飯はん、どれくらい食たべる？

"How much rice do you want?"

You can answer with your hands: このくらい. "About this much."

Casual

A friend asks how much your new bike cost.

5万まん円えんくらいかな。

"About 50,000 yen, I think."

くらい + かな: you're not quite sure of the price.

Casual

With and without

Without

駅えきまで10分ぷんかかります。

"It takes 10 minutes to the station." Sounds exact.

With ぐらい

駅えきまで10分ぷんぐらいかかります。

"It takes about 10 minutes to the station." A rough guess.

Sentence mining

Polite

Kiki's Delivery Service

40分ぷんくらいでしょうか。

"About 40 minutes, I'd say."

40分 ("40 minutes") + くらい + でしょうか ("I'd say"): a polite guess.

Polite

The Journalist · Episode 4

何なん人にんぐらい いるんですか？

"About how many people are there?"

何人 ("how many people") + ぐらい: asking for a rough number.

Casual

YouTube · video

ちょっと雪ゆきも降ふりました。ちょっとね。このぐらいかな。

"It snowed a little too. Just a bit. About this much, I guess."

このぐらい: "about this much", while showing it. Polite start, casual かな at the end.

Casual

I'm Taking the Day Off · Episode 10

35とか それぐらい。

"Around 35 or so."

35とか ("35 or so") + それ ("that") + ぐらい = "around that". Two ways of saying "roughly" at once.

Casual

Steins Gate · Episode 1

15分ふんくらい前まえ。

"About fifteen minutes ago."

15分 ("15 minutes") + くらい + 前 ("ago") = "about 15 minutes ago".

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 15

20回かいくらい。

"About twenty times."

20回 ("20 times") + くらい = "about twenty times".

Casual

Overprotected Kahoko · Episode 7

まだって どれくらい？

""Not yet"? How long is that?"

どれ + くらい = "how much?", here "how long?". まだって: "you say 'not yet', but...".

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 25

一いち週間しゅうかんに1回かいくらい。

"About once a week."

くらい comes after the amount.
