N5 Grammar

ので

Noun or な-adjective before it? Add な first.

WordAddEnding
Noun学生がくせいなのでSince I'm a student
な-adjective静しずかなのでSince it's quiet
Verb行いく–のでSince I'm going
い-adjective高たかい–のでSince it's expensive
Polite

The Wind Rises

大おおきな声こえでお返事へんじをしていたのでびっくりしました。

"You answered so loudly that it surprised me."

ので after a sentence means because or so, like から. The reason comes first: お返事をしていた + ので = "since you were answering (so loudly)".

On this card, the sentence answers "Why were you surprised?": the loud answer. ので sounds softer than から, like explaining the situation, so it's the one you hear most in polite talk.

After a noun or a な-adjective, put な before ので: 平日なので. Never 平日だので.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 45

みんなで釣つったので全員ぜんいんに出だすよ。

"We all caught them together, so everyone gets some."

ので gives the reason, and it works in casual talk too. On this card, the plain 釣った and the よ ending keep it casual.

Quick Examples

You call your boss in the morning.

熱ねつがあるので、今日きょうは休やすみます。

"I have a fever, so I'll take today off."

Verbs take ので directly. A gentle way to give a reason when asking or telling someone something.

Polite

A friend suggests a walk, but you're tired after work.

今日きょうは疲つかれたので、家いえにいるね。

"I'm tired today, so I'll stay home."

ので works in casual talk too. It just sounds a bit softer than から.

Casual

At a hotel desk, the receptionist hands you a map.

駅えきは少すこし遠とおいので、バスが便利べんりですよ。

"The station is a bit far, so the bus is handy."

い-adjectives take ので directly. No な needed.

Polite

You're showing a friend your neighborhood.

この辺へんは静しずかなので、好すきなんです。

"It's quiet around here, so I like it."

な-adjectives work like nouns: 静か + な + ので.

Polite

With and without

With から

雨あめだから、行いかない。

"It's raining, so I'm not going." Direct. Fine with friends, but can sound blunt.

With ので

雨あめなので、行いきません。

"Since it's raining, I won't go." Softer, like you're explaining the situation. Note: 雨だから but 雨なので.

Same meaning as から (Because). The two differences: ので sounds softer and more polite, and after a noun or な-adjective it takes な (平日なので), where から takes だ (平日だから).

Examples from Media

Polite

Spirited Away

私わたし、忙いそがしいので失礼しつれいします。

"I'm busy, so excuse me."

Reason (忙しい) + ので, then the result: 失礼します, "I'll be going".

Polite

Death Note · Episode 20

手錠てじょうは外はずせないので付つき合あってもらいます。

"The handcuffs can't come off, so you'll have to come along."

外せない = "can't take off". ので gives the reason.

Polite

Kotaro Lives Alone · Episode 4

お留守るすみたいなので、預あずかっていただけますか？

"They seem to be out, so could you hold on to this?"

みたい works like a な-adjective, so みたいなので. 預かる = "keep for someone".

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 46

お薬くすり出だしますので安静あんせいにして寝ねてください。

"I'll give you some medicine, so rest and get some sleep."

The doctor gives the reason first, then the instruction.

Polite

Last Samurai Standing · Episode 6

私わたしは仕事しごとがありますので。

"I have work, so..."

ので after あります: the reason, and the rest (I must go) is left out.

Casual

PiroPito · Minecraft #06

歩あるき回まわるともう、敵てきに追おい掛かけ回まわされて殺ころされるので。

"If you walk around, you get chased down and killed by enemies, [so I won't]."

The sentence stops at ので. The result is left unsaid because it's obvious.

Casual

When Marnie Was There

おばちゃんがいつも教おしえてくれるので。

"Because Auntie always teaches me."

It stops at ので: only the reason, the rest is clear.

Casual

Aggretsuko · Season 2, Episode 4

あ… 甘あまいので。

"Oh... because it's sweet."

い-adjective + ので, no な.
