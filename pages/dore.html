N5 Grammar

どれ

どれ + no noun

どれ stands alone, with no noun right after it. Noun after it? Use どの.

どれがいい？ = "which one do you want?"
どのケーキがいい？ = "which cake do you want?"

どれにしようかな

"Which one should I go with?"

どれ means "which one", picking from three or more things. It stands on its own, like これ・それ・あれ, with no noun after it.

With a noun right after it, use どの instead: どのケーキ ("which cake").

On this card, にする = "go with, decide on", かな = "I wonder".

Polite

Frieren Beyond Journey's End · Episode 3

フリーレン様はどれにしますか？

"Which one will you have, Frieren?"

On this card, どれ + にします asks what Frieren will order. The ます keeps it polite.

With and without

With これ

これがいいです。

"This one is good." You've already picked.

With どれ

どれがいいですか？

"Which one is good?" Swap これ for どれ.

Sentence mining

Polite

Kekkon Dekinai Otoko · Episode 6

お好きなのは 関西風 関東風 広島風 どれですか？

"Which do you like: Kansai style, Kanto style or Hiroshima style?" [okonomiyaki]

Three options, so どれ. With only two, you'd expect どっち or どちら.

Polite

I am Mita, Your Housekeeper · Episode 9

じゃあ パパが一番初めに 食べるのはどれでしょうか？

"So which one will Dad eat first?"

どれ can come at the end too: 食べるのは ("the one he eats") + どれ + でしょうか.

Hunter × Hunter · Episode 61

１ 自力探索 ２ トレード ３ 奪取… どれになるんだ？

"One, find them yourself. Two, trade. Three, steal... which will it be?" [ways to get cards]

Three numbered options, then どれ: which one out of the three?

どれ + が + 一番好き ("like best") = "which do you like best?", out of three or more.
