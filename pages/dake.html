N5 Grammar

だけ

Polite

YouTube · video

夢ゆめ応援おうえん面談めんだん　聞きくのは３つだけです

"The Dream Support Interview: I only ask three questions."

だけ means only, just. It goes right after the thing you're limiting: ３つ + だけ = "only three".

On this card: no fourth question, no follow-ups. He's saying it's that simple. It answers "How many questions?": just three.

だけ is the same in polite and casual speech. The politeness comes from the end: だけです here, just だけ with friends.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 5

間違まちがいはないけど、フェアなのに定番ていばんだけじゃつまらない。

"They're a safe choice, but for a special menu, [having] only the usual ones is boring."

定番 + だけ = "only the usual ones". Casual, so no です at the end.

Quick Examples

At the café, the waiter asks what you'd like.

コーヒーだけお願ねがいします。

"Just a coffee, please."

Noun + だけ. No cake, no sandwich, only coffee.

Polite

Someone asks if you speak lots of languages.

英語えいごと日本にほん語ごだけです。

"Only English and Japanese."

だけ can cover a whole list: 英語と日本語 + だけ.

Polite

Your friend offers you more snacks.

一いち個こだけちょうだい。

"Just give me one."

Number + だけ: only one, not more.

Casual

Your friend asks what you did on Sunday.

家いえで寝ねてただけ。

"I just slept at home."

Verb + だけ: that's all you did. The verb stays in its plain form (寝てた, 見る, 行った).

Casual

With and without

Without

水みずを飲のみました。

"I drank water." Maybe you had other things too.

With だけ

水みずだけ飲のみました。

"I only drank water." Nothing else. を often drops out after だけ.

Examples from Media

Polite

Comprehensible Japanese · My Five Favorite Things

でも、昔むかしのキンドルは少すこしだけ凹へこんでいます。

"But on the old Kindle, the screen is just a little sunken in."

少し + だけ = "just a little". だけ makes 少し even smaller.

Polite

Weakest Beast · Episode 9

あとは 出だすだけです。

"All that's left is to hand it in."

あとは ("the rest is") + 出す ("hand in") + だけ: the only thing left is to hand it in.

Casual

YouTube · video

それは、０号ごう車しゃまで行いって、その先さきの運転うんてん室しつでブレーキをかけるだけ。

"That's just going to car 0 and hitting the brakes in the driver's cab past it."

Verb + だけ: that's all there is to do. ブレーキをかけるだけ = "just put on the brakes".

Casual

YouTube · video

二人ふたりの生活せいかつ費ひは月つき6万まん円えんほど、以前いぜんより2万まん円えん増ふえただけ。

"Living costs for the two of them are about 60,000 yen a month, only 20,000 more than before."

増えただけ: "only went up (by that much)". だけ makes the amount sound small.

Casual

Weakest Beast · Episode 3

それだけ？

"That's it?"

それ ("that") + だけ = "only that?". Said when you expected more.

Casual

Overprotected Kahoko · Episode 3

ただ… 悲かなしいだけ。

"I'm just... sad."

悲しい ("sad") + だけ = "just sad", nothing else. ただ adds "simply".

Casual

From the New World · Episode 10

10分ぷんだけだ。

"Just ten minutes."

10分 ("ten minutes") + だけ = "only ten minutes", not a minute more.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 28

1日いちにちだけだよ。

"Just for one day."

だけ comes right after what it limits.
