What is hiragana?

Hiragana is the basic Japanese alphabet. Each character is one sound: か is ka, き is ki. Japanese mixes three scripts in one sentence, and hiragana is the glue that holds the kanji together.

Grammar
Particles and the endings of verbs and adjectives. The kanji carries the meaning, hiragana does the grammar.本を読みました · I read a book
Words
Everyday words that are usually written without kanji.ありがとう · thank you
Readings
Small hiragana over a kanji show how to read it. That's called furigana.山 → やま · mountain
