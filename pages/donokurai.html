N5 Grammar

どのくらい

Examples

Asking the hotel how far the station is.

駅まで歩いてどのくらいかかりますか？

"How long does it take to walk to the station?"

The answer slots right in: 歩いて10分ぐらいかかります.

Polite

A friend just came back from Japan.

日本にどのくらいいたの？

"How long were you in Japan?"

No unit needed. どのくらい covers days, weeks, months.

Casual

Someone tells you they study Japanese.

毎日どのくらい勉強していますか？

"How much do you study every day?"

Here it's "how much time". Context tells you which amount.

Polite

Your friend is cooking rice and asks you.

ご飯、どれくらい食べる？

"How much rice do you want?"

どれくらい is the same word. You hear both all the time.

Casual

With and without

With an amount

駅まで10分ぐらいかかります。

"It takes about 10 minutes to the station." You already know.

With どのくらい

駅までどのくらいかかりますか？

"How long does it take to the station?" Swap the amount for どのくらい, add か.
