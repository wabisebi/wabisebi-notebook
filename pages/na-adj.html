N5 Grammar

な-Adjective

Polite～です
Casual～だ plain statement
～ nothing added, very common

Same meaning: "it's ...". Before a noun it's always な: ～な + noun.

Polite

YouTube · video

ここが上高地かみこうちで一番いちばん有名ゆうめいな場所ばしょです。

"This is the most famous spot in Kamikochi."

A な-adjective is a describing word like 有名 (famous), 静か (quiet), きれい (pretty). When it sits right before a noun, you add な in between: 有名 + な + 場所.

On this card, a vlogger walks up to the river and the bridge. The adjective answers "What kind of place is this?": the most famous one. 一番 is "the most".

The な only shows up before a noun. At the end of a sentence it's gone (see the next card).

Polite

YouTube · video

ここ、夜景やけいがきれいです。

"The night view here is pretty."

Here きれい ends the sentence, so no な: just きれいです. Before a noun it would be きれいな夜景. Casual: きれいだ, or just きれい. Careful: きれい ends in い, but it's a な-adjective.

Casual

Good Morning Call · Season 2, Episode 2

あのキレイな人ひとは？

"Who's that pretty woman?"

な connects a な-adjective to the noun after it. On this card, キレイ + な + 人, and キレイ is the same word as きれい, in katakana for style.

Quick Examples

A friend asks what your studio in Chigasaki is like.

静しずかな部屋へやです。

"It's a quiet room."

静か comes before 部屋, so な goes in between.

Polite

The same friend stays over and notices how quiet it is at night.

静しずかだね。

"It's quiet, huh."

Same word, end of the sentence, so no な. だ is the casual です.

Casual

Someone asks about your favorite food.

好すきな食たべ物ものはカレー。

"My favorite food is curry."

好き looks like a verb in English ("to like"), but it's a な-adjective: 好き + な + 食べ物.

Casual

You trip on the stairs. A coworker rushes over.

大丈夫だいじょうぶです。

"I'm okay."

Nothing comes after 大丈夫 but です, so no な.

Polite

With and without

Before a noun

静しずかな町まち

"A quiet town." な connects the adjective to the noun.

End of sentence

この町まちは静しずかです。

"This town is quiet." No な. Casual: 静かだ, or just 静か.

い-adjective

寒さむい日ひ

"A cold day." い-adjectives go straight before the noun, no な.

Not this

静しずか町まち · 静しずかなです

No な before a noun, or な at the end. Both are wrong.

Examples from Media

Polite

YouTube · video

味噌みそ汁しるは、日本にほん食しょくに欠かかせない大切たいせつな料理りょうりです。

"Miso soup is an important dish, you can't have Japanese food without it."

大切 (important) + な + 料理 (dish).

Polite

Border · Episode 7

有名ゆうめいな政治せいじ家かの息子むすこです

"He's the son of a famous politician."

有名 + な + 政治家 ("politician").

Casual

YouTube · video

急きゅうな坂道さかみちを登のぼる。

"Climbing a steep slope."

急 (steep) + な + 坂道 (slope).

Casual

YouTube · video

な、ちょっとなんか厚あつみがないからなんか変へんな感かんじするな。

"Hmm, it's kind of thin, so it feels a bit weird."

変 (strange) + な + 感じ (feeling). The な at the very start and end is a different one: a "huh" said to yourself.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 4

後部こうぶ座席ざせきって微妙びみょうだよね

"The back seat is kind of meh, right?"

微妙 (so-so, iffy) ends the sentence, so no な, just だ (the casual です). って works like は here.

Casual

Weakest Beast · Episode 10

あら～ きれいな花はな　どうも

"Oh, what pretty flowers. Thank you."

きれい + な + 花: な joins it to the noun.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 45

今日きょうは暇ひまだね。

"It's quiet today."

暇 is a な-adjective, here with だ at the end.
