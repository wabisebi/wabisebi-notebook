な

Source unknown

私の友達は有名な会計士です

"My friend is a famous accountant."

な connects a な-adjective to the noun it describes. It goes right between them: 有名 + な + 会計士 = "famous accountant".

The adjective answers "What kind of accountant?": a famous one.

Only な-adjectives need it. い-adjectives connect directly: 高い車, "an expensive car". At the end of a sentence there's no な: 会計士は有名です.

Examples

A friend asks what your studio in Chigasaki is like.

静かな部屋です。

"It's a quiet room."

Someone asks about your favorite food.

好きな食べ物はカレーです。

"My favorite food is curry."

A friend asks why you never order ramen.

ラーメンが嫌いなんです。

"I don't like ramen, that's why."

With and without

い-word

重い箱

"A heavy box." Connects directly, no glue.

With な

静かな部屋

"A quiet room." Needs な to connect.

"What a beautiful space."

綺麗 is a な-word, so before a noun it needs な: 綺麗なスペース. Like 静かな部屋.
