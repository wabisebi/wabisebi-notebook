N5 Grammar

～くなる・～になる

Noun or な-adjective? Add に. い-adjective? い becomes く.

WordAddEnding
Noun学生がくせいになるbecome a student
な-adjective静しずかになるbecome quiet
い-adjective高たかくなるbecome expensive
Polite

Midnight Diner: Tokyo Stories · Season 2, Episode 4

味あじが染しみ込こみやすくなりますよ。

"The flavor soaks in more easily."

なる means to become: something changes from one state to another. With an い-adjective, swap the last い for く. After a noun or a な-adjective, add に instead.

On this card, 染み込みやすい ("easy to soak in") → 染み込みやすくなります: the flavor gets into the food more easily than before.

Polite: なります / なりました. Casual: なる / なった.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 40

寒さむくなったね。

"It's gotten cold."

An い-adjective drops い and adds く + なる. On this card, 寒い ("cold") → 寒くなった = "has gotten cold".

Quick Examples

You see Mt. Fuji on a cold morning, the day after it snowed.

富士ふじ山やまが雪ゆきで白しろくなった。

"Mt. Fuji turned white with snow."

白い ("white") → 白く + なった ("became") = "turned white". The い turns into く before なる. Don't mix it up with 白くなかった ("wasn't white").

Casual

A kid asks what you wanted to be when you were little.

医者いしゃになりたかったです。

"I wanted to become a doctor."

医者 is a noun, so it's 医者になる.

Polite

Your friend's Japanese has improved a lot.

日本にほん語ご、上手じょうずになったね！

"Your Japanese got really good!"

上手 is a な-adjective, so it takes に too: 上手になる.

Casual

You look at the clock while gaming.

え、もう十じゅう時じになった！

"Wait, it's already ten?!"

Times "become" too: it turned ten o'clock.

Casual

You look at the price tag again after a year.

高たかくなりましたね。

"It's gotten more expensive."

高い → 高く. The い always turns into く before なる.

Polite

With and without

Without

部屋へやが暗くらい。

"The room is dark." Just how it is right now.

With なる

部屋へやが暗くらくなった。

"The room got dark." It was bright before, and something changed: the sun went down, the light went off.

Examples from Media

Polite

Ocean Waves

中止ちゅうしになりました。

"It's been called off."

中止 (cancellation) is a noun, so に: 中止になる = "get called off".

Polite

Teasing Master Takagi-san · Season 2, Episode 1

そして眠ねむくなってきます。

"And then you start getting sleepy."

い-adjective: drop い, add くなる. 眠い → 眠くなる.

Polite

Quartet · Episode 4

友達ともだち、誰だれも来きてくれなくなりますよ。

"None of our friends will come anymore."

ない → なくなる: it gets to the point where nobody comes.

Polite

Kekkon Dekinai Otoko · Episode 2

勉強べんきょうになります。

"That's very instructive."

勉強になる: literally "becomes study", I learn something.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 5

すれ違ちがいになったら大変たいへん。

"It'd be a pain if we ended up missing each other."

すれ違い (missing each other) is a noun, so に. なったら = "if it becomes".

強い → 強くなる.
