N5 Grammar

～くなる・～になる

Noun or な-adjective? Add に. い-adjective? い becomes く.

WordAddEnding
Noun学生がくせいになるbecome a student
な-adjective静しずかになるbecome quiet
い-adjective高たかくなるbecome expensive
Polite

YouTube · video

でも、大人おとなになって、温泉おんせんが好すきになりました。

"But once I grew up, I came to like hot springs." [as a kid I didn't]

なる means to become: something changes from one state to another. After a noun or a な-adjective, add に: 大人になる = "become an adult", 好きになる = "come to like".

On this card, it answers "what changed?": before, he wasn't an adult and didn't like onsen. Now both are true.

Polite: なります / なりました. Casual: なる / なった.

Polite

YouTube · video

え、お家いえをちょっと、え、改築かいちくして大おおきくなりました。

"Um, we remodeled the house a bit, and it got bigger."

With an い-adjective, swap the last い for く: 大きい → 大きくなる = "get bigger". No に here.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 40

寒さむくなったね。

"It's gotten cold."

An い-adjective drops い and adds く + なる. On this card, 寒い ("cold") → 寒くなった = "has gotten cold".

Examples

You see Mt. Fuji on a cold morning, the day after it snowed.

富士ふじ山やまが雪ゆきで白しろくなった。

"Mt. Fuji turned white with snow."

白い ("white") → 白く + なった ("became") = "turned white". The い turns into く before なる. Don't mix it up with 白くなかった ("wasn't white").

Casual

A kid asks what you wanted to be when you were little.

医者いしゃになりたかったです。

"I wanted to become a doctor."

医者 is a noun, so it's 医者になる.

Polite

Your friend's Japanese has improved a lot.

日本にほん語ご、上手じょうずになったね！

"Your Japanese got really good!"

上手 is a な-adjective, so it takes に too: 上手になる.

Casual

You look at the clock while gaming.

え、もう十じゅう時じになった！

"Wait, it's already ten?!"

Times "become" too: it turned ten o'clock.

Casual

You look at the price tag again after a year.

高たかくなりましたね。

"It's gotten more expensive."

高い → 高く. The い always turns into く before なる.

Polite

With and without

Without

部屋へやが暗くらい。

"The room is dark." Just how it is right now.

With なる

部屋へやが暗くらくなった。

"The room got dark." It was bright before, and something changed: the sun went down, the light went off.

とても緊張きんちょうして、頭あたまが真まっ白しろになりました。

"I was so nervous my mind went blank."

Literally "my head became pure white". 真っ白 is a な-adjective, so に.

これは迷子まいごになりますよ。

"You could totally get lost here."

迷子 means "lost child", so 迷子になる is "become lost". The everyday way to say "get lost".

"...and it turned into a fight."

ケンカ ("a fight") + に + なって ("became, and...") = "it turned into a fight". Nouns take に before なる.

素直 ("honest, open") + に + なったら ("if you became") = "why don't you just be honest?".
