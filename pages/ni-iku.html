N5 Grammar

～に行く・～に来る

Polite～に行きます・～に来ます
Casual～に行く・～に来る

Same meaning: "go to do, come to do". Only the politeness changes.

Polite

Legal High · Season 2, Episode 8

麻里奈まりなさん、僕ぼくらは話はなし合あいに来きたんです。

"Marina, we came [here] to talk things over."

～に行く means go to do something, ～に来る come to do something. They answer "going (coming) to do what?".

Take the verb's ます-form, drop ます, add に行く: 話し合います → 話し合いに来る. Activity nouns work too: 買い物に行く.

On this card, a lawyer faces an angry crowd and says why they're there: they came (来た) to talk it over (話し合い), not to fight. The polite part is です at the end.

Casual

Teasing Master Takagi-san · Season 2, Episode 11

じゃ 私わたしと泳およぎに行いく？

"Then want to go swimming with me?"

The casual form: 泳ぎます → 泳ぎ + に行く. On this card, she asks him out to swim, as a question.

Quick Examples

You're heading out. You tell your roommate where you're going.

コンビニに飲のみ物ものを買かいに行いくね。

"I'm going to the convenience store to get drinks."

Two に: コンビニに = where, 買いに = to do what.

Casual

Monday at work. A coworker asks about your weekend.

海うみに泳およぎに行いきました。

"I went to the beach for a swim."

泳ぎます → 泳ぎ + に行きました.

Polite

Your friend shows up at your door without warning.

え、何なにしに来きたの？

"Huh, what are you here for?"

Literally "came to do what?". 何しに is a very common question.

Casual

At a party, someone asks why you came to Japan.

日本にほん語ごを勉強べんきょうしに来きました。

"I came to study Japanese."

You're in Japan now, so it's 来る, not 行く.

Polite

With and without

Without

図書としょ館かんに行いきます。

"I'm going to the library." Only where.

With ～に行く

図書としょ館かんに本ほんを返かえしに行いきます。

"I'm going to the library to return a book." Where, and what for.

Examples from Media

Polite

Oishinbo · Episode 53

お昼ひるに天丼てんどんを食たべに行いったんです。

"I went out for tendon at lunch."

食べます → 食べ + に行った: went to eat. The purpose goes before に.

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 24

パフェは また食たべに来きます。

"I'll come back for the parfait another time."

食べます → 食べ + に来ます: come to eat.

Polite

Kekkon Dekinai Otoko · Episode 5

どうしてもと おっしゃるなら もらいに行いきます。

"If you really insist, I'll go and pick it up."

もらいます → もらい + に行きます: go to receive.

Polite

Only Yesterday

のぞきに行いったんですよ。

"We went to take a peek."

のぞきます → のぞき + に行った. They heard a young woman had come from Tokyo.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 22

ごめん、明日あしたは、パターゴルフに行いく約束やくそくしちゃった。

"Sorry, I promised to go play mini golf tomorrow."

An activity noun works too: パターゴルフに行く, "go (to play) mini golf".

Casual

Odd Taxi · Episode 5

今いまから迎むかえに行いくから、そこにいて。

"I'm coming to get you now, so stay there."

迎えます → 迎え + に行く: go to pick someone up. English says "coming", but Japanese uses 行く: the speaker goes to where the other person is.

Casual

Good Morning Call · Episode 11

遊あそびに来くるのか？

"Do you come here to hang out?"

遊びます → 遊び + に来る: come to have fun, visit.

Casual

Kotaro Lives Alone · Episode 1

買かいに行いくか…

"Guess I'll go buy some..."

か at the end: talking to himself, deciding.
