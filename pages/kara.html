N5 Grammar

から

Polite

Comprehensible Japanese · これ、それ、あれ、どれ

松ぼっくりは、松の木からおちます。

"Pine cones fall from pine trees."

から means from. It marks the starting point, and it goes right after it: 松の木 + から = "from the pine tree".

The pine cone starts up in the tree and ends on the ground. The sentence answers "Where do they fall from?": the tree.

から is the same in polite and casual speech. The politeness comes from the end: おちます here, おちる with friends.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 6

昔から、マイペースだよな、お前は

"You've always done things at your own pace, haven't you."

から works for time too: 昔 + から = "from long ago", so "always". Casual, so だよな instead of ですね.

Examples

A new coworker asks where you're from.

ドイツから来ました。

"I came from Germany."

Place + から: where you started.

Polite

Someone asks when the shop opens.

十時からです。

"From ten o'clock."

Time + から: when something starts.

Polite

Your friend shows you a new sweater.

お母さんからもらった。

"I got it from my mom."

Person + から: who it came from.

Casual

You're about to start cleaning a messy room.

どこからやろう？

"Where should I start?"

Literally "from where should I do it?"

Casual

With and without

Without

駅に歩きました。

"I walked to the station." The station is where you ended up.

With から

駅から歩きました。

"I walked from the station." The station is where you started.

Sentence mining

Polite

NHK News Web Easy · article

マイコプラズマ肺炎になる人が、先月から増え続けています。

"The number of people getting mycoplasma pneumonia has kept rising since last month."

先月 + から = "since last month". With time, "from" often becomes "since" in English.

Polite

YouTube · video

次の日、朝早く名古屋からまたこの車で大阪に向かいますね。

"The next day, early in the morning, we head from Nagoya to Osaka in this car again."

名古屋から = the start, 大阪に = the goal.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 6

たった週2回のバイトなのに、朝から居眠りしないでよ。

"You only work twice a week, so don't nod off [starting right] from the morning."

朝から: the day has barely started and he's already asleep.

Casual

Pokémon: Indigo League · Episode 3

よ～し、これからビシバシゲットしていくぜ！

"Alright, from now on I'm gonna catch them like crazy!"

これから = "from now on". A very common pair, worth learning as one word.
