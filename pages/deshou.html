N5 Grammar

でしょう

Polite

He Who Can't Marry · Episode 1

開業資金はうちの父が貸してくれるでしょう。

"My dad will probably lend [us] the money to start the business."

でしょう means "probably": you think it's true, but you're not 100% sure. It goes at the very end, after a plain verb, an い-adjective, or a noun (no だ before it).

On this card, she answers "Where will the money come from?": her dad, most likely. Without でしょう it would be a sure thing: 貸してくれます, "he'll lend it".

Said with a rising voice, でしょう？ turns into "right?": you're pretty sure and want the other person to agree.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 7

旅行演習したいって言ったのペンギンさんでしょ。

"You're the one who said you wanted to practice traveling, Penguin, right?"

Casual version: the short でしょ. Men and women both use it with friends, mostly for "right?". Here he's reminding Penguin whose idea this was.

Examples

A client asks when the sketches will be done.

金曜日には終わるでしょう。

"They'll probably be done by Friday."

Polite

Someone at the shop asks if the cake is still fresh.

今日中なら大丈夫でしょう。

"It should be fine if you eat it today."

大丈夫 + でしょう, no だ in between.

Polite

Your friend lies on the couch after a long walk.

疲れたでしょ？

"You're tired, right?"

Casual

Your friend can't find their keys.

カバンの中でしょ？

"They're in your bag, right?"

Casual

With and without

Without

明日は雨です。

"It's going to rain tomorrow." Sure, like you already know.

With でしょう

明日は雨でしょう。

"It'll probably rain tomorrow." A guess. What the weather forecast says.

Without

疲れた。

"I'm tired." About you.

With でしょ？

疲れたでしょ？

"You're tired, right?" About them, asking them to agree.

Sentence mining

Polite

New Atashin'chi · Episode 1

冷蔵庫に…煮豆があったでしょう？

"There were simmered beans in the fridge, right?"

Rising でしょう？: "right?". Even at home people use the full でしょう for this.

Polite

YouTube · video

しかし本当にそうなのでしょうか。

"But is that really true?"

Same でしょうか, used to make you doubt it.

Casual

New Atashin'chi · Episode 1

裁縫セット持ってるでしょ..貸して

"You have a sewing kit, right? Lend it to me."

でしょ checks first ("you have one, right?"), then she asks for it.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 5

いやいやいやいやいや、ありえないからがんばってよ、約束でしょ？

"No no no, no way, come on, try. You promised, right?"

Noun + でしょ？: 約束でしょ？, "it's a promise, right?".

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 10

餃子…　メニューにないでしょ

"Gyoza... that's not on the menu, right?"

でしょ = "right?" when you're pretty sure. Short spoken form of でしょう.

Casual

Overprotected Kahoko · Episode 5

ウソでしょ？

"No way."

ウソ ("a lie") + でしょ = "that's a lie, right?", meaning "no way, you're kidding".

Polite

Legal High · Episode 5

でしょう。

"Isn't it?"

でしょう on its own: "isn't it?", "I told you so". She's sure you agree.
