N5 Grammar

か (Or)

YouTube · video

みなさんが日本に来る時は成田空港か羽田空港に到着すると思います。

"When you come to Japan, I think you'll arrive at Narita Airport or Haneda Airport."

か between two nouns means or: A か B, "A or B". It's one of them, not both.

Here she answers "Which airport will I land at?": 成田空港 + か + 羽田空港. The particle for the pair (に) comes after the second one.

Same in polite and casual. Between two nouns か is "or"; at the very end of a sentence it's the question か.

Examples

A coworker wants to set up a meeting.

明日か明後日、時間ありますか？

"Do you have time tomorrow or the day after?"

Two か here: the first is "or", the last one makes it a question.

Polite

The clerk at the register.

現金かカード、どちらにしますか？

"Cash or card, which will it be?"

Polite

You and a friend can't decide on dinner.

ラーメンかカレーにしよう。

"Let's go with ramen or curry."

Either is fine, you'll pick one of the two.

Casual

Your friend asks when you're free.

土曜日か日曜日なら大丈夫。

"Saturday or Sunday works."

Casual

With and without

With と

ラーメンとカレーを食べた。

"I ate ramen and curry." Both of them. (See と.)

With か

ラーメンかカレーを食べる。

"I'll eat ramen or curry." Just one of them.

Without

雨が降りそうです。

"It looks like rain." Only rain.

With か

雨か雪が降りそうです。

"It looks like rain or snow." Not sure which.

Sentence mining

Polite

メールアドレスか電話番号を登録してもらうと、後でレシートが送られてきます。

"If you register your email address or phone number, the receipt gets sent to you later."

を comes after the pair: メールアドレスか電話番号 + を.

Polite

NHK News Web Easy · article

気象庁によると、12月28日から1月1日は、日本海側で雨か雪が降りそうです。

"According to the Meteorological Agency, from December 28 to January 1 it looks like rain or snow on the Sea of Japan side."

が after the pair: 雨か雪 + が.

Casual

YouTube · video

あれは、なんかイノシシか豚みたいな感じだな。

"That's kind of like a boar or a pig."

He's not sure which, so か: one or the other.
