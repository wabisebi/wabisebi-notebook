N5 Grammar

か (Or)

Polite

YouTube · video

みなさんが日本にほんに来くる時ときは成田なりた空港くうこうか羽田はねだ空港くうこうに到着とうちゃくすると思おもいます。

"When you come to Japan, I think you'll arrive at Narita Airport or Haneda Airport."

か between two nouns means or: A か B, "A or B". It's one of them, not both.

On this card, she answers "Which airport will I land at?": 成田空港 + か + 羽田空港. The particle for the pair (に) comes after the second one.

Same in polite and casual. Between two nouns か is "or"; at the very end of a sentence it's the question か.

Casual

Goblin Slayer · Episode 11

襲撃しゅうげきは 今日きょうか明日あしたの夜よる。

"The attack will come tonight or tomorrow night."

か between two nouns means or: 今日か明日 = "today or tomorrow". On this card, he guesses when the goblins will attack.

Quick Examples

A coworker wants to set up a meeting.

明日あしたか明後日あさって、時間じかんありますか？

"Do you have time tomorrow or the day after?"

Two か here: the first is "or", the last one makes it a question.

Polite

The clerk at the register.

現金げんきんかカード、どちらにしますか？

"Cash or card, which will it be?"

Polite

You and a friend can't decide on dinner.

ラーメンかカレーにしよう。

"Let's go with ramen or curry."

Either is fine, you'll pick one of the two.

Casual

Your friend asks when you're free.

土曜どよう日ひか日曜にちよう日ひなら大丈夫だいじょうぶ。

"Saturday or Sunday works."

Casual

With and without

With と

ラーメンとカレーを食たべた。

"I ate ramen and curry." Both of them. (See と.)

With か

ラーメンかカレーを食たべる。

"I'll eat ramen or curry." Just one of them.

Without

雨あめが降ふりそうです。

"It looks like rain." Only rain.

With か

雨あめか雪ゆきが降ふりそうです。

"It looks like rain or snow." Not sure which.

Examples from Media

Polite

YouTube · video

メールアドレスか電話でんわ番号ばんごうを登録とうろくしてもらうと、後あとでレシートが送おくられてきます。

"If you register your email address or phone number, the receipt gets sent to you later."

を comes after the pair: メールアドレスか電話番号 + を.

Polite

NHK News Web Easy · article

気象きしょう庁ちょうによると、12月がつ28日にちから1月がつ1日ついたちは、日本にほん海かい側がわで雨あめか雪ゆきが降ふりそうです。

"According to the Meteorological Agency, from December 28 to January 1 it looks like rain or snow on the Sea of Japan side."

が after the pair: 雨か雪 + が.

Polite

ご神体しんたいか何なにかでしょうか？

Casual

YouTube · video

あれは、なんかイノシシか豚ぶたみたいな感かんじだな。

"That's kind of like a boar or a pig."

He's not sure which, so か: one or the other.

Casual

Samurai Champloo · Episode 16

熊くまか何なにか 見み間違まちがえて—。

"Mistook a bear or something for it..."

か何か: "or something".

Casual

Good Morning Call · Episode 5

そんな 前まえか後うしろかも 分わかんないような体からだじゃ。

"In a state where you can't even tell front from back..."

前か後ろか: front or back.

Casual

Fairy Tail · Episode 12

この調子ちょうしだと 今日きょうか明日あしたにはと…

"At this rate, today or tomorrow..."

今日 ("today") + か + 明日 ("tomorrow") = "today or tomorrow". には: "by then".

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 17

笹ささか竹たけがあればいいよ。

"Bamboo leaves or bamboo is all I need."

か between nouns = or.
