N5 Grammar

どの

どの + Noun

どの always has a noun right after it. No noun? Use どれ.

どのケーキがいい？ = "which cake do you want?"
どれがいい？ = "which one do you want?"

Polite

I'm Taking the Day Off · Episode 6

どの辺がですか？

"Which part?"

どの means "which", picking from three or more things. It always needs a noun right after it, like この・その・あの.

With no noun after it, use どれ instead: どれがいい？ ("which one do you want?").

On this card, どの + 辺 ("part, area") asks which part, and がですか keeps it polite.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 3

ねえねえ　どの寝顔が　いちばん好き？

"Hey hey, which sleeping face do you like best?"

On this card, どの + 寝顔 ("sleeping face") picks from a bunch of photos. No です at the end, so it's casual.

Examples

At a big station, asking for help.

どの電車に乗りますか？

"Which train do I take?"

どの + noun (電車). どの never stands alone.

Polite

A friend shows you a class photo.

どの人？

"Which person?"

Casual

At a shoe shop, the staff shows you a wall of colors.

どの色がいいですか？

"Which color would you like?"

Polite

Friends picking a film.

どの映画を見る？

"Which movie are we watching?"

Casual

With and without

With この

この電車に乗ります。

"I'll take this train." You've already picked.

With どの

どの電車に乗りますか？

"Which train will you take?" Swap この for どの. The noun stays.

Sentence mining

Polite

Only Yesterday

広田くんって　どの人ですかー？

"Which one is Hirota?"

どの + 人 ("person"): picking one person out of a group.

Polite

Kekkon Dekinai Otoko · Episode 4

ああ じゃ どのコースだったら 早く行けるんですか？

"Okay, then which route would get me there sooner?"

どの + コース ("route, plan"). だったら = "if it's".

Polite

Border · Episode 8

"迷惑"ってどのレベルですか？

""Trouble"... what level are we talking about?"

どの + レベル ("level"). English says "what" here, Japanese still picks with どの.

Polite

Wandering Witch: The Journey of Elaina · Episode 5

今まで訪れた国の中で どの国が 一番好きですか？

"Of all the countries you've visited, which do you like best?"

どの + 国 ("country"): picking one out of all of them.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 2

どのカフェがいいと思う

"Which cafe do you think is good?"

どの + カフェ, plus と思う ("do you think").

Casual

Weakest Beast · Episode 8

どの + とき ("time"): which of those times?

Casual

Good Morning Call · Episode 6

"Which girl? Which girl?"

どの + 子 ("girl, kid"): friends trying to spot her across the room.

Casual

"Which person?"

The shortest form: どの + one noun.
