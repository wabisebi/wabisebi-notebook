N5 Grammar

～にする

Polite～にします
Casual～にする

Same meaning: "I'll go with ___". Only the politeness changes.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 19

うん、コーヒー、今日きょうは、ブラックにする。

"Yeah, coffee. Today I'll have it black."

Noun + にする means decide on, go with: you pick one thing out of a few choices. It's what you say when ordering or choosing a day, a plan, a color.

On this card, a regular orders coffee and picks how to have it today: ブラックにする, "I'll go with black".

In the past, にした, it means the choice is already made: 理系にした, "I went with science".

Polite

Weakest Beast · Episode 3

これにします

"I'll go with this one."

にします is the polite ～にする: you've made your choice. On this card, これ + にします is what you'd say when picking something in a shop.

Quick Examples

At a café counter. The staff waits for your order.

私わたしはアイスコーヒーにします。

"I'll have an iced coffee."

Polite

The team is trying to pick a day for the meeting.

会議かいぎは金曜きんよう日ひにしましょう。

"Let's make the meeting Friday."

にしましょう: "let's go with ___".

Polite

Looking at the menu in a ramen shop with a friend.

A: 何なににする？
B: 俺おれは味噌みそラーメンにする。

A: "What are you getting?" B: "I'll go with miso ramen."

Casual

You couldn't decide between two jackets. A friend asks which one you bought.

やっぱり、黒くろい方ほうにした。

"In the end, I went with the black one."

にした: the choice is already made.

Casual

With and without

Without

コーヒーを飲のみます。

"I'll drink coffee." Just what you'll do.

With にする

コーヒーにします。

"I'll have coffee." You picked it out of the choices.

Examples from Media

Polite

1 Litre of Tears · Episode 7

国立こくりつ文系ぶんけいコースにします。

"I'll go with the national-university humanities course."

Choosing a course at school: Noun + にします.

Polite

I am Mita, Your Housekeeper · Episode 4

夕食ゆうしょくは何なににしましょう？

"What would you like for dinner?"

何にしましょう: "what shall we go with?".

Polite

Hyouka · Episode 22

私わたしは理系りけいにしました

"I chose the science track."

Past にしました: the choice is made. 理系: the science side at school.

Casual

Overprotected Kahoko · Episode 1

１万まん円えんにしとく？

"Want to make it ten thousand yen?"

にしとく = にしておく: "go with ___ for now".

Casual

Good Morning Call · Season 2, Episode 7

明日あしたにする？

"Should we make it tomorrow?"

Picking a day: 明日にする.

Casual

Kiki's Delivery Service

聞きいた？　わたしこの町まちにする

"Did you hear? I'm going with this town."

Flying over the town she'll live in. この町にする: "this town it is".

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 20

僕ぼく、これにする。

"I'll have this one."

Choosing from a menu.
