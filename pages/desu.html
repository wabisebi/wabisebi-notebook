N5 Grammar

です / だ

Politeです
Casualだ

Same meaning: "is". Only the politeness changes.

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 5

あ、いいえ試作しさく品ひんです

"Oh, no, [it's] a prototype."

です says what something is, the polite way. It goes right after a noun (and after な-adjectives, covered later): thing + です = "it's (a) thing".

On this card, someone guessed wrong, so 試作品です answers "What is it, then?": "It's a prototype." There's no word for "it". It's clear what he means, so Japanese leaves it out. That's what the [brackets] show.

Use it with strangers, clients and shops. With friends you'd say the casual version, 試作品だ: same meaning.

Casual

New Atashin'chi · Episode 1

ただの落書らくがきだ

"[It's] just a scribble."

だ answers "What's that?" (ただの is "just a", 落書き is "scribble"). Use it with friends, family, or when thinking out loud.

Quick Examples

A client asks what the sketch on your screen is.

これは表紙ひょうしです。

"This is the cover."

Polite

Your friend asks what the sketch on your screen is, over coffee.

表紙ひょうしだよ。

"It's the cover."

Casual

Someone at the post office asks where you're from.

オランダ人じんです。

"I'm Dutch."

Polite

You open the curtains in the morning and say it to yourself.

雨あめだ。

"It's raining."

Casual

With and without

です

雨あめです。

Polite. Strangers, clients, shops.

だ

雨あめだ。

Casual. Friends, or thinking out loud when you look outside.

Dropped

雨あめ！

Super casual. Just the word. Totally normal with friends.

With よ

雨あめだよ。

"It's raining, FYI." Telling someone something they don't know.

Examples from Media

Polite

YouTube · video

これは生卵なまたまごです。

"This is a raw egg."

生卵 ("raw egg") + です = "it's a raw egg". これは sets "this" as the topic: "this is...".

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 5

自信じしん作さくですよ

"It's my best work!"

自信作 ("my best work", something you're proud of) + です. よ: he's telling you something you didn't know.

Polite

YouTube · video

相撲すもうは日本にほんの国技こくぎです。

"Sumo is Japan's national sport."

相撲は ("as for sumo") + 日本の国技 ("Japan's national sport") + です. X は Y です = "X is Y".

Polite

Aggretsuko · Season 2, Episode 9

賛成さんせいです。

"I agree."

Noun + です: a whole polite sentence.

Polite

The Wind Rises

光栄こうえいです。

"It's an honor."

Casual

Pokémon: Indigo League · Episode 2

ろくに顔かおも分わからないひどい写真しゃしんだ。

"What a terrible photo, you can barely see the face."

ひどい写真 ("terrible photo") + だ = "it's a terrible photo". Everything before 写真 describes the photo.

Casual

Vinland Saga · Episode 4

冗談じょうだんだよ

"I'm joking."

冗談 ("joke") + だ + よ. Polite: 冗談ですよ.

Casual

Dungeon Meshi · Episode 15

大変たいへんだ！

"This is bad!"
