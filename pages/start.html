Welcome to Wabisebi Notebook

This is my personal Japanese notebook: one page per grammar point, with real clips from shows, plus kana and kanji charts, vocabulary lists, JLPT info and useful resources.

The grammar is sorted by JLPT level, and can also be shown in the order of the Jlab Anki deck or Minna no Nihongo: open Grammar in the menu at the top and pick a view on the right.

Everything else is in that menu too.
