N5 Grammar

何 (なに・なん)

YouTube · video

日本に帰ってまず何をしますか?

"What's the first thing you'll do when you get back to Japan?"

何 means "what". It asks for a thing, and it sits right where that thing would go in the answer. Here it's the thing you do, so it takes を like any object.

On this card, the answer would be: まずラーメンを食べます. Same shape, 何 swapped for ラーメン.

It's read なに here. Before です, だ, の and counters it turns into なん: 何ですか？ (なんですか). Casual just drops ます: 何する？

Examples

A friend hands you a snack you've never seen.

これ、何？

"What's this?"

Casual: これ ("this") + 何 with a rising voice. Read なに when it stands alone.

Casual

At a restaurant, you point at the menu.

これは何ですか？

"What is this?"

Before です it's なん: なんですか.

Polite

Friday night, texting a friend.

週末、何する？

"What are you doing this weekend?"

何 + する, no particle needed in casual speech.

Casual

You left your watch at home.

すみません、今何時ですか？

"Excuse me, what time is it now?"

何 + a counter is always なん: 何時 (なんじ), 何人 (なんにん).

Polite

With and without

With a thing

週末、映画を見ます。

"I'll watch a movie this weekend." You already know what.

With 何

週末、何を見ますか？

"What will you watch this weekend?" Swap the thing for 何, add か, and it's a question.

やる気がない時は何をしますか?

"What do you do when you have no motivation?"

やる気がない時は ("when you have no motivation") + 何をしますか ("what do you do?"). 何を + a verb asks what you do.

Polite

何をですか？

"About what?"

何を + ですか: "what (are you talking about)?". Read なに.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 11

何の香りだ？　ここのシャンプー

"What's that smell? The shampoo here."

Before の it's なん: なんの香り = "the smell of what".

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe

もう…　何悩んでるの？

"Come on... what are you worrying about?"

何 + 悩んでる ("worrying"). を is dropped, like in most casual speech.

Casual

"What is that?"

何 (なに) + あれ ("that"): "what's that?", turned around the way people say it when surprised.

Casual

"What?"

何 + だよ: before だ, 何 is read なん. よ makes it a bit annoyed: "what is it?".
