N5 Grammar

何 (なに・なん)

Polite～です・～ます at the end of the sentence
Casualno です or ます だ, the dictionary form, or nothing

This grammar has no polite or casual version: it looks the same either way. Only the end of the sentence sets the politeness.

Polite

YouTube · video

そう！10年後は何をしていますか？

"Right! What will you be doing ten years from now?"

何 means "what". It asks for a thing, and it sits right where that thing would go in the answer. Here it's the thing you're doing, so it takes を like any object.

On this card, the answer would be: 先生をしています. Same shape, 何 swapped for 先生.

It's read なに here. Before です, だ, の and counters it turns into なん: 何ですか？ (なんですか). Casual just drops ます: 何する？

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 11

何の香りだ？　ここのシャンプー

"What's that smell? The shampoo here."

何の + a noun asks "what kind of" or "of what", and before の it's read なん. On this card, なんの香り = "the smell of what".

Examples

A friend hands you a snack you've never seen.

これ、何？

"What's this?"

Casual: これ ("this") + 何 with a rising voice. Read なに when it stands alone.

Casual

At a restaurant, you point at the menu.

これは何ですか？

"What is this?"

Before です it's なん: なんですか.

Polite

Friday night, texting a friend.

週末、何する？

"What are you doing this weekend?"

何 + する, no particle needed in casual speech.

Casual

You left your watch at home.

すみません、今何時ですか？

"Excuse me, what time is it now?"

何 + a counter is always なん: 何時 (なんじ), 何人 (なんにん).

Polite

With and without

With a thing

週末、映画を見ます。

"I'll watch a movie this weekend." You already know what.

With 何

週末、何を見ますか？

"What will you watch this weekend?" Swap the thing for 何, add か, and it's a question.

Sentence mining

Polite

The Journalist · Episode 3

ご用件は何ですか？

"What is this about?"

ご用件 ("the matter you came about") + は + 何ですか. Before です, 何 is read なん.

Polite

I'm Taking the Day Off · Episode 4

何をですか？

"About what?"

A shortened question: the verb is cut because the other person just said it. 見ました ("I saw it") → 何を見ましたか？ ("What did you see?") → 何をですか？ ("Saw what?"). を stays because 何 is still the thing the verb acts on, and ですか keeps it polite.

Polite

YouTube · video

この紙袋の中に何が入っていると思いますか？

"What do you think is in this paper bag?"

何が入っている ("what's inside") + と思いますか ("do you think?"). 何 takes が here because it's the thing that's inside.

Polite

Legal High · Episode 11

何の話をしてるんですか？

"What are you talking about?"

何の話 ("a talk about what") + をしてるんですか ("are you having?"). Before の, 何 is read なん.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe

もう…　何悩んでるの？

"Come on... what are you worrying about?"

何 + 悩んでる ("worrying"). を is dropped, like in most casual speech.

Casual

Weakest Beast · Episode 2

何あれ…

"What is that?"

何 (なに) + あれ ("that"): "what's that?", turned around the way people say it when surprised.

Casual

Border · Episode 1

何を見てるの？

"What's he looking at?"

何 + を: "what" is the thing being looked at. Read なに here.

Casual

Sailor Suit and Machine Gun · Episode 1

何あれ？

"What's that?"

何 read なに. A casual, flipped way to say あれは何？
