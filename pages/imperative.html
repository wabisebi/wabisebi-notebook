N4 Grammar

Imperative form

Casual

Good Morning Call · Episode 8

お前まえは、もう帰かえれ。

"You go home now."

The imperative form is a blunt order: "do it!". う-verbs: change the last sound to the え-sound (帰る → 帰れ, 行く → 行け). る-verbs: drop る, add ろ (見る → 見ろ). する → しろ, 来る → 来い.

On this card, Uehara tells Nao to go home while he keeps searching. Rough, but caring.

It's strong and mostly male. You hear it in anime, sports and emergencies. To ask politely, use ～てください.

Casual

Samurai Champloo · Episode 1

急いそげ。

"Hurry!"

On this card, an officer shouts to his men. 急ぐ → 急げ. One word, a full order.

Without

"Please wait." A polite request.

Imperative

待まて！

"Wait!" A blunt order.
