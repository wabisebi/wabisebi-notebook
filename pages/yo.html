N5 Grammar

よ

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 8

取材しゅざいの人ひとですよ

"[Those people?] They're here for an interview, you know."

よ at the end of a sentence means "I'm telling you": you push what you're saying onto the listener. Sometimes it's news they don't know yet, sometimes it just adds weight: reassuring (いいですよ), insisting (信じてよ), firmly confirming (そうだよ). It goes on the very end, after です, ます or a plain verb or adjective.

On this card, the other person doesn't know who the visitors are. The speaker fills them in. Without よ, 取材の人です is just a flat fact; with よ it's "hey, here's the info you're missing".

Polite: ですよ / ますよ. Casual: drop です, but after a noun or な-adjective add だ: 取材の人だよ (取材の人よ without だ sounds feminine and old-fashioned)

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 2

お花見はなみらしくなってきたよ

"It's starting to look like a real hanami party!"

Here よ goes right after the plain verb. They're hanging up flags and sharing how it's coming along with everyone around them.

Casual, after a noun or な-adjective? Add だ first.

WordAddEnding
Noun学生がくせいだよI'm a student, you know.
な-adjective静しずかだよIt's quiet, you know.

Quick Examples

Your friend is about to leave without their phone. It's still on the table.

スマホ、忘わすれてるよ

"You're forgetting your phone!"

Casual

Your friend is trying to decide if the new ramen place is worth it. You've been.

あそこ、おいしいよ

"That place is good, trust me."

Casual

A customer is looking around for the bathroom. You work there.

トイレは二に階かいですよ

"The bathroom's upstairs."

Polite

A coworker is rushing to catch the 6:00 train. You know it was cancelled.

その電車でんしゃ、今日きょうは来きませんよ

"That train isn't coming today, just so you know."

Polite

With and without

Without

おいしい

"It's tasty." You're eating and just say it, to yourself or anyone.

With よ

おいしいよ

"It's tasty, try it!" You're telling someone who hasn't tried it.

Without

二に階かいです

"It's on the second floor." Neutral answer.

With よ

二に階かいですよ

"It's upstairs." Friendlier, you're helping them out.

Examples from Media

Polite

Ponyo

まだですよ。

"Not yet!"

よ tells the other person something they don't know: it's not time yet.

Polite

Good Morning Call · Episode 6

忘わすれ物ものですよ

"You forgot something!"

The other person hasn't noticed they left something behind. よ hands them that news.

Polite

Kekkon Dekinai Otoko · Episode 9

あの人ひとですよ

"[Look,] that's the guy!"

She spots someone her friend hasn't noticed. よ points it out: "that's him, you didn't see?"

Polite

Midnight Diner: Tokyo Stories · Season 2, Episode 10

雪ゆきですよ。

"It's snowing!"

よ: telling someone something they haven't noticed.

Casual

Kotaro Lives Alone · Episode 3

これからもずっと一緒いっしょだよ

"We'll always be together from now on."

一緒 is a noun, so casual needs だ before よ: 一緒だよ, not 一緒よ.

Casual

Goblin Slayer · Episode 10

そうだよ

"That's right."

そうだ + よ: adds weight, "yes, that's right!"

Casual

1 Litre of Tears · Episode 4

御飯ごはん出来できたよ

"Dinner's ready!"

Mom calls her daughter to eat. よ after the plain past: "hey, it's ready".

Casual

Aggretsuko · Season 2, Episode 10

信しんじてるよ。

"I believe you."
