N5 Grammar

よ

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 8

取材の人ですよ

"[Those people?] They're here for an interview, you know."

よ at the end of a sentence means "I'm telling you something you don't know yet". It goes on the very end, after です, ます or a plain verb or adjective.

On this card, the other person doesn't know who the visitors are. The speaker fills them in. Without よ, 取材の人です is just a flat fact; with よ it's "hey, here's the info you're missing".

Polite: ですよ / ますよ. Casual: drop です, but after a noun or な-adjective add だ: 取材の人だよ.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 2

お花見らしくなってきたよ

"It's starting to look like a real hanami party!"

Here よ goes right after the plain verb. They're hanging up flags and sharing how it's coming along with everyone around them.

Examples

Your friend is about to leave without their phone. It's still on the table.

スマホ、忘れてるよ

"You're forgetting your phone!"

Casual

Your friend is trying to decide if the new ramen place is worth it. You've been.

あそこ、おいしいよ

"That place is good, trust me."

Casual

A customer is looking around for the bathroom. You work there.

トイレは二階ですよ

"The bathroom's upstairs."

Polite

A coworker is rushing to catch the 6:00 train. You know it was cancelled.

その電車、今日は来ませんよ

"That train isn't coming today, just so you know."

Polite

With and without

Without

おいしい

"It's tasty." You're eating and just say it, to yourself or anyone.

With よ

おいしいよ

"It's tasty, try it!" You're telling someone who hasn't tried it.

Without

二階です

"It's on the second floor." Neutral answer.

With よ

二階ですよ

"It's upstairs." Friendlier, you're helping them out.

Sentence mining

Polite

YouTube · video

国際線ですよ。

"This is the international terminal."

Telling the viewer something they might not know. よ after です.

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 4

読まれたくないなら非公開にした方がいいですよ

"If you don't want people reading it, you should make it private."

Advice is a classic place for よ: you're handing over a tip they hadn't thought of.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 3

僕だってね、アリばっかり食べたくないよ

"Hey, I don't want to eat nothing but ants either!"

Everyone assumes he loves ants. He's setting them straight. よ after the plain negative ない.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 5

刻んだ竹が口に刺さりそうだよ

"The chopped bamboo looks like it'll stab me in the mouth!"

He's telling the others about a problem only he can feel. そう ("looks like") works like a な-adjective, so casual gets だよ.

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 4

食べ放題ですよ

"It's all you can eat!"

Telling them something they don't know yet. Polite: ですよ.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 21

デスクワークだよ。

"It's desk work."

Casual: だよ. Answering someone who guessed wrong.
