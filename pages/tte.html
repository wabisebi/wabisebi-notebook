N4 Grammar

って

Polite

Quartet · Episode 1

夫婦ふうふって何なんですか？

"What is a married couple, anyway?"

って is the spoken shortcut for two particles. After a word, it's a casual は: "X, I mean the thing called X". After something someone said, it's a casual と: the quote marker (～って言った).

って itself is casual and spoken, but it fits fine in a polite sentence: politeness comes from the end (です, ます).

On this card, she picks up the word 夫婦 and asks what it really means: "this thing called marriage, what is it?"

Casual

Teasing Master Takagi-san · Season 2, Episode 8

また痛いたいって言いった。

"You said 'ouch' again."

On this card, the quote job: 痛い is what he said, and って marks it, like と.

Quick Examples

Your boss asks where 田中 is.

田中たなかさん、明日あした休やすむって言いってました。

"Tanaka-san said he's taking tomorrow off."

Polite

Talking food with a friend.

ラーメンって、おいしいよね。

"Ramen is just so good, right?"

A casual は: "ramen, as a thing".

Casual

You ask if 山田 is coming tonight.

山田やまださん、行いかないって。

"Yamada-san says he's not going."

At the end of a sentence, 言ってた is often dropped: って alone = "they say".

Casual

With and without

With と

山田やまださんは、行いかないと言いいました。

"Yamada-san said he wouldn't go." Correct, but sounds like writing or a report.

With って

山田やまださん、行いかないって。

"Yamada-san says he's not going." How people actually say it.
