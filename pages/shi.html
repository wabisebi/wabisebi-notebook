N4 Grammar

～し

Polite

1 Litre of Tears · Episode 7

校舎こうしゃも古ふるいですし、階段かいだんも多おおいですし。

"The buildings are old, and there are lots of stairs." [so it's hard for her here]

し means "and also, and besides". You stack reasons with it, one after the other, and often just stop there: the listener fills in the conclusion. It goes after a whole sentence part: 古いです + し.

On this card, the school tells Aya's family why it isn't right for her: old buildings, lots of stairs. Nobody says "so she should move". The two しs say it for them.

Polite keeps です or ます before し. も often comes along: 階段も, "the stairs too".

Casual

Good Morning Call · Episode 16

もう遅おそいし

"It's late anyway." [so go to bed]

On this card, he's just told her to go to bed and adds a reason: もう遅い + し. Casual simply drops です.

Noun or な-adjective before it? Add だ first.

WordAddEnding
Noun学生がくせいだしI'm a student, and…
な-adjective静しずかだしIt's quiet, and…
Verb行いく–しI'm going, and…
い-adjective高たかい–しIt's expensive, and…

Quick Examples

Picking a place for lunch.

あの店みせは安やすいし、近ちかいし。

"That place is cheap, and it's close."

The conclusion, "let's go there", stays unsaid.

Casual

A coworker invites you out tonight.

明日あしたは仕事しごともありますし…

"I've got work tomorrow too, so…"

A soft no: give a reason and stop.

Polite

Saying why you like a city.

京都きょうとは静しずかだし、食たべ物ものもおいしいから、好すきだよ。

"Kyoto's quiet, and the food's good too, so I like it."

Here the conclusion is said out loud: から、好き.

Casual

With and without

With から

安やすいから、行いこう。

"It's cheap, so let's go." One reason, as if it's the only one.

With し

安やすいし、近ちかいし、行いこう。

"It's cheap, it's close… let's go." Several reasons, and there could be more.

Examples from Media

Polite

Quartet · Episode 1

着替きがえる場所ばしょもありますし ４人にんで十分じゅうぶん 使つかえます

"There's even a place to change, and it's plenty for the four of us."

This time the conclusion comes right after the し.

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 20

窓まどからは 竹林ちくりんも見みえますしね

"And you can see a bamboo grove from the window, too."

An estate agent listing a flat's good points, one after another.

Polite

Midnight Diner: Tokyo Stories · Season 2, Episode 8

金かねの切きれ目めが縁えんの切きれ目め… なんて言いいますしね

"When the money ends, so does the friendship, as they say."

Agreeing that friends shouldn't lend money: a saying as the reason, conclusion unsaid.

Polite

Legal High · Episode 4

台湾たいわんなんて いかがです？ 円高えんだかですし

"How about Taiwan? The yen's strong, after all."

Suggestion first, then the reason with し.

Casual

Odd Taxi · Episode 11

えっ でも電車でんしゃないし

"Huh? But there are no trains…"

Asked to come over late at night, she gives a reason and stops there.

Casual

Odd Taxi · Episode 2

なんか歌うたも怖こわいし

"And the song's kind of scary too."

Adding one more complaint: 歌も, "the song too".

Casual

Goblin Slayer · Episode 1

さらわれた女おんなの子こが 心配しんぱいだし

"Plus, I'm worried about the girl they took."

心配 is a な-adjective, so だ comes before し.

Casual

Ocean Waves

高知こうちも嫌きらいだし

"And I hate Kochi, too."

嫌い looks like an い-adjective, but it's a な-adjective: 嫌い + だ + し.
