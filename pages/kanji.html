N5 Kanji

Two kinds of reading

Most kanji can be read in more than one way. Japan took kanji from China, so each kanji kept a Chinese sound, and Japan also gave it its own Japanese word with the same meaning.

On'yomi
The Chinese sound. Written in katakana in dictionaries. You hear it mostly when two or more kanji make one word.富士山 ふじさん · Mount Fuji
Kun'yomi
The Japanese word. Written in hiragana. You hear it mostly when the kanji stands alone or has hiragana after it.山 やま · mountain

Same kanji, same meaning, different sound. It's a rule of thumb: 本 alone is ほん (on), and 手紙 てがみ (letter) uses kun in a two-kanji word.
