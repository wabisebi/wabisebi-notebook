N4 Grammar

とか

Polite

YouTube · video

日本にほんには、焼やき肉にくとか、鍋なべとかがあります。

"In Japan there are things like yakiniku and nabe."

とか lists examples: "A, B, things like that". It tells the listener there's more, you're only naming a few.

It goes after each item (A とか B とか), and the particle comes after the last one. On this card, yakiniku and nabe are two examples of Japanese food, and there are plenty of others.

とか itself doesn't change between polite and casual. It's a spoken word: in writing you'll see や instead.

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 19

特別とくべつ快速かいそくとか、通勤つうきん急行きゅうこうとか、いろいろありすぎ。

"Special Rapid, Commuter Express... there are way too many [kinds of train]."

On this card, two train types are just examples, and いろいろ ("all sorts") right after says there are lots more.

Quick Examples

A friend asks what you do on weekends.

映画えいがとか、ゲームとか。

"Movies, games, that kind of thing."

Casual

A waiter checks before taking your order.

卵たまごとか、食たべられないものはありますか？

"Is there anything you can't eat, like eggs?"

Eggs are just one example of what they mean.

Polite

Your friend looks tired.

コーヒーとか飲のむ？

"Want a coffee or something?"

One とか on its own: "or something". Softer than just コーヒー飲む？

Casual

With and without

With と

テニスとサッカーをしました。

"I played tennis and soccer." That's the full list.

With とか

テニスとかサッカーとかをしました。

"I played tennis, soccer, stuff like that." Those are examples, there was more.

えっと、海老えびとか大丈夫だいじょうぶですか？

"Um, is shrimp and that kind of thing okay [for you]?"

Only one とか: shrimp is the example, and the question gets softer.
