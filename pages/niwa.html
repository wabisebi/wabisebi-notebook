N5 Grammar

には・では・にも

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 38

春はるにはお花見はなみしましたよね

"[And] in spring, we went to see the cherry blossoms, right?"

A word in a sentence usually already has a particle after it: 魚を, 春に, 家で. Sometimes you also want も ("too") or は ("as for...") on that same word. So what happens to the particle that was already there?

  • を and が go away. も or は takes their place.
    魚さかなを食たべた → 魚さかなも食たべた "I ate fish too." Never をも.
  • に stays. も or は goes right after it.
    春はるに → 春はるには / 春はるにも "in spring" / "in spring too"
  • で stays too. Same thing.
    家いえで → 家いえでは / 家いえでも "at home" / "at home too"

Why: を and が only point at who does it and to what. も and は can do that job by themselves. に and で mean something (in, at, to), so they can't go.

On this card, they're looking back on the year. 春に is "in spring", and は makes it the topic: "as for spring, we went flower viewing."

Casual

1 Litre of Tears · Episode 6

私わたしにも見みせて。

"Show me too."

On this card, he's looking at plankton and she wants a look too. 見せて is "show", 私に is "to me", and も goes after に: "to me too".

Examples

A friend asks how your trip to Kyoto and Osaka went.

京都きょうとには行いったけど、大阪おおさかには行いかなかった。

"I went to Kyoto, but not to Osaka."

に (to) stays. は sets the two cities side by side.

Casual

A new coworker asks if you speak English at the office.

会社かいしゃでは日本語にほんごを話はなします。

"At work, I speak Japanese."

で (at) stays. は adds "at work, anyway": maybe not at home.

Polite

Back from a trip, you hand out souvenirs and remember one more person.

山田やまださんにもお土産みやげを買かいました。

"I bought a souvenir for Yamada-san too."

に (for) stays, も goes after it.

Polite

You already had a big lunch, and then someone brought cake.

ケーキも食たべた。

"I ate cake too."

ケーキを食べた, but を disappears: never ケーキをも.

Casual

With and without

Without に

田中たなかさんも話はなした。

"Tanaka talked too." Tanaka is the one talking.

With に

田中たなかさんにも話はなした。

"I told Tanaka too." Tanaka is the one being told. Drop に and the meaning changes.

Sentence mining

Polite

1 Litre of Tears · Episode 3

先生せんせいには分わかりません。

"You wouldn't understand, Doctor."

A mother to a young doctor. 分かる uses に for who understands: 先生に + は. "You, unlike a parent."

Polite

Slam Dunk · Episode 16

素人しろうとには見みえませんよ

"He doesn't look like a beginner."

に見える is "look like". に stays, は goes after it.

Polite

Kekkon Dekinai Otoko · Episode 4

このことは誰だれにも秘密ひみつですよ

"This is a secret from everyone, okay?"

誰に + も: "to anyone". 秘密 here means "not telling".

Casual

Kekkon Dekinai Otoko · Episode 4

英治えいじにも教おしえてないんで

"I haven't even told Eiji."

教える uses に for who you tell: 英治に + も. With a negative, も means "not even".

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 12

家いえにもたくさんあるんだけど

"I've got lots at home too, but..."

Talking about a collection. ある uses に for where: 家に + も.

Casual

Kotaro Lives Alone · Episode 3

ほれ　俺おれにも撮とらせろ

"Here, let me take one too."

She's taking photos and he wants a turn. 撮らせろ is a rough "let me take": 俺に + も.

Casual

Slam Dunk · Episode 97

選抜せんばつでは　絶対ぜったいに負まけない

"At the Winter Cup, we won't lose."

選抜 is the winter tournament. で + は: "at that one, unlike this time".

Casual

JoJo's Bizarre Adventure · Episode 26

ただ　太陽たいようの下したでは走はしれない

"But he couldn't run under the sun."

A man who could do anything, except this. 下で + は: anywhere else yes, under the sun no.
