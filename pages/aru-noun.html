N5 Grammar

がある + Noun

Polite～です・～ます at the end of the sentence
Casualno です or ます だ, the dictionary form, or nothing

This grammar has no polite or casual version: it looks the same either way. Only the end of the sentence sets the politeness.

Noun + がある + Noun

がある describes the noun after it, using the noun before it.

庭がある家 = "a house with a garden"
時間がある日 = "a day with time (to spare)"
生け簀があるカフェ = "a café with a fish tank"

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 2

生け簀があるカフェなんてないよ。

"There's no such thing as a café with a fish tank."

On this card, someone dreams of a café with live fish in a tank, and gets told 生け簀があるカフェ doesn't exist.

Polite

The Journalist · Episode 4

これは確実に 記事にする価値がある物です。

"This is definitely something worth writing an article about."

Same pattern: 価値がある + 物 = "a thing that has value". ある stays plain in front of the noun, and only the です at the end makes it polite.

Examples

Talking about where you'd like to live one day.

庭がある家に住みたいです。

"I want to live in a house with a garden."

Polite

A coworker asks what you do on your days off.

時間がある日は、散歩します。

"On days when I have time, I go for a walk."

Polite sentence, but still ある before the noun: 時間がある日, never 時間があります日.

Polite

The teacher looks around at the end of class.

質問がある人？

"Anyone with a question?"

質問がある + 人 = "a person who has a question".

Casual

Planning a drive with a friend.

駐車場がある店、知ってる？

"Do you know a shop with a parking lot?"

Casual

With and without

Without

家に住みたい。

"I want to live in a house." Any house.

With がある + Noun

庭がある家に住みたい。

"I want to live in a house with a garden." Now you say which kind.

Sentence mining

Polite

I'm Taking the Day Off · Episode 3

時間があるときは 凝った料理も作りますよ

"When I have time, I make fancy dishes too."

時間がある + とき ("time, when") = "when I have time". 凝った料理: elaborate cooking.

Polite

Weakest Beast · Episode 2

所詮 余裕がある人間の することなんですよ

"In the end, it's something only people with room to spare do."

余裕がある + 人間 = "people who have room to spare" (time, money, energy). 所詮: "in the end, after all".

Casual

Quartet · Episode 5

裏表がある人なんだよ

"She's a two-faced person."

裏表 is "back and front". 裏表がある人: a person who shows two different sides.

Casual

Good Morning Call · Season 2, Episode 8

やましいことがある時でしょ

"That's when they've got something to hide, right?"

やましいことがある + 時 = "when you have a guilty secret".

Casual

Border · Episode 4

右手の甲に やけどの痕があるヤツを探せ

"Find a guy with a burn scar on the back of his right hand."

やけどの痕がある + ヤツ ("guy"). The noun can be anything: a person, a place, a time.

Casual

Frieren · Episode 4

今は魔王城がある場所だ

"It's where the Demon King's castle stands now."

魔王城がある + 場所 ("place") = "the place where the castle is".

Casual

1 Litre of Tears · Episode 9

聞く気持ちがある人には 必ず伝わるから。

"It'll always reach people who are willing to listen."

聞く気持ちがある + 人 = "people who have the will to listen".
