N5 Grammar

どんな

Polite

1 Litre of Tears · Episode 3

どんな病気びょうきなんですか？

"What kind of illness is it?"

どんな means "what kind of". It always comes right before a noun: どんな + 病気 ("illness").

So it asks you to describe something. The answer is a describing word + the noun: 重い病気 ("a serious illness").

On this card, なんですか makes it a polite question: "what kind of illness is it?"

Casual

YouTube · Japanese Listening Practice #101

江戸えど弁べんってどんな感かんじ？

"What's Edo dialect like?"

Same どんな + noun (感じ, "feel"). On this card, って is casual は.

Quick Examples

Your friend has a new boyfriend.

どんな人ひと？

"What's he like?"

Answer: 優しい人 ("a kind person").

With and without

With 何

好すきな音楽おんがくは何なんですか？

"What music do you like?" Wants a name: ジャズ.

With どんな

どんな音楽おんがくが好すきですか？

"What kind of music do you like?" Wants a description: 静かな音楽.

どんな小屋こやを建たてるのでしょうか

"What kind of hut will he build, I wonder?"

どんな + 小屋 ("hut"). のでしょうか = a soft "I wonder".

Polite

YouTube · What Do Japanese People Buy at Convenience Stores!?

その中なかでどんなコンビニが1番ばん好すきですか

"Out of those, what kind of convenience store do you like best?"

どんな + コンビニ. 1番 = "the most".

Polite

The Cat Returns

どんなお礼れいをすればよいでしょう？

"How can we thank you?"

どんな + お礼 ("thanks, a thank-you") = "what kind of thank-you". すればよいでしょう: "should we do?".
