N5 Grammar

か

Polite～ですか after nouns and adjectives
～ますか after verbs
Casual～か

Both turn a sentence into a question. Casual speech often drops か and just raises the voice.

Polite

YouTube · video

レジ袋いりますか？

"Do you need a bag?" [the cashier, at the till]

か at the end turns a sentence into a question. It's a spoken question mark.

It goes right after the polite ending: いります → いりますか, です → ですか. On this card, the cashier asks a yes/no question: bag or no bag?

In casual speech you usually drop か and just raise your voice: いる？

Casual

New Atashin'chi · Episode 1

しとめたか？

"Did I get it?"

Casual か goes straight after a plain verb or a noun, with no です and no だ: しとめたか, 罠か (never 罠だか). It sounds blunt, so you mostly hear it from guys, or from someone asking themself.

Examples

You're on a platform, not sure about the train. You ask the person next to you.

この電車は渋谷に行きますか？

"Does this train go to Shibuya?"

Polite

At a party, someone you just met mentions their exams.

学生ですか？

"Are you a student?"

Polite

Your phone died and you ask a shop clerk.

今何時ですか？

"What time is it?"

Polite, with a question word

Texting a friend about tomorrow's barbecue.

明日来る？

"You coming tomorrow?"

Casual, no か

With and without

Without

レジ袋いります。

"I need a bag." You're telling the cashier.

With か

レジ袋いりますか？

"Do you need a bag?" The cashier is asking you.

Casual, no か

袋いる？

"Need a bag?" Normal, friendly. Just a rising voice.

Casual with か

袋いるか？

"Need a bag or not?" Blunt, like a gruff dad.

失礼ですが、火星人ですか？

"Excuse me, but are you a Martian?"

A weird question, but a polite one. 失礼ですが ("excuse me, but") softens it before the ですか.

Polite

YouTube · video

この作業部屋は自宅ですか？

"Is this workroom in your home?"

Interviewer asking a stranger, so ですか. Yes or no: home, or an office?

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 4

なんか　小さくて字　読みづらくないですか？

"Isn't the text kind of small and hard to read?"

ですか on a negative = "isn't it?", asking them to agree.

Polite

Weakest Beast · Episode 3

出張ですか？

"A business trip?"

出張 ("business trip") + ですか = "a business trip?". です + か turns any statement into a question.

Casual

PiroPito · First Playthrough of Minecraft #04

原木から、作れるってことか

"So you can make it from logs, huh."

Plain form + か, said to himself. He's not asking anyone, he's figuring it out out loud.
