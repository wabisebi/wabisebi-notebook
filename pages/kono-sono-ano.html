この・その・あの never change. Only the sentence ending does.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 16

この店みせは俺おれが引ひき継つぐ！

"I'll take over this shop!"

この, その and あの mean "this ___" or "that ___", and a noun always comes right after: この本, その考え方, あの人. Which one depends on where the thing is: この is near me, その is near you (or something you just said), あの is far from both of us.

On this card, the bear is standing in the shop he wants to take over. It's right where he is, so it's この店: "this shop".

They never stand alone. Without a noun you use これ, それ, あれ instead: それ、よく分かんない.

Polite

YouTube · Let's learn color names

このリュックは黒色くろいろです。

"This backpack is black."

この never changes, so only the end of the sentence sets the politeness. On this card, です makes it polite.

Quick Examples

At a bakery, you point at a bread in the case right in front of you.

このパンをください。

"This bread, please."

Near you: この.

Polite

A friend is wearing a jacket you like.

そのジャケット、どこで買かったの？

"Where did you buy that jacket?"

It's on them: その.

Casual

You and a friend see a tall tower in the distance.

あのビルは何なに？

"What's that building?"

Far from both of you: あの.

Casual

A friend mentions a ramen place they went to last week.

その店みせ、どこ？

"Where's that place?"

その also points at something the other person just talked about.

Casual

With and without

Without a noun

これは高たかいです。

"This is expensive." これ is the thing itself.

With この

この時計とけいは高たかいです。

"This watch is expensive." この needs a noun after it.

Without a noun

あれは何なに？

"What's that?" You don't say what kind of thing.

With あの

あの鳥とりは何なに？

"What's that bird?" You say which thing: the bird.

Examples from Media

Polite

Good Morning Call · Season 2, Episode 9

その実験じっけん、手伝てつだわせてもらえませんか？

"Could I help you with that experiment?"

その: the experiment she's working on, on her side.

Polite

Border · Episode 1

あの子この靴くつです。

"They're that kid's shoes."

The kid isn't here, but both of them know who she means: あの.

Casual

Pokémon The Series: Indigo League · Episode 4

強敵きょうてきだぜピジョンあの角かくに気きをつけろ！

"It's a tough one, Pidgeotto. Watch out for those horns!"

The horns are over there on the enemy: あの.

Casual

YouTube · video

このさ、砂漠さばくエリアの先さきちょっと行いってみる。

"I'm gonna check out a bit past this desert area."

The さ is just a casual pause. It's still この砂漠エリア: the area he's in.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 15

この木陰こかげなら涼すずしいし。

"It's cool in the shade of this tree, so..."

この + noun (木陰, "shade of a tree"). この never stands alone.

Casual

1 Litre of Tears · Episode 1

その考かんがえ方かた、俺おれよく分わかんない。

"I don't really get that way of thinking [the one you just said]."

The idea came from the other person, so it's その考え方: "that way of thinking of yours".

Casual

I am Mita, Your Housekeeper · Episode 3

その人ひとに聞きけば？

"Why don't you ask that person?"

その人: the person the other one just mentioned.

Casual

Samurai Champloo · Episode 4

あのバカ。

"That idiot."

あの: someone not here.
