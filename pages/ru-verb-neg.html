N5 Grammar

る-Verb (Negative)

Polite足りません
Casual足りない

Same meaning: "isn't enough". Only the politeness changes.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 2

少し塩気が足りないね

"[It] needs a little more salt."

To say a る-verb is not happening, drop the る and add ない: 足りる (to be enough) → 足りない (isn't enough).

On this card, he's tasting the food. The sentence answers "Is there enough salt?": no, it's a little short. The ね at the end is just "huh", sharing the thought.

Polite: drop the る and add ません: 足りません.

Polite

YouTube · video

あれ？！思ったほど散歩している人がいません。

"Huh?! There aren't as many people out walking as I thought."

Same move, polite: いる (to be there, for people and animals) → いません. Casual would be いない.

Examples

A friend holds out a pack of natto for you.

納豆は食べない。

"I don't eat natto."

To a coworker: 食べません.

Casual

A coworker asks if you saw the match last night.

サッカーはあまり見ません。

"I don't really watch soccer."

見る → 見ません. Just the る goes.

Polite

Your friend asks if you want to go out tonight. It's raining.

今日は出かけない。

"I'm not going out today."

ない works for "don't" and "won't". There's no separate future.

Casual

Your host family drops you at the airport on your last day.

この夏は忘れません。

"I won't forget this summer."

Polite

With and without

Plain

肉を食べる。

"I eat meat."

With ない

肉を食べない。

"I don't eat meat." る out, ない in.

Polite

肉を食べません。

Same meaning. る out, ません in.

Not this

肉を食べらない。

That's the う-verb move (帰る → 帰らない). る-verbs just drop the る.

Sentence mining

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 15

食べないの　パンダ　あんぱん

"Aren't you eating [your] anpan, Panda?"

食べる → 食べない. The の after it makes it a question that asks for the story: why aren't you eating it?

Casual

New Atashin'chi · Episode 2

あたしって２つのことが同時にできないんだよね。

"I just can't do two things at once."

できる (can do) is a る-verb, so できない. The んだよね after it is her explaining herself.

Polite

YouTube · video

もうすぐ朝６時ですけど、意外と散歩している人があんまりいないですね。

"It's almost 6 a.m., but surprisingly there aren't many people out walking."

いない + です is another polite way, a little softer than いません. Same guy, same walk as the card above.

Casual

Weakest Beast · Episode 9

世間は そう見ないから。

"Because people won't see it that way."

見る → 見ない: drop る, add ない.

Polite

Quartet · Episode 2

アイス食べないんですか？

"You're not having any ice cream?"

食べない + んですか: asking why not.

Casual

1 Litre of Tears · Episode 2

もう食べないの？

"You're done eating?"

もう食べないの: not eating any more?

Casual

Erased · Episode 9

俺が信じるか信じないかだ。

"It's about whether I believe it or not."

信じる → 信じない.
