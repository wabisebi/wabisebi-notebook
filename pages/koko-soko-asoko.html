N5 Grammar

ここ・そこ・あそこ

YouTube · video

え、ここは出汁のお店です。

"So, this place is a dashi shop."

ここ, そこ and あそこ mean "here" and "there". They point at a place, not a thing. Same split as これ・それ・あれ: ここ is where I am, そこ is near you, あそこ is far from both of us.

She's standing at the shop, so it's ここ. It answers "Where are we?": ここは sets the topic (this place), 出汁のお店です says what it is.

They work like normal nouns, so particles go right after: ここは, そこに, あそこで.

Examples

A friend visits your new flat. You open the door to one room.

ここは私の部屋です。

"This is my room."

You're standing in it: ここ.

At a café, there's an empty chair next to someone.

そこ、座ってもいいですか？

"May I sit there?"

The seat is next to them: そこ.

In a taxi, you see your hotel at the end of the street.

あそこで止めてください。

"Please stop over there."

Far from you and the driver: あそこ.

With and without

With これ

これは駅です。

"This is a station." Pointing at a thing, like a station on a map.

With ここ

ここは駅です。

"This place is a station." You're standing in it.

Sentence mining

Polite

YouTube · video

ここに、お湯を入れます。

"Pour the hot water in here."

She's pointing at the spot in front of her: ここ. に means "into" that place.

Casual

New Atashin'chi · Episode 2

ここのイチョウの木大きくなったねえ

"The ginkgo trees here have gotten big, huh."

ここの + noun = the ... of this place, the ... here.

Polite

ここから、裏庭に入ることができます。

"From here, you can get into the backyard."

ここから = from here.

Casual

"If I burp here, I'll definitely get made fun of."

ここで = here, where something happens.

Polite

YouTube · video

なんか...あそこにひよこのものがあります。

"Hmm... over there, there's something like a chick."

Something off in the distance: あそこ. あそこに + あります = there's ... over there.

Casual

YouTube · Road to Vostok #1

あ、なんかあそこにお店屋さんみたいなとこある

"Oh, there's some kind of shop-looking place over there."

Same shape as the chick, but casual: ある instead of あります.
