N5 Grammar

ね

Polite

YouTube · video

綺麗な鳴き声ですね

"[Hear that bird?] What a beautiful song, isn't it?"

ね at the end of a sentence means "you see it too, right?". It goes on the very end, after です, ます or a plain verb or adjective.

She's walking in the forest with her viewers, and they can all hear the bird. She isn't giving news, she's sharing the moment and inviting them to agree.

Polite: ですね / ますね. Casual: drop です, but after a noun or な-adjective add だ: 綺麗な鳴き声だね.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 5

楽しそうだね

"That sounds fun, huh."

Casual version. そう ("looks like") works like a な-adjective, so it gets だ before ね.

Examples

You and a friend step outside. It's freezing.

寒いね

"It's cold, huh."

Casual

You're both eating the curry your friend made. It's really good.

これ、おいしいね

"This is good, isn't it."

Casual

Small talk with a neighbor while it pours outside.

よく降りますね

"It sure is raining a lot."

Polite

A coworker shows you a photo of their new apartment.

広い部屋ですね

"What a big room."

Polite

With and without

Without

寒い

"It's cold." Just a statement, to yourself or anyone.

With ね

寒いね

"It's cold, huh." You both feel it, and you're sharing that.

With よ

寒いよ

"It's cold, you know." They don't know yet, so you tell them (maybe they're about to go out without a coat).

With ね

広い部屋ですね

"What a big room." You're both looking at it.

Sentence mining

Polite

YouTube · video

木漏れ日がきれいですね。

"The sunlight through the trees is beautiful, isn't it."

Same as the bird song: something everyone watching can see, so ね invites them to enjoy it with her.

Polite

YouTube · video

やっぱり、日本の方が寒いですね。

"Japan really is colder, huh."

She's comparing two places and expects the listener to agree. ね after です.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 14

ばればれだね

"That's totally obvious."

Sure the other person sees it too. ね after casual だ.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 7

笹子さんモテモテだね

"Sasako, you're really popular, huh."

Everyone can see the attention she's getting. モテモテ works like a な-adjective, so casual gets だね.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 22

名案だね

"Great idea!"

Reacting to what someone just said, and sharing the feeling.

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 15

遮光器土偶ですね

"That's a dogū figurine, huh."

ですね: the polite version, both looking at the same thing.
