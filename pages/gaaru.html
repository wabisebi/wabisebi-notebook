N5 Grammar

がある

There isThere isn't
Politeありますありません
Casualあるない

Same meaning: "there is". Only the politeness changes.

Making it negative: ある becomes ない (never あらない), and が often turns into は or も.
車くるまがある "I have a car"
車くるまはない "A car? Don't have one."
車くるまもない "No car either" (no bike, and no car too)

Polite

The Wind Rises

カプローニさん 質問しつもんがあります。

"Mr. Caproni, I have a question."

がある says something exists: "there is", or "it has / I have". The thing goes first, then が, then ある. It's for things, places and events, not for people or animals (those use いる).

On this card, 質問 ("question") + が + あります = "I have a question". あります is the polite ある.

"There isn't" is ありません / ない.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 6

頑張がんばったパンダ君くんにご褒美ほうびがあるよ

"There's a reward for Panda, who worked so hard."

Casual: ある. On this card, Panda gets some good news: ご褒美 + が + ある, there's a reward for him.

Quick Examples

A tourist asks if there's a convenience store nearby.

駅えきの前まえにコンビニがありますよ。

"There's a convenience store in front of the station."

Where first (駅の前に), then what (コンビニ), then があります.

Polite

A coworker asks if you can go for drinks tomorrow.

明日あしたはテストがあります。

"I have a test tomorrow."

Events work too: a test, a meeting, plans.

Polite

Your friend opens your fridge.

ビールある？

"Is there any beer?"

Casual question: no が, no か, just a rising voice.

Casual

A friend wants to watch one more episode.

もう時間じかんがない。

"There's no time left."

The "not" of ある is ない (never あらない).

Casual

With and without

Without

背せもたれです。

"It's a backrest." You're naming the thing.

With がある

背せもたれがあります。

"It has a backrest." You're saying it's there.

Without

時間じかんです。

"It's time." Time to go, time to start.

With がある

時間じかんがあります。

"I have time." The time exists, you're free.

Examples from Media

Polite

Midnight Diner: Tokyo Stories · Season 2, Episode 10

あなたにいい知しらせと悪わるい知しらせがあります。

"I have good news and bad news for you."

知らせ = "news". The things go first, then があります.

Polite

Death Note · Episode 11

北村きたむら次長じちょうにお願ねがいがあります。

"I have a request for Deputy Director Kitamura."

お願いがあります = "I have a favor to ask." A set phrase you hear all the time.

Polite

Samurai Champloo · Episode 9

いい手てがあります。

"I have a good idea."

手: here "a way, a trick".

Polite

The Wind Rises

いつもの所ところに おやつがあります。

"Your snack is in the usual place."

Casual

Pokémon The Series: Indigo League · Episode 2

でも大丈夫だいじょうぶよ自家じか発電はつでんがあるから。

"But it's okay, there's a generator."

あるから: "because there is".

Casual

Goblin Slayer · Episode 11

頼たのみがある。

"I have a favor to ask."

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 43

いい方法ほうほうがあるよ。

"I've got a good way."

A method is a thing, so it takes ある.

Casual

Aggretsuko · Season 1, Episode 1

秘密ひみつの趣味しゅみがある。

"I have a secret hobby."
