～ましょうか

Polite～ましょうか
Casual～ようか る-verbs: drop る
～おうか う-verbs: u sound → o

Same meaning: "shall I, shall we". Only the politeness changes.

Polite

YouTube · video

お箸はし、お付つけしましょうか？

"Would you like chopsticks?" [at the register]

ましょうか means shall I or shall we: you offer to do something, or suggest doing it together.

Swap ます for ましょうか. On this card, it's the extra polite shop version: お付けします (I'll add some) → お付けしましょうか. It answers "Do you want this?" before the customer has to ask.

Casual: the よう form + か: 付けようか, 持とうか. Without か, ましょう is just "let's".

Casual

Overprotected Kahoko · Episode 2

そうしようか

"Shall we do that?"

The casual form is the よう form + か. On this card, する → しよう → しようか, and そう means "that way".

Quick Examples

A coworker is carrying a heavy box.

持もちましょうか。

"Shall I carry that?"

持ちます → 持ちましょうか.

Polite

Your friend looks cold.

窓まど、閉しめようか？

"Want me to close the window?"

Casual: 閉める → 閉めよう + か.

Casual

It's getting late at a meeting.

じゃあ、帰かえりましょうか。

"Well, shall we head home?"

Polite

Planning dinner with a friend.

何なに食たべようか。

"What should we eat?"

With a question word: "what shall we...?"

Casual

With and without

Without

窓まどを閉しめましょう。

"Let's close the window." A suggestion, already decided.

With ましょうか

窓まどを閉しめましょうか。

"Shall I close the window?" An offer: you ask first.
