N4 Grammar

はず

Polite～はずです
Casual～はずだ・～はず

Same meaning: "should be, I expect". Only the politeness changes.

Noun before it? Add の. な-adjective? Add な.

WordAddEnding
Noun学生がくせいのはずHe should be a student
な-adjective静しずかなはずIt should be quiet
Verb行いく–はずHe should be going
い-adjective高たかい–はずIt should be expensive
Polite

1 Litre of Tears · Episode 7

適切てきせつな対応たいおうをしてくれるはずです。

"They should handle it properly."

はず means "should be": you expect it, because of what you know. It's your reasoning, not a promise.

It goes after a verb or adjective as is (くれる + はず), after a noun with の (休みのはず), after a な-adjective with な. On this card, the speaker trusts them to deal with it properly: that's what you'd expect from them.

In English it's often "should", but it never means "you should do it" (advice). That's a different word.

Casual

Kekkon Dekinai Otoko · Episode 5

たぶん この中なかにあるはずだ

"It should be in here, probably."

On this card, he's looking for something and reasons it out: it has to be in here. Casual drops です; it can end on はず alone too.

Examples

Your friend's train left an hour ago.

彼かれはもう着ついたはずだよ。

"He should have arrived by now."

You worked it out from the time.

Casual

A coworker asks if the meeting is on Monday.

月曜日げつようびのはずです。

"It should be on Monday."

After a noun, add の.

Polite

Someone says your shy friend went to a party.

そんなはずない！

"No way, that can't be!"

はずない (or はずがない): "it can't be".

Casual

With and without

Without

彼かれは来くる。

He's coming. You know it for sure.

With はず

彼かれは来くるはずだ。

He should be coming. That's what you expect, from what you know.

Sentence mining

Polite

Demon Slayer · Episode 6

必かならず近ちかくにいるはずです

"It's sure to be close by."

必ず makes the guess stronger: "it has to be nearby".

Polite

1 Litre of Tears · Episode 7

娘むすめは、分わかっているはずです。

"Our daughter should understand."

A parent reasoning about their child: they expect it, they don't know it.

Casual

Demon Slayer · Episode 1

俺おれのことは ちゃんと分わかってるはずだ

"She should know who I am."

He's counting on it, because they're family.

Casual

Slam Dunk · Episode 11

何なにか こつがあるはずだ

"There must be some trick to it."

Others can do it, so there has to be a trick.

Casual

Slam Dunk · Episode 43

負まけるはずがねえ

"There's no way I'd lose."

はずがない (here rough ねえ): "no way".

Casual

Kotaro Lives Alone · Episode 7

辛つらい思おもい出での方ほうが多おおいはず

"There must be more painful memories."

Ends on はず alone, no だ. Very common in speech.

Casual

JoJo's Bizarre Adventure · Episode 4

やつは近ちかいはず

"He should be close."

After an い-adjective, はず comes straight after.
