N5 Grammar

～のが好き

Polite～です・～ます at the end of the sentence
Casualno です or ます だ, the dictionary form, or nothing

This grammar has no polite or casual version: it looks the same either way. Only the end of the sentence sets the politeness.

To say you like doing something, put のが after the verb, then 好き: 泳ぐのが好き, "I like swimming". The の turns the action into a thing you can like.

The verb stays in its dictionary form (泳ぐ, 見る, 作る). The same frame works with 大好き, 嫌い, 得意 ("good at") and 苦手 ("bad at, not into"). Polite or casual only shows at the end: 好きです / 好きだ / just 好き.

Examples

Introducing yourself at a new job.

料理を作るのが好きです。

"I like cooking."

Polite

A coworker asks if you're a morning person.

朝早く起きるのが嫌いです。

"I hate getting up early."

Same frame with 嫌い: 起きる + のが嫌い.

Polite

A friend asks why you went to the cinema alone.

一人で映画を見るのが好きなんだ。

"I just like watching movies on my own."

Casual

Your friend notices the camera you always carry.

写真を撮るのが大好き！

"I love taking photos!"

Casual

With and without

Without

音楽が好きです。

"I like music." You like a thing.

With のが好き

音楽を聞くのが好きです。

"I like listening to music." You like doing something.

Sentence mining

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 30

ひっそり　山岳地帯で暮らすのが好きです

"They like living quietly up in the mountains."

暮らす ("to live") + のが好き. 山岳地帯: mountain areas.

Polite

Frieren · Episode 7

フリーレン様は 本当に 時間をムダにするのが好きですね

"Lady Frieren, you really do love wasting time."

時間をムダにする ("waste time") + のが好き.

Polite

Fullmetal Alchemist Brotherhood · Episode 8

バリーは肉を切り分けるのが大好きでした

"Barry loved cutting up meat."

切り分ける ("cut into pieces") + のが大好き. でした: past.

Casual

Howl's Moving Castle

あんた　逆さになるのが好きだねぇ。

"You sure like hanging upside down."

逆さになる ("be upside down") + のが好き.

Casual

Overprotected Kahoko · Episode 8

逆に いるのかよ？ 警察に行くのが好きなヤツ

"Is there anyone who actually likes going to the police?"

警察に行く ("go to the police") + のが好き + なヤツ ("a guy who...").

Casual

Boku no Hero Academia · Episode 9

俺は 同じことを言うのが嫌いだ｡

"I hate repeating myself."

Same frame with 嫌い: 言う + のが嫌い.

Casual

1 Litre of Tears · Episode 5

亜湖みたいに 絵を描くのが得意な人…

"People who are good at drawing, like Ako..."

Same frame with 得意 ("good at"): 描く + のが得意.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 44

カフェでだらだらするのが大好きなヤツなんだよ。

"He's a guy who loves lazing around in cafes."

のが turns the action into something you can love.
