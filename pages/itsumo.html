N5 Grammar

いつも・よく・時々

Polite

YouTube · video

いつも持もち歩あるいています。

"I always carry it with me."

いつも (always), よく (often) and 時々 (ときどき, sometimes) say how often something happens. They go before the action: いつも + 持ち歩いています.

On this card, the sentence answers "How often do you carry it?": every time. Without いつも it would just be "I carry it".

They never change. Polite or casual only shows at the end: いつも持ち歩いています / いつも持ち歩いてる.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 28

いつも持もち歩あるいてるの。

"I always carry it around."

いつも means every time, and goes before the verb. On this card, 持ち歩いてる is the casual 持ち歩いている ("carry around").

Quick Examples

A friend asks what you have for breakfast.

いつもパン。

"Always bread."

Casual

The café owner says they've seen you before.

よく来きます。

"I come here often."

Polite

A friend asks if you cook.

時々ときどき作つくる。

"Sometimes."

Casual

A coworker asks if you watch Japanese TV.

時々ときどきドラマを見みます。

"I watch dramas sometimes."

Polite

With and without

Always

いつも歩あるいて行いきます。

"I always walk there."

Often

よく歩あるいて行いきます。

"I often walk there."

Sometimes

時々ときどき歩あるいて行いきます。

"I sometimes walk there."

Not often

あまり歩あるいて行いきません。

"I don't walk there much." あまり needs a negative at the end.

Examples from Media

Polite

YouTube · video

母ははは私わたしはいつも少すこし変かわっていると思おもっていました

"My mother always thought I was a bit odd."

いつも + 変わっている: always a bit different.

Polite

Kekkon Dekinai Otoko · Episode 9

私わたし よく来くるんです。

"I come here often."

よく + verb: often.

Polite

Samurai Champloo · Episode 12

時々ときどき 考かんがえるんです。

"Sometimes I think about it."

Polite

Last Samurai Standing · Episode 1

いつもと違ちがいます。

"It's not like usual."

いつも as a noun: "the usual".

Casual

New Atashin'chi · Episode 2

昔むかしよくギンナン拾ひろったねえ。

"We used to pick ginkgo nuts a lot, didn't we?"

昔 (back then) + よく: something you did often in the past.

Casual

Good Morning Call · Episode 8

時々ときどき 子供こどもみたい。

"Sometimes he's like a child."

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 15

よく落おちてるんだ。

"They're often lying around."

よく = often.

Casual

Midnight Diner: Tokyo Stories · Season 2, Episode 8

よく知しってんな。

"You sure know a lot."

Careful: here よく means "well", not "often".
