N5 Grammar

いつも・よく・時々・たまに

いつもalways
よくoften
時々sometimes
たまにonce in a while
あまり～ないnot often 全然～ないnever
This pageOwn pages: verb goes negative
いつもコーヒーを飲のみます。I always drink coffee.
よくコーヒーを飲のみます。I often drink coffee.
時々ときどきコーヒーを飲のみます。I sometimes drink coffee.
たまにコーヒーを飲のみます。I drink coffee once in a while.
あまりコーヒーを飲のみません。I don't drink coffee much.
全然ぜんぜんコーヒーを飲のみません。I never drink coffee.
Polite

Aggretsuko · Season 1, Episode 8

いつも田園都市線でんえんとしせん使つかってるんですか？

"Do you always take the Den-en-toshi Line?"

いつも (always), よく (often), 時々 (ときどき, sometimes) and たまに (once in a while) say how often something happens. They go before the action: いつも + 使ってる.

On this card, she's asking a coworker about the commute. The sentence asks "How often?": every day? Without いつも it would just be "Do you use that line?".

They never change. Polite or casual only shows at the end: いつも使ってるんですか / いつも使ってるの？

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 28

いつも持もち歩あるいてるの。

"I always carry it around."

いつも means every time, and goes before the verb. On this card, 持ち歩いてる is the casual 持ち歩いている ("carry around").

A friend asks what you have for breakfast.

いつもパン。

"Always bread."

"Sometimes."

Casual

時々ときどきドラマを見みます。

Polite

With and without

Always

いつも歩あるいて行いきます。

"I always walk there."

Often

よく歩あるいて行いきます。

"I often walk there."

Sometimes

時々ときどき歩あるいて行いきます。

"I sometimes walk there."

Not often

あまり歩あるいて行いきません。

"I don't walk there much." あまり needs a negative at the end.

Examples from Media

Polite

Quartet · Episode 5

弟おとうとさんにはいつもお世話せわになっております。

"Your brother always takes such good care of me."

お世話になっております is a very polite set phrase, "thank you for all your help". いつも = always.

Polite

Oishinbo · Episode 90

房さんのおうちでもよく作つくりますか？

"Do you make them often at your house too, Fang-san?"

They're talking about dumplings. よく + verb asks how often.

Polite

Samurai Champloo · Episode 12

時々ときどき 考かんがえるんです。

"Sometimes I think about it."

Polite

Slam Dunk · Episode 26

ホントによく入はいりますね、あの三井みついは。

"That Mitsui really makes a lot of his shots."

よく = often, a lot: his shots go in again and again.

Polite

Legal High · Season 2, Episode 8

たまに食たべるからうまいんです。

"It's tasty because you only eat it once in a while."

たまに + verb: once in a while. Eat it every day and you'd get sick of it.

Casual

New Atashin'chi · Episode 2

昔むかしよくギンナン拾ひろったねえ。

"We used to pick ginkgo nuts a lot, didn't we?"

昔 (back then) + よく: something you did often in the past.

Casual

Good Morning Call · Episode 8

時々ときどき 子供こどもみたい。

"Sometimes he's like a child."

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 15

よく落おちてるんだ。

"They're often lying around."

よく = often.
