Polite

Aggretsuko · Season 1, Episode 8

いつも田園都市線でんえんとしせん使つかってるんですか？

"Do you always take the Den-en-toshi Line?"

いつも (always), よく (often) and 時々 (ときどき, sometimes) say how often something happens. They go before the action: いつも + 使ってる.

On this card, she's asking a coworker about the commute. The sentence asks "How often?": every day? Without いつも it would just be "Do you use that line?".

They never change. Polite or casual only shows at the end: いつも使ってるんですか / いつも使ってるの？

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 28

いつも持もち歩あるいてるの。

"I always carry it around."

いつも means every time, and goes before the verb. On this card, 持ち歩いてる is the casual 持ち歩いている ("carry around").

Quick Examples

A friend asks what you have for breakfast.

いつもパン。

"Always bread."

"Sometimes."

Casual

Polite

With and without

Always

いつも歩あるいて行いきます。

"I always walk there."

Often

よく歩あるいて行いきます。

"I often walk there."

Sometimes

時々ときどき歩あるいて行いきます。

"I sometimes walk there."

Not often

あまり歩あるいて行いきません。

"I don't walk there much." あまり needs a negative at the end.

Examples from Media

Polite

Quartet · Episode 5

弟おとうとさんにはいつもお世話せわになっております。

"Your brother always takes such good care of me."

お世話になっております is a very polite set phrase, "thank you for all your help". いつも = always.

Polite

Kekkon Dekinai Otoko · Episode 9

私わたし よく来くるんです。

"I come here often."

よく + verb: often.

Polite

Samurai Champloo · Episode 12

時々ときどき 考かんがえるんです。

"Sometimes I think about it."
