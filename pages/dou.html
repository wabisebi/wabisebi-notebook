N5 Grammar

どう・いかが

Politeどうですか everyday polite
いかがですか extra polite, for offers
Casualどう

Same meaning: "how, how about". Only the politeness changes.

Casual

Shirokuma Cafe · Season 1, Episode 5

どうしたの？何なにか考かんがえ事ごと？

"What's wrong? Thinking about something?"

どう means "how" or "how about". It asks what something is like or how it went, not for a thing or a place.

On this card, どうした = "how did it go, what happened (to you)". It's the go-to line when someone looks off.

Polite: どうしましたか？ いかが is an extra polite どう, mostly for offers: コーヒーはいかがですか？

Polite

Kekkon Dekinai Otoko · Episode 2

今夜こんやどうですか？

"How about tonight?"

どうですか is the polite どう, and it can suggest something. On this card, he invites her to dinner: 今夜 ("tonight") + どうですか.

Quick Examples

Your friend tries the ramen you recommended.

味あじはどう？

"How does it taste?"

Casual

A teacher asks a new student.

日本にほんの生活せいかつはどうですか？

"How's life in Japan?"

Polite

A café staff member, holding a jug.

お水みずはいかがですか？

"Would you like some water?"

いかが = very polite どう. Staff use it to offer things.

Polite

Planning to meet up.

明日あしたはどう？

"How about tomorrow?"

どう also suggests something: "how about ___?"

Casual

With and without

With 何

何なにを見みた？

"What did you watch?" Asks for a thing.

With どう

映画えいがはどうだった？

"How was the movie?" Asks what it was like.

Examples from Media

Polite

When Marnie Was There

具合ぐあいはどうですか？

"How are you feeling?"

具合 = "condition, how you feel". どうですか = "how is it?", the polite way to check on someone.

Polite

Shirokuma Cafe · Episode 22

駅前えきまえでポケットティッシュと一緒いっしょに配くばるのはどうでしょう？

"How about handing them out with pocket tissues in front of the station?"

どうでしょう = a soft, polite "how about ___?" for suggesting an idea.

Polite

Kotaro Lives Alone · Episode 4

わたあめいかがですか。

"Would you like some cotton candy?"

いかが: the extra polite どう, for offers.

Casual

Last Samurai Standing · Episode 3

どういう意味いみだ。

"What do you mean?"

どういう: "what kind of".

Casual

Teasing Master Takagi-san · Season 2, Episode 10

どうだ！

"How's that!"

Casual

Dungeon Meshi · Episode 3

"What's wrong?"

Casual

Samurai Champloo · Episode 12

Casual
